Basil + Clover

Basil is a smug, fluffy white cat who kicks. Clover, the spicy little muffin, eats everything. Nothing is safe.

Tap or click the items, or use the keys ← ↓ → ↑ or A S D W.

How it works

Kick it

  • The game runs in 30-second rounds: Round 1 Basil (KICK!), Round 2 Clover (NOM!), Round 3 Basil, and so on.
  • Every kick or nom scores 10 points × the round number (10 in round 1, 20 in round 2…), so later rounds are worth much more.
  • Each thing has a timer ring. If it runs out, Basil gets bored: -15% health.
  • Basil and Clover each have a health bar (Basil on the left, Clover on the right), starting at 100%. Mistakes knock it down. The game ends when the cat on the bench hits 0%. There’s no finish line: it keeps getting faster.
  • The cat that’s resting slowly recovers: Clover naps on her cushion during Basil’s turn, Basil sulks in the sink during hers.

Combos

  • Kick again within about a second to build a combo.
  • Every 3 quick kicks raises the multiplier: x2, x3, up to x4. A slow kick or a miss resets it.

Specials

  • Basil gets dirtier the more he kicks (hence Basil Brown Pants). Sink time washes most of it off.
  • Tap: sink time. Clears the bench (2 kicks’ worth each) and doubles points for 5 seconds.
  • Fish: +25% health.
  • Vase: needs 3 to 5 kicks, then shatters for 5 kicks’ worth. TV: needs 8 to 10 kicks, then smashes for 12 kicks’ worth. Red dots show kicks left; multipliers apply to the smash.
  • Fish tank (rare): 25 kicks, smashes for 50 kicks’ worth and +40% health (the goldfish flop out).
  • Moths and butterflies flutter past. Tap one (or press ↑ or W) and Basil (or Clover) eats it: 2 kicks’ worth and 6 seconds of FRENZY: double points, timers freeze and every kick hits twice as hard.
  • Wasps (yellow and black, red no sign) fly past too. Don’t tap them: a sting costs 20% health. Leave them and they buzz off.
  • Spiky things (cactus, pincushion, rose, pineapple), marked with a red no sign: don’t kick. Ouch: -20% health. Leave them and they vanish.

Clover

  • His sister Clover (aka Cute Clover, the Whingapotamus, the Chief Complaints Officer, Wiggle Butt, the Spicy Little Muffin, Miss Piggy, Clover Piggington) wanders by and sits on the bench. Swipe her for 3 kicks’ worth. She’ll be cross next time.
  • Cross Clover says HISS. Swipe her and she swipes back: -20% to Basil.
  • Leave cross Clover alone and she kicks one of Basil’s things herself. No points. Petty, but fair.

Clover’s rounds

  • Every second round is Clover’s. She doesn’t kick. She eats, and gets visibly rounder with every bite (up to twice as wide).
  • Food: cat food, banana, steak, chicken drumstick, sausage, pizza. Let it sit and she gets hungry (-12% health). Pineapple, coffee, grapes and garlic (red no sign) cost her 20%: pointy, or not safe for cats.
  • Every so often Basil sneaks up from either side to swipe her: tap him (or press ← or → on his side) to shoo him, or he steals points.

Score and titles

  • Your score is points: everything Basil kicks plus everything Clover noms, with all the multipliers. The end card splits it into Basil’s kick points and Clover’s nom points.
  • Whoever was on when the game ended stars on the end card.
  • Basil (by his kick points): Fluffy white rat (0+), Basil Brown Pants (1,000+), Furby with Fangs (2,500+), Fat Basil (5,000+), Basil Blanket Biter (10,000+), Fat white seal (15,000+), Basil Bad Cat (40,000+), Albino Satan (100,000+).
  • Clover (by her nom points): Cute Clover (0+), Wiggle Butt (1,000+), The Whingapotamus (3,000+), Chief Complaints Officer (7,500+), Spicy Little Muffin (15,000+), Miss Piggy (30,000+), Clover Piggington (60,000+), Queen Piggington (100,000+).
  • The cat on the bench has a slow-recharging rest coming: Clover naps during Basil’s rounds, Basil sulks in the sink during Clover’s.
  • Night falls every few rounds (rounds 4–5, 9–10…): the room dims and the moon comes out. At night the resting cat gets random bursts of zoomies and tears across the room. Tap them (or press ↑ / W) for 4 kicks’ worth.
  • Catnip (rare): kick or eat it for 8 seconds of chaos. Anything safe that lands gets dealt with automatically.
  • Rainbow butterfly (very rare): it drifts across the top. Catch it (tap, ↑ or W) and 20 treats rain down across the bench, each worth a kick.

Visitors and streaks

  • Stray tom (turf war): a scruffy neighbour cat glares through the window. Tap him 3 times (or ↑ / W) in time for 6 kicks’ worth. Too slow and he raids the bench: everything gone, no points, -15% health.
  • Dog at the window: 5 seconds of panic. Things land twice as fast.
  • Laser dot: tap it 3 times for 5 kicks’ worth. While it’s on, nobody can focus and the timers run faster.
  • Hairball (Clover’s rounds): she coughs one up. Tap it to bin it. Leave it and you lose 3 noms’ worth.
  • Streak titles: 6 in a row is a Menace, then Agent of Chaos (11), Kitchen Overlord (16), Gravity’s Favourite (26) and Unstoppable Fluff (41).

Daily challenge and leaderboards

  • Daily challenge: everyone gets the same items and surprises in the same order. New one at midnight, Sydney time.
  • Make the top 10 and you can post your name to the world or today’s leaderboard. Names are public, so keep it clean.
  • Pause with the button, P or Esc.
