Steinberg sign

Steinberg sign points to marfan syndrome (long thumbs, lax joints).

How it’s done
With the thumb folded into a closed fist, the tip pokes out past the edge of the palm.
What a positive result points to
Marfan syndrome (long thumbs, lax joints)
Who it’s named after
Israel Steinberg, an American radiologist, in 1966. Also called the thumb sign.
Good to know
One of several hand signs of long, slender fingers; genetic testing and an eye and heart check confirm Marfan.
