McMurray test

McMurray test points to meniscal tear.

How it’s done
Flex the knee fully, rotate the shin and straighten it: a painful click along the joint line.
What a positive result points to
Meniscal tear
Who it’s named after
Thomas Porter McMurray, an orthopaedic surgeon in Liverpool, in 1942.
Good to know
Pain along the joint line counts as much as a click. MRI is the usual next step if it matters.

How accurate is it?

In studies, it picks up about 61% of people with a torn meniscus (knee cartilage), and correctly clears about 84% of people without it. A coin toss with ambitions. It misses about 4 in 10 tears, and a click on its own proves nothing.

Source: Smith 2015 meta-analysis (PubMed abstract)
