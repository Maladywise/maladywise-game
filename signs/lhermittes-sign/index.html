Lhermitte’s sign

Lhermitte’s sign points to A lesion in the cervical spinal cord, classically multiple sclerosis.

How it’s done
Bending the neck forward sends an electric shock down the spine and limbs.
What a positive result points to
A lesion in the cervical spinal cord, classically multiple sclerosis
Who it’s named after
Jean Lhermitte, a French neurologist, in 1924.
Good to know
Also seen with B12 deficiency, neck radiotherapy and cervical spine disease.
