Weber test

Weber test points to hearing loss: conductive on the loud side or sensorineural on the quiet side.

How it’s done
A tuning fork on the middle of the forehead sounds louder in one ear.
What a positive result points to
Hearing loss: conductive on the loud side or sensorineural on the quiet side
Who it’s named after
Ernst Heinrich Weber, a German physiologist.
Good to know
Read together with the Rinne test to tell conductive from sensorineural loss.
