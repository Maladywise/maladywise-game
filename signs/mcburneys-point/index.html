McBurney’s point

McBurney’s point points to appendicitis.

How it’s done
Maximal tenderness one third of the way from the right hip bone (ASIS) to the umbilicus.
What a positive result points to
Appendicitis
Who it’s named after
Charles McBurney, a New York surgeon, described it in 1889.
Good to know
Pain often starts around the belly button, then moves here as the inflamed appendix irritates the lining nearby.
