Blumberg’s sign

Blumberg’s sign points to peritonitis.

How it’s done
Pain is worse when your hand is suddenly released from the abdomen than when it presses down (rebound tenderness).
What a positive result points to
Peritonitis
Who it’s named after
Jacob Moritz Blumberg, a German surgeon and gynaecologist, in 1907.
Good to know
Gentler options like the cough test or heel drop hurt less and tell you much the same.
