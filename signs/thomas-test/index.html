Thomas test

Thomas test points to A fixed flexion deformity of the hip.

How it’s done
Lying flat, the patient hugs one knee to the chest and the other thigh lifts off the bed.
What a positive result points to
A fixed flexion deformity of the hip
Who it’s named after
Hugh Owen Thomas, a Welsh bonesetter’s son and surgeon, also famous for the Thomas splint.
Good to know
The lumbar spine arches to hide a tight hip; flattening it reveals the fixed flexion.
