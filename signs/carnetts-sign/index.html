Carnett’s sign

Carnett’s sign points to pain coming from the abdominal wall, not inside the abdomen.

How it’s done
Abdominal tenderness stays the same or gets worse when the patient tenses their abdominal muscles.
What a positive result points to
Pain coming from the abdominal wall, not inside the abdomen
Who it’s named after
John Berton Carnett, an American surgeon, in 1926. A cheap test that saves many scans.
Good to know
Abdominal wall pain (often a trapped nerve) is common and easy to miss; a local anaesthetic injection can treat it.
