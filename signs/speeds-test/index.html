Speed’s test

Speed’s test points to long head of biceps tendon problems.

How it’s done
Resisting forward lift of the straight, palm-up arm causes pain at the front of the shoulder.
What a positive result points to
Long head of biceps tendon problems
Who it’s named after
J. Spencer Speed, an American orthopaedic surgeon.
Good to know
Biceps tendon problems often come with rotator cuff disease.
