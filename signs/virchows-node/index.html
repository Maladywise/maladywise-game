Virchow’s node

Virchow’s node points to abdominal cancer, classically stomach.

How it’s done
An enlarged lymph node above the left clavicle (Troisier’s sign).
What a positive result points to
Abdominal cancer, classically stomach
Who it’s named after
Rudolf Virchow, the German ‘father of pathology’, in 1848. The thoracic duct drains into the left neck.
Good to know
The thoracic duct drains into the left side of the neck, so abdominal cancers can show up here (Troisier’s sign).
