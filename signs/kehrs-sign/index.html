Kehr’s sign

Kehr’s sign points to blood irritating the diaphragm, classically a ruptured spleen.

How it’s done
Pain at the tip of the left shoulder in someone with abdominal trauma.
What a positive result points to
Blood irritating the diaphragm, classically a ruptured spleen
Who it’s named after
Hans Kehr, a German gallbladder surgeon, around the start of the 1900s. The pain is referred along the phrenic nerve (C3 to C5).
Good to know
Blood under the left diaphragm irritates the phrenic nerve (C3–5), so pain is felt at the left shoulder tip.
