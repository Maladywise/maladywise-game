Chvostek’s sign

Chvostek’s sign points to low calcium (also seen in some healthy people).

How it’s done
Tapping in front of the ear makes the corner of the mouth twitch.
What a positive result points to
Low calcium (also seen in some healthy people)
Who it’s named after
František Chvostek, a Czech-Austrian surgeon, in 1876.
Good to know
Up to about 1 in 4 healthy people have a positive Chvostek’s, so it’s a weak test on its own.
