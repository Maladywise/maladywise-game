Patrick’s (FABER) test

Patrick’s (FABER) test points to hip joint or sacroiliac joint problems.

How it’s done
The ankle rests on the opposite knee and the bent knee is pushed down: pain in the groin or buttock.
What a positive result points to
Hip joint or sacroiliac joint problems
Who it’s named after
Hugh Talbot Patrick, a Chicago neurologist. FABER = flexion, abduction, external rotation.
Good to know
Groin pain suggests the hip; pain at the back suggests the sacroiliac joint.
