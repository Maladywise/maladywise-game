Lasègue’s sign

Lasègue’s sign points to lumbar nerve root irritation (sciatica).

How it’s done
Lifting the straight leg reproduces shooting pain down the back of the leg.
What a positive result points to
Lumbar nerve root irritation (sciatica)
Who it’s named after
Charles Lasègue, a French physician, credited by his student; today usually called the straight leg raise.
Good to know
Pain should reproduce leg symptoms below the knee between about 30° and 70° to count.
