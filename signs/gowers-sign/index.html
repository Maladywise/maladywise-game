Gowers’ sign

Gowers’ sign points to proximal muscle weakness, classically Duchenne muscular dystrophy.

How it’s done
A child gets up from the floor by ‘walking’ their hands up their own legs.
What a positive result points to
Proximal muscle weakness, classically Duchenne muscular dystrophy
Who it’s named after
William Gowers, a London neurologist, in 1879.
Good to know
Weak hip and thigh muscles mean the child ‘walks up’ their own legs; check CK and genetics.
