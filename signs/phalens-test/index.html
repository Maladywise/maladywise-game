Phalen’s test

Phalen’s test points to carpal tunnel syndrome.

How it’s done
Holding both wrists fully bent for 60 seconds brings on tingling in the thumb, index and middle fingers.
What a positive result points to
Carpal tunnel syndrome
Who it’s named after
George Phalen, a Cleveland hand surgeon, in the 1950s.
Good to know
Hold the backs of the hands together for up to 60 seconds. Nerve conduction studies confirm carpal tunnel.

How accurate is it?

In studies, it picks up about 68% of people with carpal tunnel syndrome, and correctly clears about 73% of people without it. Middling both ways. A third of people with carpal tunnel pass it, and plenty without it fail. Nerve conduction studies are the usual tiebreaker.

Source: Wipperman and Potter 2012, Carpal tunnel syndrome: try these diagnostic maneuvers (J Fam Pract)
