Froment’s sign

Froment’s sign points to ulnar nerve weakness (weak adductor pollicis).

How it’s done
Gripping paper between thumb and index finger, the thumb tip bends to compensate.
What a positive result points to
Ulnar nerve weakness (weak adductor pollicis)
Who it’s named after
Jules Froment, a French neurologist, in 1915.
Good to know
The patient bends the thumb tip to pinch, using a muscle the median nerve still supplies.
