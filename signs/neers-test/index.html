Neer’s test

Neer’s test points to subacromial impingement (rotator cuff pain).

How accurate is it?
79%picks up53%correctly clears

In studies, it picks up about 79% of people with shoulder impingement, and correctly clears about 53% of people without it. Good at catching it, bad at clearing it: about half of people without impingement say ouch anyway. Shoulders are complicated.

Source: Hegedus 2008 meta-analysis (Br J Sports Med abstract)

How it’s done
Lifting the straight, inwardly rotated arm forwards causes pain at the front of the shoulder.
What a positive result points to
Subacromial impingement (rotator cuff pain)
Who it’s named after
Charles Neer, an American shoulder surgeon, in 1972.
Good to know
Injecting local anaesthetic and repeating it (Neer’s impingement test) can confirm the source of pain.
