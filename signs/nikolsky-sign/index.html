Nikolsky sign

Nikolsky sign points to blistering diseases such as pemphigus vulgaris or toxic epidermal necrolysis.

How it’s done
Rubbing normal-looking skin makes the top layer slide off.
What a positive result points to
Blistering diseases such as pemphigus vulgaris or toxic epidermal necrolysis
Who it’s named after
Pyotr Nikolsky, a Russian dermatologist, in 1896.
Good to know
Positive in pemphigus, SJS/TEN and staph scalded skin. Usually negative in bullous pemphigoid.
