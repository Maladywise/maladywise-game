Jobe’s test

Jobe’s test points to supraspinatus tendon tear or tendinopathy.

How it’s done
Arms out at 30° forwards, thumbs down (‘empty can’): weakness or pain pressing them down.
What a positive result points to
Supraspinatus tendon tear or tendinopathy
Who it’s named after
Frank Jobe, the American surgeon who also invented ‘Tommy John’ elbow surgery.
Good to know
Weakness counts more than pain for spotting a full-thickness tear.
