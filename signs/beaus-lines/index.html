Beau’s lines

Beau’s lines points to A past serious illness or chemotherapy that paused nail growth.

How it’s done
Horizontal grooves across all the nails.
What a positive result points to
A past serious illness or chemotherapy that paused nail growth
Who it’s named after
Joseph Honoré Simon Beau, a French physician, in 1846.
Good to know
You can date the illness: nails grow about 3 mm a month.
