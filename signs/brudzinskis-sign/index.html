Brudziński’s sign

Brudziński’s sign points to meningeal irritation, e.g. meningitis.

How it’s done
Bending the neck forward makes the hips and knees bend.
What a positive result points to
Meningeal irritation, e.g. meningitis
Who it’s named after
Józef Brudziński, a Polish paediatrician, in 1909.
Good to know
Josef Brudziński described several signs; this neck one is the best known.
