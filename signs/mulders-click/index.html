Mulder’s click

Mulder’s click points to morton’s neuroma.

How it’s done
Squeezing the forefoot while pressing between the toes produces a painful click.
What a positive result points to
Morton’s neuroma
Who it’s named after
Jacob Mulder, a Dutch surgeon, in 1951.
Good to know
Ultrasound can confirm a neuroma; wider shoes often help.
