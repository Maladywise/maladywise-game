Apley grind test

Apley grind test points to meniscal tear.

How it’s done
Patient lies face down, knee bent to 90°: pressing down and twisting the foot causes knee pain.
What a positive result points to
Meniscal tear
Who it’s named after
Alan Apley, a British orthopaedic surgeon and author of a famous textbook, in 1947.
Good to know
Only modestly accurate; combine with the history and other knee tests.

How accurate is it?

In studies, it picks up about 61% of people with a meniscal tear in the knee, and correctly clears about 70% of people without it. Only a little better than a coin toss either way. Doctors combine it with the history and other tests, or get an MRI.

Source: Hegedus et al. 2007, JOSPT meta-analysis, summarised by BestBETs
