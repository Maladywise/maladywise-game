Janeway lesions

Janeway lesions points to infective endocarditis.

How it’s done
Painless red-purple spots on the palms and soles.
What a positive result points to
Infective endocarditis
Who it’s named after
Edward Janeway, a New York physician, in 1899.
Good to know
Painless (unlike Osler nodes). Blood cultures and echo confirm endocarditis.
