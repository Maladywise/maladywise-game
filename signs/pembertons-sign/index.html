Pemberton’s sign

Pemberton’s sign points to A goitre extending behind the sternum, blocking the neck veins.

How it’s done
Raising both arms above the head makes the face flush and swell.
What a positive result points to
A goitre extending behind the sternum, blocking the neck veins
Who it’s named after
Hugh Pemberton, a Liverpool physician, in 1946.
Good to know
Arms up narrows the chest inlet, so a goitre there blocks veins: face flushes within a minute.
