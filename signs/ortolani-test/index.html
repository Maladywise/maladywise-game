Ortolani test

Ortolani test points to developmental dysplasia of the hip (a dislocated hip that can be reduced).

How it’s done
Gently abducting a newborn’s flexed hip produces a clunk as the hip slips back into the socket.
What a positive result points to
Developmental dysplasia of the hip (a dislocated hip that can be reduced)
Who it’s named after
Marino Ortolani, an Italian paediatrician, in 1937.
Good to know
Only works in the first few months; ultrasound is better for babies at risk.
