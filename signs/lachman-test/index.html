Lachman test

Lachman test points to anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) tear.

How accurate is it?
85%picks up94%correctly clears

In studies, it picks up about 85% of people with a torn ACL (a key knee ligament), and correctly clears about 94% of people without it. One of the better bedside tests in all of medicine. It catches most torn ACLs and rarely cries wolf.

Source: Benjaminse 2006 meta-analysis (PubMed abstract)

How it’s done
With the knee bent about 20 to 30 degrees, the shin slides too far forward on the thigh, with a soft end point.
What a positive result points to
Anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) tear
Who it’s named after
Named by Joseph Torg in 1976 after his teacher, John Lachman, in Philadelphia.
Good to know
The most accurate bedside test for an ACL tear, better than the anterior drawer test.
