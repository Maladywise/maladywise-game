Walker–Murdoch sign

Walker–Murdoch sign points to marfan syndrome (long fingers, thin wrists).

How it’s done
Wrapping the thumb and little finger around the other wrist, they overlap.
What a positive result points to
Marfan syndrome (long fingers, thin wrists)
Who it’s named after
Arthur Walker and John Murdoch described the wrist sign in 1955.
Good to know
Pair it with the thumb sign. In the hEDS checklist, either sign on both hands counts.
