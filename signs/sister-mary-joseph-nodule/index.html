Sister Mary Joseph nodule

Sister Mary Joseph nodule points to A metastasis from abdominal or pelvic cancer.

How it’s done
A firm lump in the umbilicus.
What a positive result points to
A metastasis from abdominal or pelvic cancer
Who it’s named after
Named for Sister Mary Joseph Dempsey, surgical assistant to William Mayo, who noticed it while prepping patients. One of very few signs named after a nurse.
Good to know
Usually means cancer has spread through the abdomen, most often from the stomach, ovary, bowel or pancreas.
