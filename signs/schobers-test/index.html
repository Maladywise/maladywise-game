Schober’s test

Schober’s test points to reduced lumbar movement, e.g. ankylosing spondylitis.

How it’s done
Two marks on the lower back barely move apart when the patient bends forward.
What a positive result points to
Reduced lumbar movement, e.g. ankylosing spondylitis
Who it’s named after
Paul Schober, a German physician, in 1937.
Good to know
Normal is at least 5 cm of stretch between marks 10 cm apart. Less suggests a stiff lower spine.
