Schamroth’s window test

Schamroth’s window test points to finger clubbing.

How it’s done
Placing matching fingernails back to back: the diamond-shaped gap is gone.
What a positive result points to
Finger clubbing
Who it’s named after
Leo Schamroth, a South African cardiologist, who tested it on himself, in 1976.
Good to know
Clubbing has many causes: lung cancer, bronchiectasis, heart disease, liver disease.
