Ober’s test

Ober’s test points to A tight iliotibial band.

How it’s done
Lying on one side, the upper leg stays up in the air instead of dropping when released.
What a positive result points to
A tight iliotibial band
Who it’s named after
Frank Ober, a Boston orthopaedic surgeon, in 1936.
Good to know
Tight iliotibial bands are linked with pain on the outside of the knee in runners.
