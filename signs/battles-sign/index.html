Battle’s sign

Battle’s sign points to fracture of the base of the skull.

How it’s done
Bruising behind the ear, over the mastoid, a day or more after a head injury.
What a positive result points to
Fracture of the base of the skull
Who it’s named after
William Henry Battle, a London surgeon, in 1890.
Good to know
Often takes a day or two to appear, so its absence early on doesn’t rule out a fracture.
