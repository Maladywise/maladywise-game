Rinne test

Rinne test points to conductive hearing loss in that ear.

How it’s done
A tuning fork is heard better on the bone behind the ear than in front of it.
What a positive result points to
Conductive hearing loss in that ear
Who it’s named after
Heinrich Rinne, a German otologist, in 1855.
Good to know
Uses a 512 Hz tuning fork. Normal is ‘Rinne positive’: air louder than bone.
