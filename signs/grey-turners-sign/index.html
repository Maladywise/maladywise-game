Grey Turner’s sign

Grey Turner’s sign points to bleeding behind the peritoneum, classically haemorrhagic pancreatitis.

How it’s done
Bruising of the flanks.
What a positive result points to
Bleeding behind the peritoneum, classically haemorrhagic pancreatitis
Who it’s named after
George Grey Turner, an English surgeon, in 1920.
Good to know
Like Cullen’s, a late sign. Seen in severe pancreatitis and ruptured ectopic pregnancy.
