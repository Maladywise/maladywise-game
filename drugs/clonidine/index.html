Drug info

Clonidine

Alpha-2 agonist Prescription only

An older blood pressure tablet that calms the body’s “fight or flight” signals. Now used more for ADHD, sleep and tics than for blood pressure.

Brand names

  • Catapres
  • Several generic brands sold as clonidine

How it works

Clonidine switches on alpha-2 receptors in the brainstem. This turns down adrenaline-type (sympathetic) signals, which lowers blood pressure and heart rate and has a calming, sedating effect.

Used for

  • High blood pressure (now rarely a first choice)
  • ADHD, and trouble getting to sleep with ADHD, usually in children and started by a specialist (off-label in Australia)
  • Tics and Tourette syndrome (off-label)
  • Menopausal hot flushes
  • Easing withdrawal from opioids or alcohol, under supervision (off-label)

Common side effects

  • Drowsiness and tiredness
  • Dry mouth
  • Dizziness, especially when standing up
  • Constipation
  • Headache
  • Low mood or vivid dreams (less common)

Get help urgently if

  • Fainting, a very slow pulse or feeling very faint
  • Extreme sleepiness or slowed breathing
  • A child swallows any tablets not meant for them: even one can be dangerous. Call 000 or the Poisons Information Centre on 13 11 26

Good to know

  • Don’t stop suddenly. Blood pressure can rebound and cause agitation, headache and a racing heart. Your doctor will reduce it slowly.
  • Alcohol, sleeping tablets and other sedating medicines add to the drowsiness.
  • Take care driving until you know how it affects you.
  • Store locked away from children.
