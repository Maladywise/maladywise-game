All medical games and quizzes

Free daily medical puzzles: a medical Wordle, an anatomy quiz, an ECG quiz, medical crosswords, medical trivia and more doctor games, for students, nurses, doctors and curious people.

Word games

Medical Wordle, crosswords, word search, spelling bee and sudoku.

  • Wordemic: a daily medical Wordle. Guess the five-letter medical word in six tries.
  • Comorbix: a daily medical connections game. Sixteen terms, four hidden groups.
  • Gridectomy: a daily medical mini crossword. Five by five, medical clues.
  • Morphemia: a daily medical spelling bee. Make words from seven letters around a centre letter.
  • Suturix: a daily medical word search with a hidden theme.
  • Linkase: a daily medical word chain. Each word links to the next.
  • Sudokrine: a daily medical sudoku with a hidden word.

Quizzes

Medical trivia, diagnosis games, rare diseases and test accuracy.

  • Triviosis: a daily medical trivia quiz. Ten questions, one theme, every answer explained.
  • Dxtective: a daily diagnosis game. Six clues, one fictional patient, name the condition.
  • Zebritis: a daily rare disease quiz game where the rarest right answer scores most.
  • Sensitosis: a daily sensitivity and specificity game. How good are real medical tests?

Image, ECG and blood test quizzes

Anatomy quiz, X-ray quiz, spot the diagnosis, ECG quiz and blood results.

  • Anatomegaly: a daily anatomy quiz. Five rounds of structures, bones and nerves.
  • Arrhythmix: a free ECG quiz every Monday and Thursday.
  • Opacitis: a daily X-ray and radiology quiz using real images.
  • Glimpsis: a daily spot-the-diagnosis photo quiz using real clinical images.
  • Panelysis: a daily blood test quiz. Read the results, name the diagnosis.

Weekly specials

A medical cryptic crossword on Saturday, hard trivia on Sunday.

  • Cryptoplexy: a weekly medical cryptic crossword, every Saturday.
  • Grand Rounds: a weekly hard medical trivia quiz, every Sunday.

Arcade

Quick bonus games for a break.

  • Stackulus: a falling-block arcade game with organs and a daily challenge.
  • Mergeon: an organ merge game. Drop, match and merge from microbe to brain.
  • Basil + Clover: a cat arcade game. Kick things off the bench, then survive the vet.
