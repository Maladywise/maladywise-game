Privacy

Malady Wise (maladywise.com) collects as little information as it can, and never sells it.

The short version

  • No accounts, no ads.
  • Simple, cookie-free visitor counts. No tracking across sites, no personal profiles.
  • Your scores, streaks and settings stay in your own browser.

Newsletter

If you subscribe, your email address is stored by our newsletter provider, Buttondown, which may store it outside Australia. We use it only to send the newsletter. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your address at any time.

Your puzzle progress

Your answers, stats, streaks, Einstein badges and settings (like dark mode) are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. They are never sent to us, and we can't see them. Clearing your browser data deletes them. They don't carry across devices.

Our hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log basic technical details, such as your IP address, to keep the service secure. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Visitor counts

We use GoatCounter to count visits, which pages and games are popular, and how often games are finished. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you across sites and doesn't build a profile of you. It records things like the page, the referring site, browser, screen size and approximate country. Your IP address isn't stored. If you use an ad blocker, you probably won’t be counted.

Fonts

The pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers. See Google's privacy policy.

Cookies

The site doesn't set cookies.

Sharing results

When you tap "Copy result", the text is copied to your clipboard. Nothing is posted anywhere unless you paste it yourself.

Health information

Please don't send us personal health information. We don't give personal medical advice, so we have no reason to keep it, and we'll delete any we receive.

Questions or complaints

Email maladywise@gmail.com. We'll reply within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
