Privacy

Malady Wise (maladywise.com) is a free medical puzzles and health information site. It collects as little as it can, never sells it, and has no accounts and no ads.

The short version

  • No accounts, no ads, no cookies, no tracking across sites.
  • Cookie-free visitor counts (GoatCounter).
  • Your scores, streaks and settings stay on your own device.
  • Some features send small amounts of data to our own server in the United States: live player counts, leaderboards, the “Report a problem” form and specialist tag suggestions. Each is explained below.
  • The mental health checks send nothing anywhere.
  • Leaderboard nicknames are public. If you’re a child, use a nickname, not your real name.

Visitor counts (GoatCounter)

We use GoatCounter to count visits, which pages and games are popular, and how often puzzles are finished. It doesn’t set cookies, doesn’t track you across sites and doesn’t build a profile of you. It records the page, the referring site, browser, screen size and approximate country. It doesn’t store your IP address. If you use an ad blocker, you probably won’t be counted.

Some game actions are counted the same way, such as finishing a puzzle. In Zebritis, the listed answers people pick are counted so rarer answers can score more, and if you tap “Suggest” on a missed answer, that answer and the prompt are recorded so we can review the list. Nothing else is attached.

On your device (local storage)

Your answers, stats, streaks, badges, leaderboard nickname, a random game code and settings (like dark mode and Normie mode) are saved in your browser’s local storage. They stay on your device and don’t carry across devices. Clearing your browser data deletes them. The site may also keep copies of its own pages on your device so it works offline.

Our own server (live counts, leaderboards and reports)

A small server run for Malady Wise on Render, hosted in Singapore, handles the features below. It doesn’t set cookies. Your IP address is used briefly in memory to stop abuse (rate limiting) and isn’t saved, except as a scrambled code for tag suggestions (see below).

  • “Playing now” and “games played today”. While a page is open it sends a “still here” signal about every 30 seconds with a random code that exists only in that tab and is forgotten when you close it, plus one signal when you finish a puzzle. Daily totals are kept for about a week. These counts aren’t linked to you.
  • Leaderboards (Basil + Clover, including the vet stage, Stackulus, Mergeon and Sudokrine). If you post a score, we keep the nickname you type, your score (or time) and round or level, and the random game code saved in your browser, so a new best replaces your old one. Nicknames and scores are public. Daily boards are deleted after 3 days. All-time boards keep the top 200 scores until they’re pushed off. If you don’t type a name, you appear as “Anonymous”.
  • “Report a problem” form. We keep what you write, the page you were on, the category, the game mode and, only if you give it, your email address (so we can reply). We keep the latest 500 reports and delete each one once it’s dealt with. Please don’t include personal health details.
  • Specialist tag suggestions. On the specialists directory you can suggest practical tags (like parking or wheelchair access) from a fixed list. To count different visitors, we keep a one-way scrambled code (hash) made from your IP address, never the IP address itself, for up to 180 days.

Mental health checks

The mental health checks run entirely in your browser. Your answers and scores aren’t saved, aren’t put in the web address and aren’t sent to us or anyone else. The live player count and game-action counts are switched off on those pages; only the page visit itself is counted by GoatCounter.

Newsletter (Zebra Mail)

If you subscribe to Zebra Mail, your email address is stored by our newsletter provider, Buttondown, which is based in the United States. We use it only to send the newsletter. Buttondown may record whether emails are opened or links clicked. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your address at any time.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log basic technical details, such as your IP address, to keep the service secure. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Fonts

Our fonts are hosted on our own site, so pages don’t make requests to Google Fonts.

Cookies and sharing

The site doesn’t set cookies. When you tap “Copy result”, the text goes to your clipboard. Nothing is posted anywhere unless you paste it yourself.

Overseas transfers

Malady Wise is run from Australia. The services above (GitHub, GoatCounter, Render and Buttondown) store data outside Australia, mainly in the United States, Singapore and the European Union. We choose services with published privacy policies and send them only what each feature needs.

Your rights

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988. If you’re in the European Union or the United Kingdom, the GDPR and UK GDPR also give you rights.

  • Legal basis. We send the newsletter, and keep your email with a report, because you asked us to (consent). Visitor counts, leaderboards and abuse prevention rely on our legitimate interest in running a small, free site, using as little data as possible.
  • Access, correction and deletion. You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it (for example a leaderboard name, a report or your newsletter address). Most data here isn’t linked to a name, so tell us what to look for, such as your nickname and the game.
  • Withdrawing consent and objecting. You can unsubscribe at any time or object to how we use your data.
  • Complaints. If you’re not happy with our reply, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, or your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner’s Office).

Children

The puzzles are suitable for all ages, but we don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. Children should use a nickname on leaderboards, never their real name, and shouldn’t send us their email address. If you think a child has given us personal information, email us and we’ll delete it.

Health information

Please don’t send us personal health information. We don’t give personal medical advice, so we have no reason to keep it, and we’ll delete any we receive.

Contact

Email maladywise@gmail.com with any privacy question or request. We’ll reply within 30 days.
