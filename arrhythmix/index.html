About Arrhythmix

Arrhythmix is a free ECG quiz, released twice a week, every Monday and Thursday. Each one brings a short fictional case and an ECG drawn on standard paper, from rhythm strips to full 12-leads. Pick the diagnosis from six options in three tries. Afterwards, the key features are highlighted on the trace, with a teaching pearl.

How to play

  • Read the short case and the ECG. Tap Zoom to enlarge it.
  • Pick the diagnosis from six options. You get three tries.
  • Each wrong pick unlocks a hint.
  • Finish to see the key features highlighted on the trace.

Who it’s for

Students, nurses, paramedics, doctors and anyone learning ECG interpretation. Rhythm strips are usually gentler; 12-leads are harder.

Common questions

Is there a free ECG quiz?

Yes. Arrhythmix is a free ECG quiz with a new case every Monday and Thursday. Past ECGs make good practice too.

Are the ECGs real?

No. Every trace is computer-generated to show the textbook features, and every case is fictional. General education, not medical advice.

Is Arrhythmix free?

Yes. It's free to play in your browser, with no sign-up, no download and no ads.

When is the next ECG?

Every Monday and Thursday at midnight, Sydney time.
