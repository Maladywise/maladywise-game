Fact sheet

Stress incontinence

Leaking when you cough, sneeze, laugh, jump or lift. It’s very common, especially after childbirth and menopause, and supervised pelvic floor training cures or improves most people.

Why it happens

  • The pelvic floor muscles and tissues that support the urethra are weakened or stretched.
  • Risks: pregnancy and vaginal birth, menopause, chronic cough, constipation and straining, obesity, heavy lifting, high-impact sport.
  • In men, it’s most often after prostate surgery.

Treatment

  • Pelvic floor muscle training, ideally supervised by a continence physiotherapist, for at least 3 months: the first-line treatment, with good evidence.
  • Lose weight if overweight; treat cough and constipation.
  • Vaginal pessaries or continence devices support the urethra, handy for exercise.
  • Vaginal oestrogen may help tissue health after menopause.
  • Surgery if conservative treatment fails: colposuspension, autologous fascial sling, urethral bulking injections, or a mid-urethral sling. Transvaginal mesh for prolapse was removed in Australia in 2017–18; mid-urethral slings for stress incontinence remain available with stricter consent and credentialing. Discuss risks carefully.

When to see a doctor

  • Leaks are bothering you or stopping you exercising
  • Blood in the urine, pain, or recurrent UTIs
  • A bulge or dragging feeling in the vagina (possible prolapse)

Where to get help

  • National Continence Helpline: 1800 33 00 66 (free)

Common questions

Can pelvic floor exercises cure stress incontinence?

For many people, yes. Supervised training for at least 3 months cures or greatly improves most mild to moderate stress incontinence.

Is mesh still used for incontinence in Australia?

Transvaginal mesh for prolapse was removed in 2017–18. Mid-urethral slings for stress incontinence are still available, with stricter consent rules, alongside non-mesh operations.

Does stress incontinence get worse with age?

It can, especially after menopause, but pelvic floor training helps at any age.
