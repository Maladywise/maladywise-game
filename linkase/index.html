About Linkase

Linkase is a free daily medical word chain game. You see the first and last words of a six-word chain; each word pairs with the next to make a common phrase or term, like blood → pressure → sore → throat. Work in from either end to fill the gaps before the chain snaps.

How to play

  • Guess the hidden words from the top or the bottom of the chain.
  • Each word must make a common phrase with the one before it.
  • Each wrong guess reveals another letter and costs a strike.
  • Five strikes and the chain snaps.

Who it’s for

Word-game lovers, students, nurses, doctors and anyone who says 'blood pressure' more than they'd like.

Common questions

Is Linkase free?

Yes. It's free to play in your browser, with no sign-up, no download and no ads.

How do hints work?

Each wrong guess reveals another letter of the next word, at the cost of a strike.

When is there a new chain?

Every day at midnight, Sydney time. Past chains are in the archive.
