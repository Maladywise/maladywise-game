Fact sheet

Chronic Rhinosinusitis

A blocked, runny nose with facial pressure or a lost sense of smell for 12 weeks or more. It’s long-term inflammation, not a lingering infection, so antibiotics rarely fix it. Daily rinses and a steroid spray, used properly, are the backbone.

Symptoms (for 12 weeks or more)

  • Blocked or congested nose, and/or discharge down the front or back of the throat
  • Facial pain or pressure
  • Reduced or lost sense of smell, a strong clue to nasal polyps
  • Some have nasal polyps (soft, grape-like swellings); others don’t

See a doctor urgently for swelling around the eye, double vision, a severe headache, confusion or a stiff neck. A one-sided blocked nose with bleeding also needs checking.

Save your money

Popular, heavily marketed or often asked about, but the evidence is weak or says no.

  • Repeated antibiotic coursesChronic rhinosinusitis is mostly inflammation, not infection. Antibiotics help only flare-ups with signs of bacterial infection.
  • Decongestant sprays for more than 5 daysOxymetazoline and similar sprays cause rebound blockage (rhinitis medicamentosa).
  • Antihistamines without allergiesThey don’t reduce sinus inflammation unless allergy is part of the problem.

Weird but true

  • Some people with polyps and asthma react to aspirin and anti-inflammatories with wheeze and a streaming nose. It’s called aspirin-exacerbated respiratory disease (Samter’s triad).

Common questions

What is chronic rhinosinusitis?

Inflammation of the nose and sinuses lasting 12 weeks or more, causing blockage, discharge, facial pressure or reduced smell, with or without nasal polyps.

Do antibiotics cure chronic sinusitis?

Usually not. It’s mainly inflammation, so daily saltwater rinses and a steroid nasal spray are the mainstays. Antibiotics are kept for flare-ups with signs of bacterial infection.

When is sinus surgery needed?

When symptoms persist despite at least 2–3 months of daily rinses and steroid spray, or with large polyps. Surgery opens the sinuses so treatment can reach inside.
