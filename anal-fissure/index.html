Fact sheet

Anal Fissure

A small tear in the lining of the anus, usually from passing a hard stool. It causes sharp pain during and for hours after a bowel motion, often with bright red blood on the paper. Most heal within 6 to 8 weeks.

Treatment options

Ratings show how strong the research is.

  • Strong evidenceSurgery (sphincterotomy)A small cut in the anal muscle for fissures that don’t heal. Heals about 9 in 10, but carries a small risk of leaking wind or stool.
  • Moderate evidenceFibre, water and stool softenerKeeps stools soft so the tear can heal. The first step for everyone.
  • Moderate evidenceGlyceryl trinitrate (GTN) ointmentRelaxes the muscle and improves blood flow. 0.2% ointment twice daily for 6 to 8 weeks, from a pharmacist. Headaches are common.
  • Moderate evidenceDiltiazem or nifedipine ointmentWorks about as well as GTN with fewer headaches. Made by a compounding pharmacy on prescription.
  • Limited evidenceBotulinum toxin injectionRelaxes the muscle for 2 to 3 months. An option from a surgeon if ointments fail.
  • Limited evidenceWarm bathsSit in warm water for 10 to 15 minutes, 2 to 3 times a day, especially after a bowel motion.
  • Limited evidenceNumbing creamEases pain short-term but doesn’t help healing.

What you can do

  • Aim for 25 to 30 g of fibre a day: legumes, wholegrains, fruit and vegetables.
  • Go when you feel the urge. Don’t strain or put it off.
  • Paracetamol helps. Avoid codeine, which causes constipation.
  • Raise your knees on a footstool and pat dry gently.

See your GP if

  • Pain is severe or not improving after 2 weeks
  • Not healed after 8 weeks, or keeps coming back
  • You also have diarrhoea, weight loss or tummy pain

See a doctor the same day for a hot, swollen, very painful lump near the anus, fever, or heavy bleeding.

Common questions

How long does an anal fissure take to heal?

Most heal within 6 to 8 weeks once stools are kept soft. See your GP if it hasn’t healed after 8 weeks or keeps coming back.

Does GTN ointment work for a fissure?

Yes, it relaxes the anal muscle and improves blood flow so the tear can heal. It’s used twice daily for 6 to 8 weeks, and headaches are common. Diltiazem ointment works about as well with fewer headaches.

Is it a fissure or haemorrhoids?

A fissure typically causes sharp pain during and for hours after a bowel motion, with bright red blood on the paper. Haemorrhoids more often cause bleeding, itch or a lump. Your GP can check which it is.

When is surgery needed for an anal fissure?

Only for fissures that don’t heal with fibre and ointments. A small cut in the anal muscle heals about 9 in 10, but carries a small risk of leaking wind or stool.
