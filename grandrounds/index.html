About Grand Rounds

Grand Rounds is a free weekly hard medical trivia quiz, released every Sunday. Twenty medical and medical-adjacent questions with no multiple choice, ending with a brain-melter. Play solo, or head to head with a friend: one reads, the other answers. Every answer comes with a short explanation.

How to play

  • Read each question and say or think your answer.
  • Tap Show answer, then mark whether you got it. Honesty optional.
  • Pick Head to head to play against a friend.
  • Ten or more out of twenty is a good Sunday.

Who it’s for

Doctors, nurses, students and anyone who likes a genuinely hard medical quiz. Great for study groups and staff rooms.

Common questions

Is there a hard medical quiz for doctors?

Yes. Grand Rounds is a free weekly set of twenty hard medical trivia questions.

Is Grand Rounds free?

Yes. It's free to play in your browser, with no sign-up, no download and no ads.

Can I play with a friend?

Yes. Head to head mode works well: one reads, the other answers.

When is the new quiz?

Every Sunday at midnight, Sydney time. Past quizzes and answers are in the archive.
