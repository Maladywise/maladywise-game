Seeing a Psychologist

Talking therapy works for depression, anxiety and much more. In Australia, Medicare helps pay for up to 10 sessions a year, and there are free options while you wait.

  3. Step 2 · PsychologistUp to 6 sessions, then a reviewYour plan refers you for an initial course of up to 6 individual sessions. Go back to your GP for a review to unlock the rest of your 10 for the year.

    Running out of sessions isn’t the end of the road.

Choosing a psychologist

  • General vs clinical psychologist: both are registered and trained; clinical psychologists have extra training and a higher Medicare rebate.
  • Ask about the gap fee before booking, and whether they offer bulk billing or reduced fees.
  • Look for fit: the issues they work with, their approach (such as CBT, ACT or trauma-focused therapy), and whether you feel heard.
  • Telehealth (video or phone) counts towards your sessions and can widen your choice.
  • You can’t use private health insurance to top up the Medicare rebate: claim one or the other.

See your GP if

  • Low mood, worry or stress has lasted more than two weeks or is affecting work, study or relationships
  • You’re using alcohol or other drugs to cope
  • Sleep, appetite or energy have changed a lot

Call 000 (or your local emergency number) if you or someone else is in immediate danger. Lifeline 13 11 14 and Suicide Call Back Service 1300 659 467 answer 24/7.

Common questions

How many Medicare psychology sessions can I get?

Up to 10 individual sessions each calendar year under Better Access, plus up to 10 group sessions. Your first referral covers up to 6; a review with your GP unlocks the rest.

Do I need a mental health plan to see a psychologist?

Not to see one privately, but you need a Mental Health Treatment Plan (or a referral from a psychiatrist or paediatrician) to claim the Medicare rebate.

Who can write my mental health plan?

Since 1 November 2025, your usual GP or a GP at the practice that provides most of your care. You don’t need to be registered with MyMedicare.

How much does a psychologist cost with Medicare?

Psychologists set their own fees. Medicare pays a fixed rebate per session, and most people pay a gap of tens of dollars or more. Ask before you book.

Is there free mental health support?

Yes. The Medicare Mental Health phone service (1800 595 212), Lifeline (13 11 14), Beyond Blue and many PHN-funded programs are free.

Can I see a psychologist by video?

Yes. Telehealth sessions can count towards your Medicare sessions.
