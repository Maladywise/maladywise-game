About Sudokrine

Sudokrine is a free daily medical sudoku with a hidden word. Each grid uses the letters of a medical word with no repeated letters, often a hormone or gland. Fill every row, column and box with each letter once, and the shaded line spells the word. Early in the week it's a 6x6 mini; Friday to Sunday it's a full 9x9.

How to play

  • Tap a square, then tap a letter. Notes mode adds pencil marks.
  • Every row, column and box gets each letter exactly once.
  • Guess the hidden word under the grid before you finish for a Word bonus.
  • Prefer digits? Tick Show numbers.

Who it’s for

Sudoku lovers of every kind. You don't need any medicine: the grid is pure logic.

Common questions

Is there a medical sudoku?

Yes. Sudokrine is a free daily medical sudoku that hides a medical word in the grid.

Is Sudokrine free?

Yes. It's free to play in your browser, with no sign-up, no download and no ads.

Do I need to know any medicine?

No. The grid is pure logic; medicine only helps you guess the word early.

Is there always one answer?

Yes. Every grid is computer-checked to have exactly one solution, solvable by logic alone.
