How it works
  • Organs fall into the 10 × 20 board. Move and rotate them as they fall.
  • Fill a whole row and it clears. Four rows at once is a transplant, worth the most.
  • Hold tucks an organ away for later (once per drop). The faint outline shows where it will land.
  • Combos: clear rows on back-to-back drops for a code blue combo bonus. A cleared row made of one organ type is a perfect match, like a matched donor: triple points.
  • Every 10 rows: level up, faster drops and a new organ fact.
  • Sound: soft monitor beeps (and a buzz on phones). Tap 🔊 in the game bar to mute.
  • Daily challenge: everyone gets the same organs in the same order (new each midnight, Sydney time). Your first go each day is scored; replays are practice.
  • On a phone, use the buttons under the board, or swipe the board: drag to move, tap to rotate, flick down to drop.

        About Stackulus

        Stackulus is a free falling-block arcade game where the blocks are organs. Move and rotate them as they fall, fill whole rows to clear them, and send each patient home. It speeds up every level, and every level brings a light organ fact. The daily challenge gives everyone the same organs in the same order.

        How to play

        • Move and rotate each organ as it falls.
        • Fill a whole row to clear it. Four rows at once is a transplant.
        • Hold tucks an organ away for later.
        • Your first daily challenge go each day is scored for the world board.

        Who it’s for

        Anyone who likes classic falling-block games, from students on a break to clinicians on a night shift.

        Common questions

        Is Stackulus free?
