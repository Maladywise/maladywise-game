Gaslitis

You're the doctor, and every shortcut is a bias. Dismiss eight patients, watch how they react, then see what you missed.

Ageism, weight bias, stigma, overshadowing, racial and gender bias. Every line is something real patients report hearing.

How to play: 25 seconds per patient. Play up to 3 cards. Every dismissal card is a type of bias, and each one makes the patient more upset and less likely to come back. Drain their confidence and they leave. Run out of time and they're rushed out. Or play the green card and do the work. Each reveal shows the diagnosis, the harm, and the evidence on who usually gets dismissed.
