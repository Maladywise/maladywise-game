How a round works

  • Patient: draw Gerald's complaint. The real diagnosis stays hidden.
  • Dismiss: play up to 3 dismissal or label cards. Each scores gaslight power and time saved.
  • Reveal: see what he really had, the test that would have caught it, and the real-world fact about who usually gets dismissed.
  • Score: high gaslight score = worst doctor. The end screen links to the GP School Blind spots room.
