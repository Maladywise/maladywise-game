Drug info

Saw palmetto

Herbal medicine Complementary medicine (AUST L) or food

Popular for prostate symptoms, but large trials and a Cochrane review found it no better than placebo.

Quick answer: A Cochrane review of 27 trials found saw palmetto doesn’t improve urinary symptoms or flow from an enlarged prostate, even at double or triple doses. Proven medicines (like tamsulosin and finasteride) work. It’s generally well tolerated.

How it works

Thought to block testosterone conversion, like finasteride, but this doesn’t translate into symptom relief.

Used for

  • Hair loss

What the evidence says

From systematic reviews and large trials where they exist. ✅ good evidence · 🟡 some or mixed evidence · ❌ doesn’t work.

  • 🟡 Hair lossVery small, low-quality studies.
  • ❌ Enlarged prostate symptomsNo better than placebo (Cochrane).

Avoid or check first if

  • You take blood thinners
  • You’re having a PSA test (tell your doctor what you take)

Side effects

Most people have no side effects at usual doses. Supplements can still interact with medicines: tell your doctor and pharmacist what you take.

Common questions

Does saw palmetto shrink the prostate?

No. Trials show no improvement in symptoms or flow.

What works for an enlarged prostate?

Medicines like tamsulosin and finasteride, and procedures if needed. Talk to your GP.
