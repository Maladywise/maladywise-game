Drug info

Tamsulosin

Alpha blocker Prescription only (S4)

Relaxes the muscle at the prostate and bladder neck, so urine flows more easily. Also helps kidney stones pass.

Quick answer: Tamsulosin improves symptoms of an enlarged prostate (weak stream, hesitancy, frequent urination) within days to weeks. It doesn’t shrink the prostate. It also helps some kidney stones pass. Tell your eye surgeon you take it before cataract surgery.

Brand names

  • Flomaxtra, Tamsil, Tamsulosin generics; Duodart (with dutasteride)

How it works

It blocks alpha-1A receptors in the prostate and bladder neck muscle.

Used for

  • Enlarged prostate (benign prostatic hyperplasia)
  • Helping ureteric stones up to about 10 mm pass (off-label)
  • Combined with dutasteride or finasteride for larger prostates

Side effects

Usually mild.

Full side-effect list (from the product information)

How often, from the official product information. Bold = serious.

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Dizziness, especially standing up
  • Reduced or no ejaculate
  • Stuffy nose

Uncommon 1 in 100 to 1 in 1,000

  • Fainting
  • Palpitations

Rare 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000

  • Floppy iris syndrome during cataract surgery
  • Prolonged painful erection

Get help urgently if

  • Unable to pass urine: emergency
  • Fainting
  • Erection lasting over 4 hours

Good to know

  • Stand up slowly, especially with the first doses.
  • Tell your eye surgeon before cataract surgery.
  • Swallow capsules whole.
  • See your doctor yearly to review symptoms and need.

Common questions

How long does tamsulosin take to work?

Many men notice better flow within a few days, with the full effect in 2 to 4 weeks.

Does tamsulosin affect ejaculation?

Commonly, it reduces or stops ejaculate fluid. It’s harmless and reverses on stopping.

Does tamsulosin help kidney stones?

It can help larger stones (about 5 to 10 mm) in the lower ureter pass.
