Drug info

Methotrexate

Disease-modifying medicine (DMARD) Prescription only (S4)

The cornerstone of rheumatoid arthritis treatment. Taken ONCE A WEEK: daily dosing by mistake can be fatal.

Quick answer: Low-dose methotrexate calms the immune system in rheumatoid arthritis, psoriasis, psoriatic arthritis and other conditions. It’s taken once weekly, with folic acid on another day. Regular blood tests check blood counts, liver and kidneys. It’s harmful in pregnancy.

At a glance

For
Rheumatoid and psoriatic arthritis, psoriasis, some vasculitis, Crohn’s disease, other autoimmune conditions
Works
Improves symptoms and slows joint damage in most people with RA
Cost
PBS: up to $25 a script ($7.70 concession)
Kicks in
6 to 12 weeks
Dose
Usually 7.5 to 25 mg ONCE A WEEK (tablets or injection), plus folic acid on a different day
Watch for
Nausea, mouth ulcers, tiredness, infections

Once a week. Never daily.

Brand names

  • Methoblastin, Trexject, Metoject (injections)

How it works

At low doses it has anti-inflammatory effects (partly via adenosine) and reduces overactive immune cells.

Used for

  • Rheumatoid arthritis
  • Psoriatic arthritis and psoriasis
  • Polymyalgia and vasculitis (steroid-sparing)
  • Crohn’s disease
  • Ectopic pregnancy and cancers (different doses and settings)

Side effects

Folic acid reduces many side effects.

Full side-effect list (from the product information)

How often, from the official product information. Bold = serious.

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Nausea, especially the day after
  • Mouth ulcers
  • Tiredness
  • Raised liver tests

Uncommon 1 in 100 to 1 in 1,000

  • Hair thinning
  • Low blood counts
  • Infections

Rare 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000

  • Lung inflammation (cough, breathlessness)
  • Severe bone marrow suppression, especially with trimethoprim or overdose

Get help urgently if

  • New cough, fever or breathlessness
  • Sore throat, mouth ulcers and bruising together
  • Accidentally taken more than once in a week: seek advice today

Good to know

  • Take on the same day each week; mark it on a calendar.
  • Avoid trimethoprim and co-trimoxazole; check new medicines with your pharmacist.
  • Keep alcohol low.
  • Plan pregnancy with your specialist: methotrexate is stopped beforehand (both partners should discuss).
  • Get the recommended vaccines; avoid live vaccines unless advised.

Common questions

Why is methotrexate taken weekly?

At weekly doses it calms the immune system safely. Taking the same dose daily can cause life-threatening toxicity.

Why do I take folic acid with methotrexate?

Folic acid reduces nausea, mouth ulcers and liver effects, without reducing benefit. Take it on a different day from methotrexate.

Can I drink alcohol on methotrexate?

Small amounts may be OK for some people, but both affect the liver. Ask your specialist.
