Drug info

Iron tablets

Iron supplement Pharmacy and supermarket (unscheduled/S2)

Cheap and effective for iron deficiency, if you take them in a way your gut can absorb and tolerate.

Quick answer: Iron tablets rebuild iron stores and haemoglobin. Studies show taking a dose once daily or on alternate days absorbs as well or better than several doses a day, with fewer side effects, because a dose raises hepcidin, a hormone that blocks absorption for about a day. Check the elemental iron content: brands vary a lot.

Brand names

  • Ferro-Grad C, Ferro-Tab, FGF, Maltofer, Ferro-Liquid; check the elemental iron per tablet

How it works

Iron is absorbed in the upper small bowel. After a dose, the liver releases hepcidin, which temporarily reduces absorption of the next dose.

Used for

  • Iron deficiency (low ferritin)
  • Iron deficiency anaemia
  • Pregnancy (when low or as advised)
  • After blood loss

Side effects

Gut side effects are the main reason people stop.

Good to know

  • Take on an empty stomach if you can, or with a little food if it upsets you.
  • Vitamin C (or orange juice) can help absorption; avoid tea, coffee, calcium and antacids within 2 hours.
  • Alternate-day dosing is a good option if side effects are a problem.
  • Recheck ferritin and haemoglobin after about 3 months; ask why you’re low (periods, diet, coeliac disease, bleeding).

Common questions

Is it better to take iron every second day?

Studies in iron-deficient women found alternate-day dosing absorbed as much or more iron overall, with fewer side effects, than daily doses.

Why are my stools black on iron?

Unabsorbed iron turns stools black. It’s harmless. Black, sticky stools with dizziness need urgent review.

When is an iron infusion better?

If tablets don’t work, aren’t tolerated, or iron is needed quickly (e.g. late pregnancy, before surgery, or absorption problems).
