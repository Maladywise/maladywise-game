Drug info

Atomoxetine

Non-stimulant ADHD medicine Prescription only (S4)

A non-stimulant ADHD capsule that works all day, every day. Slower and a bit less powerful than stimulants, but no peaks, crashes or controlled-drug paperwork.

Quick answer: Atomoxetine is a once- or twice-daily non-stimulant for ADHD. It takes 4–6 weeks to work, sometimes 12, and smooths symptoms across the whole day, including mornings and evenings. It’s a good option when stimulants don’t suit, cause anxiety or tics, or when misuse is a worry. Common side effects are nausea, less appetite, tiredness and, in adults, erection or ejaculation problems.

No buzz, no crash, no S8 forms. The trade-off is patience: give it 6 weeks before you judge it.

Brand names

  • Strattera, and generic atomoxetine brands

How it works

Atomoxetine blocks the reuptake of noradrenaline (and, in the front of the brain, dopamine), so more is available where attention and impulse control are managed. Unlike stimulants, it doesn’t act on the brain’s reward centre, so it isn’t habit-forming.

Used for

  • ADHD in children aged 6 and over, teenagers and adults (TGA-approved)
  • Often chosen when stimulants cause anxiety, tics, poor sleep or appetite loss, or when misuse is a concern
  • Can be combined with a stimulant under specialist guidance

Side effects

Nausea, less appetite and tummy pain are the most common, especially early; taking it with food helps. Tiredness or trouble sleeping, mood changes and a slightly higher pulse and blood pressure are common. Adults may notice erection, ejaculation or period changes.

Get help urgently if

  • Thoughts of self-harm, or a big change in mood or behaviour. Call 000 or Lifeline 13 11 14
  • Yellow skin or eyes, dark urine or itching with tummy pain (liver)
  • An erection lasting more than 4 hours
  • Chest pain, fainting or a racing heartbeat

Good to know

  • Give it time. Judge it at 6–12 weeks at the right dose.
  • Not a controlled (S8) medicine: no state permit, and it’s fine to travel with.
  • Some antidepressants (fluoxetine, paroxetine) and bupropion raise atomoxetine levels a lot. Tell your doctor what else you take.
  • Not with MAO inhibitors. Caution with heart disease or high blood pressure: pulse and blood pressure are checked.
  • Can be stopped without tapering, but symptoms return.

Common questions

How long does atomoxetine take to work?

Some effect may show in 1–2 weeks, but full benefit usually takes 4–6 weeks and can take up to 12. Judge it after that, at the right dose.

Is atomoxetine a stimulant?

No. It’s a non-stimulant that works on noradrenaline. It isn’t habit-forming and isn’t a controlled (S8) medicine.

What are the side effects of atomoxetine?

Nausea, tummy pain and less appetite are the most common, especially early. Tiredness, mood changes, higher pulse and blood pressure, and sexual side effects in adults can also happen. Low mood or suicidal thoughts in young people need urgent review.
