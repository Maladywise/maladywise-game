Drug info

Biotin

Vitamin supplement Complementary medicine (AUST L) or food

Popular for hair and nails, but only helps if you’re deficient, which is rare. High doses can make blood tests wrong, including heart attack tests.

Quick answer: Biotin deficiency is rare, and there’s no good evidence biotin improves hair or nails in people who aren’t deficient. High-dose biotin (often 5,000 to 10,000 mcg in hair products) interferes with many lab tests, giving falsely normal troponin (heart attack test) or falsely abnormal thyroid results.

How it works

Biotin is a B vitamin used in energy metabolism. Many lab tests use biotin-streptavidin chemistry, which extra biotin in the blood disrupts.

Used for

  • Biotin deficiency

What the evidence says

From systematic reviews and large trials where they exist. ✅ good evidence · 🟡 some or mixed evidence · ❌ doesn’t work.

  • ✅ Biotin deficiencyRare: some inherited conditions, long-term raw egg white intake, some anticonvulsants.
  • ❌ Hair loss or brittle nails in people with normal biotinNo good evidence of benefit.

Avoid or check first if

  • You’re having blood tests: tell the doctor, and stop biotin 2 to 3 days before (longer for very high doses)

Side effects

Most people have no side effects at usual doses. Supplements can still interact with medicines: tell your doctor and pharmacist what you take.

Get help urgently if

  • Signs of an allergic reaction: swelling of the face or throat, trouble breathing (call 000)

Good to know

  • Hair loss has many causes (iron, thyroid, hormones, stress): see your GP rather than buying biotin.
  • Check ‘hair, skin and nails’ products for biotin content.

Common questions

Does biotin help hair growth?

Only if you’re deficient, which is rare. Studies in people with normal levels show no benefit.

Why does biotin affect blood tests?

Many lab tests use biotin as part of the measuring chemistry. High blood biotin can make results falsely high or low, including troponin and thyroid tests.
