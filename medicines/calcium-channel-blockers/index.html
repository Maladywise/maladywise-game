Drug info

Calcium channel blockers

Blood pressure medicine Prescription only (S4)

Some of the most prescribed blood pressure medicines in the world. They relax artery walls; some also slow the heart. Ankle swelling is the classic side effect.

Quick answer: Calcium channel blockers lower blood pressure by relaxing the muscle in artery walls. Amlodipine, felodipine and lercanidipine mainly work on arteries and are first-line blood pressure medicines. Diltiazem and verapamil also slow the heart, so they’re used for angina and some fast heart rhythms.

Quietly effective, once a day, and the main complaint is puffy ankles.

Brand names

  • Amlodipine: Norvasc and generics. Felodipine: Plendil. Lercanidipine: Zanidip. Diltiazem: Cardizem, Vasocardol. Verapamil: Isoptin. Combinations with sartans or statins (e.g. Exforge, Caduet)

How it works

They block calcium from entering muscle cells in artery walls (and, for diltiazem and verapamil, the heart). Less calcium means the muscle relaxes, arteries widen and pressure falls.

Used for

  • High blood pressure (first-line choice, alone or with an ACE inhibitor or sartan)
  • Angina
  • Raynaud’s phenomenon (nifedipine, amlodipine, off label for some)
  • Diltiazem and verapamil: slowing a fast heart rate in atrial fibrillation; verapamil for cluster headache prevention

Side effects

Most people tolerate them well. Side effects often relate to widened blood vessels.

Get help urgently if

  • Fainting, very slow pulse or severe dizziness
  • Chest pain that’s new or worse: call 000
  • Yellow skin or eyes

Good to know

  • Grapefruit juice can raise levels of some (felodipine, nifedipine, verapamil).
  • Diltiazem and verapamil interact with many medicines, including some statins; don’t combine them with beta-blockers without specialist advice.
  • Ankle swelling doesn’t respond to diuretics; a lower dose or switching usually fixes it.

Common questions

Why does amlodipine cause swollen ankles?

It widens arteries more than veins, so fluid leaks from tiny vessels in the legs. It isn’t heart failure and water tablets don’t help much; a lower dose or another medicine can.

Is amlodipine a good blood pressure tablet?

Yes. It’s one of the main first-line choices, taken once a day, and works well in older people and people of African or Asian descent.

Can I drink grapefruit juice with amlodipine?

Amlodipine is only mildly affected; felodipine, nifedipine and verapamil are affected more. Check with your pharmacist.
