Drug info

Mesalazine and aminosalicylates

5-aminosalicylate (5-ASA) Prescription only (S4)

The mainstay for mild to moderate ulcerative colitis, acting directly on the bowel lining. Much less useful in Crohn’s disease.

Quick answer: Mesalazine calms inflammation in the bowel lining. It treats flares and keeps ulcerative colitis in remission, as tablets, granules, suppositories or enemas. In Crohn’s disease the evidence is weak, so it’s rarely the main treatment.

At a glance

For
Ulcerative colitis (induction and maintenance); occasionally mild Crohn’s colitis
Works
Well in mild to moderate ulcerative colitis; little benefit in Crohn’s
Cost
PBS: up to $25 a script ($7.70 concession)
Kicks in
2 to 4 weeks
Dose
Often 2 to 4.8 g daily by mouth, once daily is fine; plus rectal forms for distal disease
Watch for
Headache, nausea; rarely kidney inflammation or a flare-like reaction

Colitis workhorse, Crohn’s bystander.

Brand names

  • Mesalazine: Salofalk, Pentasa, Mezavant, Asacol. Sulfasalazine: Salazopyrin. Balsalazide: Colazide. Olsalazine: Dipentum

How it works

It acts topically on the bowel lining to reduce inflammatory chemical production.

Used for

  • Ulcerative colitis: flares and maintenance
  • Proctitis (suppositories)
  • Sulfasalazine also treats inflammatory arthritis

Side effects

Usually well tolerated.

Full side-effect list (from the product information)

How often, from the official product information. Bold = serious.

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Headache
  • Nausea, tummy pain
  • Rash

Uncommon 1 in 100 to 1 in 1,000

  • Worsening diarrhoea (intolerance can mimic a flare)

Rare 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000

  • Kidney inflammation (interstitial nephritis)
  • Pancreatitis
  • Inflammation of the heart lining
  • Low blood counts (sulfasalazine)

Get help urgently if

  • Fever, rash and worsening diarrhoea after starting
  • Chest pain
  • Severe tummy pain

Good to know

  • Kidney function blood tests before starting and then yearly.
  • Rectal forms work best for disease in the lower bowel.
  • Sulfasalazine can turn urine and contact lenses orange and lowers sperm count (reversible).

Common questions

Is mesalazine used for Crohn’s disease?

Rarely. It works well for ulcerative colitis but has little benefit in most Crohn’s disease.

Do I need blood tests on mesalazine?

Yes, kidney function tests before starting and usually yearly.

Can I take mesalazine once a day?

Yes, once-daily dosing works as well as divided doses for most people.
