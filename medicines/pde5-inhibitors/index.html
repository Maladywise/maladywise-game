Drug info

PDE5 inhibitors

Medicine family Prescription only (S4)

Sildenafil, tadalafil and vardenafil. They don’t create desire; they help blood flow do its job when you’re aroused. Also used for prostate symptoms and high blood pressure in the lungs.

Quick answer: PDE5 inhibitors (sildenafil, tadalafil, vardenafil) treat erectile dysfunction by improving blood flow when you’re sexually aroused. They work for about 2 in 3 men. Tadalafil lasts up to 36 hours and, at 5 mg daily, also eases prostate urine symptoms. Common side effects are headache, flushing, a blocked nose and indigestion. Never take them with nitrates (angina sprays or tablets) or amyl nitrite (“poppers”).

Plumbing, not mood. It needs arousal to work, and it’s a check-up prompt: erection problems can be an early sign of heart disease.

Brand names

  • Sildenafil: Viagra and many generics
  • Tadalafil: Cialis and many generics
  • Vardenafil: Levitra and generics

How it works

An erection happens when nitric oxide relaxes the blood vessels in the penis so blood flows in. PDE5 is the enzyme that switches that signal off. Blocking it keeps the signal on for longer, but only once arousal has started it.

Used for

  • Erectile dysfunction (TGA-approved)
  • Prostate enlargement urine symptoms: tadalafil 5 mg daily (TGA-approved)
  • Pulmonary arterial hypertension: specific brands and doses, specialist-prescribed
  • Off label: Raynaud’s phenomenon, altitude sickness prevention in some people, sexual side effects of antidepressants

Side effects

Headache and flushing are the most common, followed by a blocked nose, indigestion and dizziness. Tadalafil more often causes back and muscle aches; sildenafil can tint vision blue. Serious effects are rare.

Get help urgently if

  • An erection lasting more than 4 hours: go to an emergency department
  • Sudden loss of vision or hearing
  • Chest pain during or after sex: stop, don’t take a nitrate, call 000 and tell them you took a PDE5 inhibitor
  • Fainting

Good to know

  • Never with nitrates (glyceryl trinitrate spray or tablets, isosorbide) or “poppers”: blood pressure can crash. Not with riociguat.
  • Alpha blockers (e.g. tamsulosin, prazosin): start low and space doses; dizziness is more likely.
  • Grapefruit and some antibiotics, antifungals and HIV medicines raise levels.
  • Avoid heavy alcohol; it worsens dizziness and erections.
  • Get checked: erection problems can be an early sign of heart disease, diabetes or low testosterone. Buy only from an Australian pharmacy: online fakes are common.

Common questions

Do PDE5 inhibitors work without arousal?

No. They help blood flow once you’re sexually aroused, but they don’t create desire or an erection on their own.

What’s the difference between sildenafil and tadalafil?

Sildenafil works for about 4–5 hours and is slowed by fatty food. Tadalafil lasts up to 36 hours, isn’t affected by food, and can be taken as 5 mg daily, which also helps prostate urine symptoms.

Why can’t you take Viagra with nitrates?

Both lower blood pressure, and together they can cause a dangerous drop. That includes angina sprays and tablets and amyl nitrite (“poppers”).
