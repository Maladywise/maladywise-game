Probiotics

Probiotic supplement Complementary medicine (AUST L) or food

Some specific strains help prevent antibiotic-related diarrhoea. For general ‘gut health’, the evidence is thin, and results depend on the exact strain.

Quick answer: Certain probiotics (such as Saccharomyces boulardii and Lactobacillus rhamnosus GG) reduce diarrhoea from antibiotics. Some strains may ease IBS symptoms. For general wellbeing, immunity or weight, good evidence is lacking. Probiotics are strain-specific, so a result for one product doesn’t apply to another.

How it works

Live microbes that may temporarily change the gut environment, crowd out harmful bacteria and interact with the gut’s immune system.

Used for

  • Preventing antibiotic-associated diarrhoea
  • IBS
  • Preventing necrotising enterocolitis in premature babies

What the evidence says

From systematic reviews and large trials where they exist. ✅ good evidence · 🟡 some or mixed evidence · ❌ doesn’t work.

  • ✅ Preventing antibiotic-associated diarrhoeaSpecific strains reduce the risk, including C. difficile diarrhoea.
  • 🟡 IBSSome strains help symptoms in some people; try one for 4 weeks.
  • 🟡 Preventing necrotising enterocolitis in premature babiesUsed in neonatal units under specialist care.
  • ❌ General gut health and immunity in healthy peopleNo convincing evidence.
  • ❌ Weight lossNo good evidence.

Avoid or check first if

  • You’re immunocompromised, critically ill, or have a central line or artificial heart valve

Side effects

Most people have no side effects at usual doses. Supplements can still interact with medicines: tell your doctor and pharmacist what you take.

Good to know

  • Choose a product with the specific strain that was studied for your problem.
  • Take a few hours apart from the antibiotic dose.
  • Fermented foods (yoghurt, kefir) are a food-first option.

Common questions

Should I take a probiotic with antibiotics?

It may lower your chance of antibiotic-related diarrhoea, especially with Saccharomyces boulardii or Lactobacillus rhamnosus GG.

Do probiotics help everyday gut health?

There’s little good evidence for healthy people. Results depend on the exact strain.
