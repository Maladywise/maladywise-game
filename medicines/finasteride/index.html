Drug info

Finasteride

5-alpha reductase inhibitor Prescription only (S4)

Blocks the hormone that shrinks hair follicles and enlarges the prostate. Effective for both, with a small risk of sexual and mood side effects.

Quick answer: Finasteride lowers DHT, the androgen behind male pattern hair loss and prostate enlargement. At 1 mg it stops hair loss in most men; at 5 mg it shrinks the prostate. Sexual side effects affect a small minority. Regulators warn about mood changes, including depression.

At a glance

For
Male pattern hair loss (1 mg); enlarged prostate (5 mg)
Works
Hair: about 80 to 90% of men stop losing hair; prostate: shrinks about 20 to 30% over months
Cost
Hair: private script, about $15 to $40 a month; prostate: PBS
Kicks in
Hair 3 to 6 months; prostate 3 to 6 months
Dose
1 mg daily (hair); 5 mg daily (prostate)
Watch for
Lower libido, erection or ejaculation changes, low mood

A small tablet with a big following, and a warning label.

Brand names

  • Hair: Propecia, Finpecia, generics. Prostate: Proscar, generics. Dutasteride (Avodart, Duodart) is a related medicine

How it works

It blocks 5-alpha reductase type 2, which converts testosterone to DHT.

Used for

  • Male pattern hair loss
  • Enlarged prostate (benign prostatic hyperplasia)
  • Off-label: some women with hair loss after menopause, under specialist care

Side effects

Most men have no side effects.

Full side-effect list (from the product information)

How often, from the official product information. Bold = serious.

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Reduced libido
  • Erectile difficulties
  • Less ejaculate

Uncommon 1 in 100 to 1 in 1,000

  • Breast tenderness or enlargement
  • Low mood or depression

Rare 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000

  • Persistent sexual or mood symptoms after stopping (reported, poorly understood)
  • Allergic reactions

Get help urgently if

  • Low mood or thoughts of self-harm: stop and get help
  • Breast lump or nipple discharge

Good to know

  • Finasteride halves the PSA reading: tell your doctor so they adjust for it.
  • Women who are or may be pregnant shouldn’t handle crushed or broken tablets.
  • Don’t donate blood while taking it, and for a period after.
  • Hair benefit stops if you stop the tablet.

Common questions

Does finasteride cause depression?

Product information warns about mood changes, including depression and, rarely, suicidal thoughts. Stop it and see your doctor if your mood changes.

How effective is finasteride for hair loss?

It stops further loss in most men and regrows some hair in many, with results after 3 to 12 months.

Does finasteride affect PSA?

Yes, it roughly halves PSA, so results need adjusting.
