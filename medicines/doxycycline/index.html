Drug info

Doxycycline

Tetracycline antibiotic Prescription only (S4)

A versatile antibiotic used for chest infections, chlamydia, acne, rosacea and malaria prevention. Take it upright with plenty of water, and watch the sun.

Quick answer: Doxycycline treats a wide range of infections, from pneumonia and chlamydia to Q fever and tick-borne illnesses. At lower doses, it calms acne and rosacea. It must be swallowed with a full glass of water while upright, as it can burn the oesophagus, and it makes the skin burn easily in the sun.

The Swiss Army knife of antibiotics, with a sunburn warning.

Brand names

  • Doryx, Doxsig, Doxy, Vibramycin and generics

How it works

Doxycycline stops bacteria making proteins. It also has anti-inflammatory effects, which is why low doses help acne and rosacea.

Used for

  • Community-acquired pneumonia and chest infections
  • Chlamydia and some other sexually transmitted infections; doxy-PEP for STI prevention in some groups
  • Acne and rosacea
  • Malaria prevention when travelling
  • Q fever, Lyme disease and rickettsial infections (e.g. Queensland tick typhus)
  • Cellulitis caused by MRSA (sometimes)

Side effects

Mostly gut and skin side effects, and oesophagus irritation if not taken properly.

Full side-effect list (from the product information)

How often, from the official product information. Bold = serious.

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Nausea and indigestion
  • Sun sensitivity (sunburn)
  • Diarrhoea

Uncommon 1 in 100 to 1 in 1,000

  • Oesophageal ulcers (pain swallowing)
  • Thrush
  • Headache

Rare 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000

  • Raised pressure in the head (headache, blurred vision)
  • Nail separation in the sun
  • Severe skin reactions

Get help urgently if

  • Painful swallowing or chest pain after a dose
  • Severe headache with vision changes
  • Blistering rash or severe sunburn

Good to know

  • Swallow with a full glass of water, sitting or standing, and don’t lie down for 30 minutes.
  • Take with food if it upsets your stomach; avoid taking with antacids, iron or calcium (separate by 2 to 3 hours).
  • Use strong sun protection.
  • Usually avoided in pregnancy and young children, but short courses are used when needed (e.g. tick-borne infections).

Common questions

Why can’t I lie down after taking doxycycline?

If the capsule sticks in the oesophagus it can cause a painful ulcer. Take it upright with a full glass of water and stay upright for 30 minutes.

Does doxycycline make you sunburn?

Yes. It increases sun sensitivity, so use sunscreen, a hat and cover up.

Can I take doxycycline with milk?

Calcium reduces absorption somewhat. Taking it with a meal is fine, but avoid dairy, antacids, iron and calcium supplements around the dose.
