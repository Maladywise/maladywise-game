Drug info

Ondansetron

5-HT3 antagonist anti-nausea Prescription only (S4)

A highly effective anti-nausea medicine, often as a melt-in-the-mouth wafer. Common in emergency departments for gastro in children.

Quick answer: Ondansetron blocks serotonin signals that trigger vomiting. A single dose helps children with gastro keep fluids down and avoid a drip. It’s used for nausea from chemotherapy, surgery and severe pregnancy sickness.

Brand names

  • Zofran, Ondaz, Onsetron, generics (tablets and wafers)

How it works

It blocks 5-HT3 receptors in the gut and the brain’s vomiting centre.

Used for

  • Gastroenteritis (single dose in children to help oral fluids)
  • Chemotherapy and radiotherapy nausea
  • After surgery
  • Severe nausea and vomiting in pregnancy

Side effects

Usually mild.

Full side-effect list (from the product information)

How often, from the official product information. Bold = serious.

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Constipation
  • Headache
  • Feeling warm or flushed

Uncommon 1 in 100 to 1 in 1,000

  • Dizziness
  • Hiccups

Rare 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000

  • Heart rhythm changes (QT prolongation), mainly at high IV doses or with other QT drugs
  • Serotonin syndrome with other serotonergic drugs

Get help urgently if

  • Signs of dehydration: little urine, drowsy, sunken eyes (especially in children)
  • Palpitations or fainting
  • Vomiting blood or green fluid

Good to know

  • Wafers dissolve on the tongue: no water needed.
  • Keep up small, frequent sips of oral rehydration fluid.
  • Pregnancy: large studies are mostly reassuring; any very small risk of cleft palate in early pregnancy should be discussed.
  • Tell your doctor about heart rhythm problems or other QT-prolonging medicines.

Common questions

Is ondansetron safe in pregnancy?

It’s widely used for severe pregnancy sickness. Large studies are mostly reassuring, with a possible very small increase in cleft palate when used in the first trimester. Discuss with your doctor.

Can children take ondansetron for gastro?

Yes, a single weight-based dose often stops vomiting long enough to rehydrate by mouth.

Does ondansetron cause constipation?

Yes, it’s one of the most common side effects.
