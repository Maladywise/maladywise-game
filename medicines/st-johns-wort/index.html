Drug info

St John’s wort

Herbal medicine Complementary medicine (AUST L) or food

Works for mild to moderate depression about as well as antidepressants in trials, but interacts with a huge number of medicines.

Quick answer: A Cochrane review found St John’s wort better than placebo and similar to standard antidepressants for mild to moderate depression. The catch is interactions: it speeds up the liver’s breakdown of many medicines, making the contraceptive pill, warfarin, some heart, HIV, epilepsy and transplant medicines fail. With SSRIs it can cause serotonin syndrome.

Brand names

  • Blackmores Hypericum, Herbs of Gold St John’s Wort, Remotiv (overseas)

How it works

Hyperforin raises serotonin and other brain messengers, and switches on liver enzymes (CYP3A4) and drug pumps that clear medicines.

Used for

  • Mild to moderate depression

What the evidence says

From systematic reviews and large trials where they exist. ✅ good evidence · 🟡 some or mixed evidence · ❌ doesn’t work.

  • ✅ Mild to moderate depressionSimilar to standard antidepressants in a Cochrane review.
  • ❌ Severe depressionNot proven; needs proper treatment.

Avoid or check first if

  • You take the pill, implant or other hormonal contraception
  • You take warfarin, digoxin, ciclosporin, HIV or epilepsy medicines
  • You take an antidepressant, triptan or tramadol
  • You have bipolar disorder (can trigger mania)

Side effects

Most people have no side effects at usual doses. Supplements can still interact with medicines: tell your doctor and pharmacist what you take.

Full side-effect list (from the product information)

How often, from the official product information. Bold = serious.

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Dry mouth, dizziness, upset stomach
  • Sun sensitivity

Rare 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000

  • Serotonin syndrome with SSRIs, SNRIs, triptans or tramadol
  • Contraceptive failure and unplanned pregnancy

Get help urgently if

  • Signs of an allergic reaction: swelling of the face or throat, trouble breathing (call 000)

Good to know

  • Always tell your doctor and pharmacist: this is the most interacting herbal medicine.
  • If depression is moderate or severe, or you’ve had thoughts of self-harm, see your GP.

Common questions

Does St John’s wort work for depression?

For mild to moderate depression, trials show it works about as well as standard antidepressants.

Can I take St John’s wort with the pill?

No. It can make the pill, implant and other hormonal contraception fail.
