Drug info

Coenzyme Q10 (CoQ10)

Supplement Complementary medicine (AUST L) or food

Often taken with statins for muscle aches, but trials mostly show no benefit there. There’s some evidence in heart failure and migraine.

Quick answer: Statins lower the body’s CoQ10, but trials of CoQ10 for statin muscle aches are mixed and mostly negative. One trial (Q-SYMBIO) suggested fewer heart events in heart failure. It may modestly reduce migraine frequency. It can weaken warfarin.

How it works

CoQ10 helps mitochondria make energy and acts as an antioxidant.

Used for

  • Heart failure
  • Migraine prevention

What the evidence says

From systematic reviews and large trials where they exist. ✅ good evidence · 🟡 some or mixed evidence · ❌ doesn’t work.

  • 🟡 Heart failureOne trial showed fewer heart events; not standard treatment yet.
  • 🟡 Migraine preventionSmall trials show fewer attacks.
  • ❌ Statin muscle achesMost trials show no benefit over placebo.
  • ❌ Energy and anti-ageingNo good evidence.

Avoid or check first if

  • You take warfarin (may reduce its effect)
  • You take blood pressure medicines (may lower BP a little)

Side effects

Most people have no side effects at usual doses. Supplements can still interact with medicines: tell your doctor and pharmacist what you take.

Good to know

  • If statins cause aches, talk to your doctor about a different statin or dose: that works better than CoQ10.
  • Ubiquinol and ubiquinone are both fine.

Common questions

Should I take CoQ10 with a statin?

Most trials show it doesn’t reduce statin muscle aches. Try a dose change or different statin with your doctor first.

Does CoQ10 help the heart?

One trial in heart failure showed benefit. It doesn’t replace standard heart medicines.
