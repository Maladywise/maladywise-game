Drug info

Alendronate and bisphosphonates

Bone-strengthening medicine Prescription only (S4)

The first-line osteoporosis treatment. Weekly tablets or a yearly drip that cut fracture risk. Take tablets exactly right.

Quick answer: Bisphosphonates slow bone breakdown, increasing bone density and reducing spine fractures by roughly half and hip fractures by about a third to 40%. Tablets must be taken upright with plain water on an empty stomach. Rare risks rise with long use, so many people have a ‘drug holiday’ after 3 to 5 years.

The weekly ritual that saves hips.

Brand names

  • Fosamax (alendronate), Fosamax Plus (with vitamin D), Actonel (risedronate), Aclasta (zoledronic acid)

How it works

They bind to bone and slow the osteoclasts that break bone down.

Used for

  • Osteoporosis in women and men
  • After a hip or spine fracture
  • Long-term steroid treatment
  • Paget’s disease of bone

Side effects

Most side effects are mild; the serious ones are rare.

Full side-effect list (from the product information)

How often, from the official product information. Bold = serious.

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Heartburn, indigestion (tablets)
  • Flu-like aches and fever for 1 to 3 days after the first infusion

Uncommon 1 in 100 to 1 in 1,000

  • Muscle, bone or joint aches

Rare 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000

  • Oesophageal ulcers (if taken incorrectly)
  • Osteonecrosis of the jaw: about 1 in 10,000 in osteoporosis doses
  • Atypical thigh-bone fracture: rises with use beyond 5 years

Get help urgently if

  • New thigh or groin pain
  • Painful swallowing or chest pain
  • Exposed bone in the mouth or jaw pain

Good to know

  • Take tablets first thing with a full glass of plain water, stay upright and don’t eat, drink or take other medicines for 30 minutes.
  • Make sure your vitamin D and calcium are adequate.
  • See your dentist for a check-up; tell them you take it.
  • Ask about a drug holiday after 5 years of tablets or 3 years of infusions.

Common questions

How do I take alendronate?

Once a week, first thing in the morning, with a full glass of plain water. Stay upright and wait at least 30 minutes before food, drink or other medicines.

What’s a bisphosphonate drug holiday?

Because bisphosphonates stay in bone, many people can stop after 3 to 5 years and keep much of the benefit, which lowers the rare long-term risks. Your doctor decides based on your fracture risk.

Do I need to stop alendronate before dental work?

Usually not for routine dental care. Tell your dentist; good dental health lowers jaw risks.
