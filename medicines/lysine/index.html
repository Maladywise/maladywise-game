Lysine for cold sores

Amino acid supplement Complementary medicine (AUST L) or food

Popular for cold sores. Trials are small and inconsistent; antiviral medicines work better.

Quick answer: Some older small trials suggested lysine reduces cold sore outbreaks; others found no benefit. If you get frequent cold sores, antiviral tablets (valaciclovir or famciclovir) are proven to prevent and shorten them.

How it works

Lysine may compete with arginine, which the herpes virus needs to multiply.

Used for

  • Preventing cold sores

What the evidence says

From systematic reviews and large trials where they exist. ✅ good evidence · 🟡 some or mixed evidence · ❌ doesn’t work.

  • 🟡 Preventing cold soresSmall, inconsistent trials.
  • ❌ Treating an outbreakNo good evidence.

Side effects

Most people have no side effects at usual doses. Supplements can still interact with medicines: tell your doctor and pharmacist what you take.

Common questions

Does lysine prevent cold sores?

Evidence is weak and inconsistent. Antiviral tablets are proven.

Is lysine safe?

Usually at normal doses. Avoid with kidney disease.
