Drug info

Minoxidil

Hair growth stimulant Topical: pharmacy (S2). Oral: prescription (S4)

The most used hair-loss treatment. Topical foam, or a low-dose tablet that’s become popular. Works only while you keep using it.

Quick answer: Minoxidil prolongs the growth phase of hair follicles. It treats male and female pattern hair loss. Low-dose oral minoxidil (an old blood pressure tablet, used off-label at tiny doses) is increasingly prescribed. Expect shedding in the first months, and results after 4 to 6 months.

At a glance

For
Male and female pattern hair loss; some other hair loss types
Works
Slows loss in most; regrowth in some
Cost
Topical about $30 to $60 a month; oral tablets often compounded or split, a few dollars a month
Kicks in
4 to 6 months; full effect at 12 months
Dose
Topical 5% foam or solution once or twice daily; oral often 0.25 to 2.5 mg daily (prescribed)
Watch for
Early shedding, unwanted facial or body hair, ankle swelling, palpitations (oral)

Keep it up, or lose the gains.

Brand names

  • Topical: Regaine, Hair-a-Gain, Hair Revive. Oral: Loniten (10 mg tablets, cut down for low doses) or compounded capsules

How it works

Minoxidil widens blood vessels and acts on follicles to extend the growth phase.

Used for

  • Male pattern hair loss
  • Female pattern hair loss
  • Off-label: other hair loss conditions

Side effects

Topical side effects are mostly local; oral ones are body-wide and dose-related.

Full side-effect list (from the product information)

How often, from the official product information. Bold = serious.

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Increased shedding in the first 2 to 8 weeks
  • Scalp itch or dryness (topical)
  • Unwanted facial or body hair (more with oral)

Uncommon 1 in 100 to 1 in 1,000

  • Ankle swelling
  • Fast heartbeat, light-headedness (oral)

Rare 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000

  • Chest pain, fluid around the heart (oral, higher doses)

Get help urgently if

  • Chest pain, breathlessness or marked swelling
  • Fainting or very fast heartbeat

Good to know

  • Results disappear within months of stopping.
  • Topical minoxidil is toxic to cats: avoid them licking treated skin or pillows.
  • Oral minoxidil isn’t suitable in pregnancy or with some heart conditions.
  • Combine with finasteride in men for a better result.

Common questions

How long does minoxidil take to work?

Usually 4 to 6 months, with the full effect at about a year. Early shedding is a sign it’s working.

Is oral minoxidil better than foam?

Low-dose oral minoxidil appears roughly as effective, and easier for many people, but has more body-wide side effects. It needs a prescription.

What happens if I stop minoxidil?

Hair gained or kept is usually lost over 3 to 6 months.
