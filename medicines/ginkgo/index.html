Drug info

Ginkgo biloba

Herbal medicine Complementary medicine (AUST L) or food

Sold for memory, but large trials found it doesn’t prevent dementia or improve memory in healthy people.

Quick answer: The large GEM trial (over 3,000 older adults, 6 years) found ginkgo didn’t prevent dementia or memory decline. It doesn’t help tinnitus either. It may increase bleeding risk, especially with blood thinners.

Brand names

  • Blackmores Ginkgo, Nature’s Way, Herbs of Gold Ginkgo, Tebonin

How it works

Ginkgo extracts affect blood flow and platelets.

Used for

  • Existing dementia

What the evidence says

From systematic reviews and large trials where they exist. ✅ good evidence · 🟡 some or mixed evidence · ❌ doesn’t work.

  • 🟡 Existing dementiaSome small trials suggest a small benefit; quality is low.
  • ❌ Preventing dementiaNo benefit in the GEM trial.
  • ❌ Memory in healthy peopleNo benefit.
  • ❌ TinnitusNo benefit in a Cochrane review.

Avoid or check first if

  • You take warfarin, aspirin, clopidogrel or other blood thinners
  • You have surgery coming up (stop 2 weeks before)
  • You have epilepsy (raw ginkgo seeds can cause seizures)

Side effects

Most people have no side effects at usual doses. Supplements can still interact with medicines: tell your doctor and pharmacist what you take.

Full side-effect list (from the product information)

How often, from the official product information. Bold = serious.

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Headache, upset stomach

Rare 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000

  • Bleeding, especially with blood thinners or before surgery

Get help urgently if

  • Signs of an allergic reaction: swelling of the face or throat, trouble breathing (call 000)

Good to know

  • Exercise, blood pressure control, hearing aids and staying social do more for brain health.
  • Never eat raw ginkgo seeds.

Common questions

Does ginkgo improve memory?

Not in healthy people, and it didn’t prevent dementia in a large 6-year trial.

Can ginkgo cause bleeding?

It may increase bleeding risk, especially with blood thinners. Stop before surgery.
