Drug info

Benzodiazepines

Sedative (benzodiazepine) Prescription only (S4; alprazolam S8)

Fast, calming and habit-forming. Useful for days to a couple of weeks; risky as a long-term habit.

Quick answer: Benzodiazepines quickly reduce anxiety, help sleep and stop seizures. Tolerance builds within weeks and dependence follows, so they’re for short-term use. They cause falls and memory problems in older people and are dangerous with opioids or alcohol.

At a glance

For
Short-term severe anxiety, short-term insomnia, alcohol withdrawal, seizures, muscle spasm, sedation for procedures
Works
Fast, but benefit fades with regular use
Cost
PBS: up to $25 a script ($7.70 concession)
Kicks in
30 to 60 minutes
Dose
Lowest dose for the shortest time, ideally 2 to 4 weeks maximum
Watch for
Drowsiness, unsteadiness, memory problems, dependence

Quick relief, slow exit.

Brand names

  • Valium (diazepam), Normison/Temaze (temazepam), Serepax/Alepam (oxazepam), Xanax/Kalma (alprazolam), Rivotril (clonazepam), Ativan (lorazepam)

How it works

They enhance GABA, the brain’s main calming chemical messenger.

Used for

  • Severe anxiety in a crisis (short term)
  • Short-term insomnia
  • Alcohol withdrawal (supervised)
  • Seizures and status epilepticus
  • Before medical procedures

Side effects

Effects are dose-related and add up with alcohol and other sedatives.

Full side-effect list (from the product information)

How often, from the official product information. Bold = serious.

Very common more than 1 in 10

  • Drowsiness

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Unsteadiness, falls (older people)
  • Memory and concentration problems
  • Tolerance and dependence after a few weeks

Uncommon 1 in 100 to 1 in 1,000

  • Paradoxical agitation
  • Low mood

Rare 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000

  • Breathing suppression, especially with opioids, alcohol or sleep apnoea
  • Withdrawal seizures after stopping high doses suddenly

Get help urgently if

  • Very drowsy, slow breathing or hard to wake: call 000
  • Seizure after stopping
  • Thoughts of self-harm

Good to know

  • Don’t drive or operate machinery if drowsy; it’s illegal to drive impaired.
  • Never mix with opioids or alcohol.
  • After more than a few weeks, taper slowly with your doctor.
  • Alprazolam is Schedule 8 in Australia: tighter rules apply.

Common questions

Are benzodiazepines addictive?

They cause dependence with regular use beyond a few weeks. Stopping suddenly can cause rebound anxiety, insomnia and, after high doses, seizures.

Is diazepam safe for sleep?

Only short term. For ongoing insomnia, CBT for insomnia works better and lasts.

Can I drink alcohol on benzodiazepines?

No. Together they increase sedation, falls, memory loss and breathing problems.
