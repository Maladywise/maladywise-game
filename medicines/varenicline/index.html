Drug info

Varenicline

Smoking cessation medicine Prescription only (S4)

The most effective quit-smoking tablet. Reduces cravings and makes cigarettes less satisfying.

Quick answer: Varenicline partially stimulates nicotine receptors to ease cravings and withdrawal, while blocking the reward from smoking. It roughly doubles to triples quit rates. Nausea is common. The large EAGLES trial found no significant increase in serious mental health problems compared with placebo.

The best tablet for the hardest habit.

Brand names

  • Apo-Varenicline, Varenicline generics (Champix brand was withdrawn in 2021)

How it works

It’s a partial agonist at alpha-4 beta-2 nicotine receptors in the brain.

Used for

  • Quitting smoking
  • Off-label: quitting vaping
  • Can be combined with nicotine replacement in some cases

Side effects

Taking it with food and a full glass of water reduces nausea.

Good to know

  • Combine with Quitline (13 78 48) support for the best chance.
  • Lower dose in severe kidney disease.
  • Caution driving until you know how it affects you.

Common questions

How effective is varenicline?

It roughly doubles long-term quit rates compared with placebo, and is more effective than single forms of nicotine replacement.

Does varenicline cause depression?

The large EAGLES trial found no significant increase in serious neuropsychiatric events compared with placebo, including in people with mental illness. Report any mood change.

Is Champix still available?

The Champix brand was withdrawn in 2021, but generic varenicline is available and PBS-listed.
