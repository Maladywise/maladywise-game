Drug info

Turmeric and curcumin

Herbal medicine Complementary medicine (AUST L) or food

May modestly ease osteoarthritis pain. The TGA has warned about rare liver injury from turmeric and curcumin products.

Quick answer: Turmeric’s active compound, curcumin, is poorly absorbed, so products add black pepper extract (piperine) or special formulations. Small trials suggest a modest effect on knee osteoarthritis pain. For most other claims (cancer, depression, ‘inflammation’), good human evidence is lacking. The TGA has reported rare but serious liver injury, especially with high-absorption products.

How it works

Curcumin dampens inflammatory signals in lab studies. Piperine boosts absorption but also changes how the liver handles some medicines.

Used for

  • Knee osteoarthritis pain

What the evidence says

From systematic reviews and large trials where they exist. ✅ good evidence · 🟡 some or mixed evidence · ❌ doesn’t work.

  • 🟡 Knee osteoarthritis painSmall trials show modest benefit, similar to low-dose anti-inflammatories in some.
  • ❌ Cancer prevention or treatmentNo good human evidence.
  • ❌ Weight lossNo good evidence.

Avoid or check first if

  • You take warfarin or other blood thinners
  • You have liver or gallbladder disease
  • You’re pregnant

Side effects

Most people have no side effects at usual doses. Supplements can still interact with medicines: tell your doctor and pharmacist what you take.

Full side-effect list (from the product information)

How often, from the official product information. Bold = serious.

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Upset stomach, reflux

Rare 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000

  • Liver injury (rare): yellow skin or eyes, dark urine, severe tiredness

Get help urgently if

  • Signs of an allergic reaction: swelling of the face or throat, trouble breathing (call 000)
  • Yellow skin or eyes, dark urine or severe tiredness (possible liver injury)

Good to know

  • Stop and see a doctor if you develop yellow eyes, dark urine or itch.
  • Tell your pharmacist: piperine changes how some medicines are absorbed.

Common questions

Does turmeric help arthritis?

It may modestly reduce knee osteoarthritis pain. The evidence comes from small trials.

Is turmeric safe for the liver?

Usually, but the TGA has received reports of liver injury from turmeric and curcumin products, some serious. Cooking amounts are fine.
