Drug info

Mirtazapine

Antidepressant (NaSSA) Prescription only

An antidepressant taken at night. Often causes drowsiness and a bigger appetite, which can help some people and be a problem for others.

Brand names

  • Avanza (incl. SolTab, which melts on the tongue), Remeron, plus generic brands

How it works

It blocks alpha-2 receptors, boosting noradrenaline and serotonin release. Strong histamine blocking causes sleepiness and a bigger appetite. Mood usually improves after 2 to 4 weeks.

Used for

  • Major depression (TGA-approved)
  • Low doses for sleep problems: off-label, and not a first choice because of weight gain and next-day drowsiness

Side effects

How often, from the official product information. Bold = serious.

Very common more than 1 in 10

  • Weight gain and increased appetite
  • Drowsiness or sedation
  • Headache
  • Dry mouth

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Odd dreams, confusion, anxiety
  • Poor sleep, sluggishness, tremor
  • Dizziness, memory problems
  • Nausea, diarrhoea, vomiting, constipation
  • Rash, joint, muscle or back pain
  • Swollen ankles, tiredness

Uncommon 1 in 100 to 1 in 1,000

  • Nightmares, agitation, restlessness
  • Hallucinations, mania
  • Pins and needles, restless legs
  • Fainting, low blood pressure
  • Numb mouth

Rare 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000

  • Aggression
  • Muscle jerks
  • Pancreatitis
  • Raised liver enzymes

Frequency not known reported, rate unclear

  • Bone marrow suppression
  • Low sodium, suicidal thoughts
  • Seizures, serotonin syndrome
  • Severe skin reactions
  • Muscle breakdown, sleepwalking
  • Breast swelling or milk discharge
  • Urine retention, prolonged erection

Get help urgently if

  • Fever, sore throat or mouth ulcers (a blood test is needed to check white cells)
  • Fever, shaking, muscle jerks, confusion or a racing heart (possible serotonin syndrome)
  • Thoughts of suicide or self-harm: Lifeline 13 11 14, or 000
  • Yellow skin or eyes, a seizure, or a blistering rash

Good to know

  • Take at bedtime. Driving: don’t drive until you know you’re not drowsy the next day.
  • Avoid alcohol and other sedating medicines. Weight gain is common.
  • Interacts with other serotonin medicines, some antibiotics and antifungals. Never combine with an MAOI.
  • Stopping: reduce slowly to avoid dizziness, nausea, anxiety and poor sleep.
  • Under 25s: suicidal thoughts can increase early on or after a dose change. Thoughts of self-harm: see your doctor now or call Lifeline 13 11 14.
  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding: discuss with your doctor.

Common questions

Does mirtazapine cause weight gain?

Yes. Weight gain and increased appetite are very common, affecting more than 1 in 10 people.

Is mirtazapine used for sleep?

Low doses are sometimes used for sleep problems, but this is off-label and not a first choice because of weight gain and next-day drowsiness.

When should I take mirtazapine?

At bedtime. Don’t drive until you know you’re not drowsy the next day.
