Drug info

Zolpidem and zopiclone (Z-drugs)

Z-drug hypnotic Prescription only (S4)

Fast-acting sleeping tablets for short-term use. Known for complex sleep behaviours like sleepwalking, eating and even driving while asleep.

Quick answer: Zolpidem and zopiclone help you fall asleep and are meant for up to 4 weeks. They carry a boxed warning about complex sleep behaviours. Next-morning impairment, falls in older people and dependence are the other risks. CBT for insomnia is the first-line long-term treatment.

Short term only, and in bed when you take it.

Brand names

  • Zolpidem: Stilnox, Stilnox CR, Dormizol. Zopiclone: Imovane, Imrest

How it works

Z-drugs act on the same GABA receptors as benzodiazepines, but more selectively.

Used for

  • Short-term insomnia (up to 4 weeks)
  • Occasional use, e.g. during a crisis or shift changes, under medical advice

Side effects

Most effects relate to sedation lasting too long or acting at the wrong time.

Full side-effect list (from the product information)

How often, from the official product information. Bold = serious.

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Next-day drowsiness
  • Headache
  • Dizziness
  • Bitter metallic taste (zopiclone)

Uncommon 1 in 100 to 1 in 1,000

  • Complex sleep behaviours: sleepwalking, sleep-eating, sleep-driving, with no memory
  • Memory lapses
  • Falls

Rare 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000

  • Hallucinations
  • Dependence and rebound insomnia

Get help urgently if

  • Any sleepwalking, sleep-driving or other complex behaviour: stop and tell your doctor
  • Breathing problems, especially with sleep apnoea

Good to know

  • Take it only when in bed with 7 to 8 hours available.
  • No alcohol or other sedatives.
  • Don’t drive the next morning if at all drowsy.
  • Ask about CBT for insomnia, which works better long term.

Common questions

Is Stilnox addictive?

It can cause dependence and rebound insomnia, especially with nightly use beyond a few weeks.

What are the side effects of zolpidem?

Drowsiness, dizziness and headache are common. Complex sleep behaviours like sleepwalking or sleep-driving are less common but serious.

What works better than sleeping pills?

CBT for insomnia (CBT-I) is the recommended first-line treatment and keeps working after it ends.
