Drug info

Psyllium (Metamucil) and fibre supplements

Fibre supplement Complementary medicine (AUST L) or food

Cheap, well studied and useful: helps constipation, IBS and cholesterol. Start low and drink plenty of water.

Quick answer: Psyllium is a soluble fibre that softens stools and bulks them up. It helps constipation, improves IBS symptoms (especially constipation-type), lowers LDL cholesterol by about 5 to 10%, and modestly improves blood sugar in type 2 diabetes. Start with a small dose to limit bloating.

Evidence first, marketing second.

Brand names

  • Metamucil, Nature’s Way psyllium husk, Benefiber (wheat dextrin, a different fibre)

How it works

Psyllium forms a gel that holds water in the stool and binds bile acids, which lowers cholesterol.

Used for

  • Constipation
  • IBS
  • LDL cholesterol
  • Blood sugar in type 2 diabetes

What the evidence says

From systematic reviews and large trials where they exist. ✅ good evidence · 🟡 some or mixed evidence · ❌ doesn’t work.

  • ✅ ConstipationSoftens and bulks stools.
  • ✅ IBSSoluble fibre improves symptoms; insoluble bran can worsen them.
  • ✅ LDL cholesterolAbout 10 g a day lowers LDL by 5 to 10%.
  • 🟡 Blood sugar in type 2 diabetesModest improvement.

Avoid or check first if

  • You have trouble swallowing or a bowel narrowing
  • You take other medicines: separate by 2 hours

Side effects

Most people have no side effects at usual doses. Supplements can still interact with medicines: tell your doctor and pharmacist what you take.

Full side-effect list (from the product information)

How often, from the official product information. Bold = serious.

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Bloating, wind (settles over 1 to 2 weeks)

Rare 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000

  • Choking or bowel blockage if taken without enough water or with swallowing problems

Get help urgently if

  • Signs of an allergic reaction: swelling of the face or throat, trouble breathing (call 000)

Good to know

  • Always mix with a full glass of water and drink more through the day.
  • Combine with diet changes for cholesterol.

Common questions

Does Metamucil lower cholesterol?

Yes, modestly. About 10 g of psyllium a day lowers LDL cholesterol by 5 to 10%.

Is psyllium good for IBS?

Yes, soluble fibre like psyllium helps IBS symptoms. Insoluble wheat bran can make them worse.
