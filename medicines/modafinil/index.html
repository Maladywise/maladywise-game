Drug info

Modafinil

Wakefulness-promoting agent Prescription only (S4)

A tablet that helps people stay awake. Used for excessive daytime sleepiness from narcolepsy, sleep apnoea or shift work.

Brand names

  • Modavigil, and generic modafinil brands

How it works

Exactly how modafinil works isn’t fully known. It raises dopamine in the brain by blocking its reuptake and boosts other wake-promoting signals. It causes less of a “high” than amphetamines.

Used for

  • Narcolepsy (TGA-approved)
  • Sleepiness in obstructive sleep apnoea despite CPAP, alongside CPAP (TGA-approved)
  • Moderate to severe shift work disorder, when other measures haven’t worked (TGA-approved)
  • Off-label (specialist use): e.g. fatigue in multiple sclerosis. Not approved for boosting study or work performance

Side effects

How often, from the official product information. Bold = serious.

Very common more than 1 in 10

  • Headache
  • Nausea

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Nervousness or anxiety
  • Trouble sleeping
  • Dizziness or sleepiness
  • Low mood
  • Pins and needles
  • Back pain
  • Runny nose, sore throat or flu-like symptoms
  • Diarrhoea, constipation, indigestion or dry mouth
  • Less appetite
  • Fast heartbeat, palpitations or flushing
  • Raised blood pressure
  • Chest pain
  • Abnormal liver tests

Rare 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000

  • Agitation, aggression, psychosis, mania or suicidal thoughts
  • Tremor or unusual movements
  • Rash, acne, eczema or itch

Very rare fewer than 1 in 10,000

  • Serious skin reactions (SJS, TEN, DRESS)
  • Anaphylaxis or angioedema
  • Multi-organ allergic reaction, seizures
  • Heart rhythm problems or reduced heart blood flow
  • Sweating, swelling

Get help urgently if

  • Any rash, especially with blistering, peeling, mouth sores or fever: stop and seek help the same day
  • Swelling of the face or throat, or trouble breathing. Call 000
  • Chest pain, a racing heartbeat, hallucinations or thoughts of self-harm

Good to know

  • Makes hormonal contraception less effective (pill, implant, injection, hormonal IUD). Use another method during treatment and for 2 months after stopping.
  • Not for use in pregnancy.
  • Interacts with some medicines (e.g. ciclosporin, warfarin). Check with your pharmacist.
  • Doesn’t replace sleep or CPAP. Don’t drive while sleepy.
  • Can be habit-forming in some people. Blood pressure is checked during treatment.

Common questions

What is modafinil approved for?

Narcolepsy, sleepiness in obstructive sleep apnoea despite CPAP, and moderate to severe shift work disorder. It’s not approved for boosting study or work performance.

Does modafinil affect contraception?

Yes. It makes hormonal contraception (pill, implant, injection, hormonal IUD) less effective. Use another method during treatment and for 2 months after stopping.

What are the side effects of modafinil?

Headache and nausea are very common. Any rash, especially with blistering, peeling, mouth sores or fever, needs you to stop and seek help the same day.
