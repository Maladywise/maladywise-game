Drug info

Modafinil

Wakefulness-promoting agent Prescription only (S4)

A tablet that keeps people awake when a sleep disorder won’t let them. Not a brain booster, and not a substitute for sleep.

Quick answer: Modafinil is a prescription tablet that reduces daytime sleepiness in narcolepsy, sleep apnoea despite CPAP, and shift work disorder. Its most common side effects are headache and nausea. It makes hormonal contraception less effective, and it’s not approved for boosting study or work performance. Any rash, especially with blistering or fever, means stop and seek help the same day.

Brand names

  • Modavigil, and generic modafinil brands

How it works

Exactly how modafinil works isn’t fully known. It raises dopamine by blocking its reuptake and boosts other wake-promoting signals, with less of a “high” than amphetamines.

Used for

  • Narcolepsy (TGA-approved)
  • Sleepiness in obstructive sleep apnoea despite CPAP, alongside CPAP (TGA-approved)
  • Moderate to severe shift work disorder, when other measures haven’t worked (TGA-approved)
  • Off-label (specialist use): e.g. fatigue in multiple sclerosis. Not approved for boosting study or work performance

Side effects

Most common: headache and nausea. Nervousness or anxiety, trouble sleeping, dizziness, a fast heartbeat, dry mouth, less appetite and tummy upsets are also common. Serious skin rashes are rare, so any rash needs checking the same day.

Get help urgently if

  • Any rash, especially with blistering, peeling, mouth sores or fever: stop and seek help the same day
  • Swelling of the face or throat, or trouble breathing. Call 000
  • Chest pain, a racing heartbeat, hallucinations or thoughts of self-harm

Good to know

  • Makes hormonal contraception less effective (pill, implant, injection, hormonal IUD). Use another method during treatment and for 2 months after stopping.
  • Not for use in pregnancy.
  • Interacts with some medicines (e.g. ciclosporin, warfarin). Check with your pharmacist.
  • Doesn’t replace sleep or CPAP. Don’t drive while sleepy. Can be habit-forming in some people.

Common questions

What is modafinil approved for?

Narcolepsy, sleepiness in obstructive sleep apnoea despite CPAP, and moderate to severe shift work disorder. It’s on the PBS for narcolepsy only. It’s not approved for boosting study or work performance.

Does modafinil affect contraception?

Yes. It makes hormonal contraception (pill, implant, injection, hormonal IUD) less effective. Use another method during treatment and for 2 months after stopping.

What are the side effects of modafinil?

Headache and nausea are very common. Nervousness or anxiety, trouble sleeping, dizziness, a fast heartbeat, dry mouth and less appetite are common. Any rash, especially with blistering, peeling, mouth sores or fever, needs you to stop and seek help the same day.
