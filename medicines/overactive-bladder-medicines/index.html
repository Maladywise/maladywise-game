Drug info

Overactive bladder medicines

Anticholinergics and beta-3 agonists Prescription only (S4)

For urgency, frequency and leaks from an overactive bladder, after bladder training. Mirabegron avoids the memory concerns of older anticholinergics.

Quick answer: These medicines calm bladder muscle contractions. Bladder training, pelvic floor exercises and fluid adjustments come first. Anticholinergics like oxybutynin cause dry mouth and constipation and may affect memory in older people; mirabegron works differently but can raise blood pressure.

At a glance

For
Overactive bladder: urgency, frequency, night-time urination, urge leaks
Works
Modest: about 1 to 2 fewer urgency episodes or leaks a day
Cost
PBS: up to $25 a script ($7.70 concession)
Kicks in
Within 2 weeks; full effect by 8 to 12 weeks
Dose
Oxybutynin 2.5 to 5 mg two to three times daily (or patch); solifenacin 5 to 10 mg daily; mirabegron 25 to 50 mg daily
Watch for
Dry mouth, constipation, confusion (anticholinergics); raised blood pressure (mirabegron)

Calms the bladder, not the cause.

Brand names

  • Oxybutynin: Ditropan, Oxytrol (patch). Solifenacin: Vesicare. Darifenacin: Enablex. Tolterodine: Detrusitol. Mirabegron: Betmiga. Vibegron: Beova

How it works

Anticholinergics block muscarinic receptors on bladder muscle; mirabegron and vibegron stimulate beta-3 receptors that relax it.

Used for

  • Overactive bladder
  • Urge incontinence
  • Night-time urination (with other measures)
  • Bladder spasms after surgery or with catheters

Side effects

Anticholinergic effects add up with other medicines that have them.

Good to know

  • Try bladder training and pelvic floor exercises first or alongside.
  • Older people and those at risk of dementia: discuss mirabegron or vibegron.
  • Check blood pressure on mirabegron.
  • Review after 3 months: stop if not helping.

Common questions

Does oxybutynin cause dementia?

Long-term use of strongly anticholinergic medicines has been linked with higher dementia risk in studies. It’s an association, but many doctors prefer alternatives in older people.

Which overactive bladder medicine is best?

They work about equally well. The choice depends on side effects: mirabegron and vibegron avoid dry mouth and memory concerns.

How long before bladder medicines work?

Some improvement within 2 weeks, with full benefit by 8 to 12 weeks.
