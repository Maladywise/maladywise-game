Drug info

Salbutamol (blue reliever puffer)

Short-acting reliever Pharmacy medicine (S3)

The blue puffer. Opens the airways within minutes. Brilliant in an attack, but needing it often is a sign your asthma needs a preventer.

Quick answer: Salbutamol relaxes the muscles around the airways, easing wheeze and tightness within a few minutes, for about 4 hours. It’s the standard reliever in asthma first aid. It doesn’t treat the underlying inflammation, so if you need it more than twice a week, see your doctor about a preventer.

Brand names

  • Ventolin, Asmol, Airomir, Zempreon

How it works

Salbutamol stimulates beta-2 receptors on airway muscle, which makes the muscle relax and the airways widen.

Used for

  • Asthma attacks and flares
  • Before exercise if exercise triggers symptoms
  • COPD flare-ups (with other medicines)
  • Asthma first aid: sit upright, 4 separate puffs via a spacer, wait 4 minutes, repeat; call 000 if no better

Side effects

Side effects are usually brief and harmless.

Full side-effect list (from the product information)

How often, from the official product information. Bold = serious.

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Shakiness (tremor)
  • Fast heartbeat or palpitations
  • Headache

Uncommon 1 in 100 to 1 in 1,000

  • Muscle cramps
  • Feeling anxious or restless

Rare 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000

  • Low potassium with very high doses
  • Worsening wheeze straight after a puff (paradoxical bronchospasm)

Get help urgently if

  • Reliever isn’t helping or lasting under 3 hours: urgent help
  • Can’t speak in sentences, lips blue, drowsy: call 000
  • Needing it more than twice a week

Good to know

  • A spacer gets much more medicine into the lungs than a puffer alone, for adults as well as children.
  • If you use it more than twice a week (excluding before exercise), your asthma isn’t well controlled: see your doctor.
  • Keep one with you, and check the expiry date.

Common questions

How many puffs of Ventolin can I take?

Usually 2 to 4 puffs when needed. In an asthma attack, follow asthma first aid: 4 puffs via a spacer, repeat every 4 minutes, and call 000 if not improving.

Is it bad to use salbutamol every day?

Needing it most days means the airways are inflamed. Relying on a reliever alone is linked to worse attacks; a preventer (or an anti-inflammatory reliever) is safer.

Why do I feel shaky after Ventolin?

Salbutamol stimulates muscles and the heart as well as the airways. Shakiness and a fast pulse are common and settle within an hour.
