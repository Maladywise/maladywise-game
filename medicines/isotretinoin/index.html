Drug info

Isotretinoin

Oral retinoid Prescription only (S4), specialist-initiated

The most effective acne treatment, often clearing it for good. Strict pregnancy prevention is essential.

Quick answer: Isotretinoin shrinks oil glands and treats severe or scarring acne that hasn’t responded to other treatments. A course usually runs 4 to 8 months, and many people stay clear afterwards. It causes severe birth defects, so pregnancy must be avoided during treatment and for a month after.

Life-changing for acne, with rules to match.

Brand names

  • Roaccutane, Oratane, Rexet

How it works

It shrinks sebaceous glands, reduces oil, normalises skin shedding in pores and reduces acne bacteria and inflammation.

Used for

  • Severe nodular or scarring acne
  • Acne that hasn’t responded to antibiotics and topical treatment
  • Some rosacea and other skin conditions

Side effects

Dryness is nearly universal and manageable.

Full side-effect list (from the product information)

How often, from the official product information. Bold = serious.

Very common more than 1 in 10

  • Dry, cracked lips
  • Dry skin and eyes
  • Nosebleeds

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Muscle and joint aches
  • Raised cholesterol or liver tests
  • Sun sensitivity

Uncommon 1 in 100 to 1 in 1,000

  • Mood changes, depression

Rare 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000

  • Raised pressure in the head (severe headache, vision changes), especially with tetracyclines
  • Severe skin reactions

Get help urgently if

  • Low mood, anxiety or thoughts of self-harm: stop and get help
  • Severe headache with vision changes
  • Pregnancy, or a missed period

Good to know

  • Use effective contraception for 1 month before, during and 1 month after treatment, with pregnancy tests.
  • Don’t combine with doxycycline or other tetracyclines.
  • Use lip balm and moisturiser constantly; avoid waxing and laser.
  • Don’t donate blood during treatment.

Common questions

Does Roaccutane cause depression?

Mood changes have been reported, though large studies give mixed results. Any mood change should be reported, and treatment stopped if needed.

How long is a Roaccutane course?

Usually 4 to 8 months, depending on dose and response.

Can a GP prescribe isotretinoin?

In Australia it’s usually started by a dermatologist. Rules differ between states.
