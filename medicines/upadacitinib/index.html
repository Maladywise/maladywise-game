Drug info

Upadacitinib and JAK inhibitors

JAK inhibitor (small molecule) Prescription only (S4), specialist

A once-daily tablet that works fast for moderate to severe Crohn’s and colitis, with specific risks that need monitoring.

Quick answer: JAK inhibitors block signalling inside immune cells. Upadacitinib works quickly, often within weeks, in Crohn’s disease and ulcerative colitis, including after biologics fail. Risks include shingles, raised cholesterol, blood clots and, in older people with heart risks, cardiovascular events. It must be avoided in pregnancy.

Brand names

  • Upadacitinib: Rinvoq. Tofacitinib: Xeljanz (colitis). Filgotinib: Jyseleca (colitis)

How it works

They inhibit Janus kinase enzymes that relay cytokine signals inside immune cells.

Used for

  • Crohn’s disease
  • Ulcerative colitis
  • Also rheumatoid arthritis, eczema and other inflammatory conditions

Side effects

Monitoring blood counts, liver tests and cholesterol keeps it safe.

Full side-effect list (from the product information)

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Acne
  • Colds and upper respiratory infections
  • Raised cholesterol and CK
  • Headache

Uncommon 1 in 100 to 1 in 1,000

  • Shingles
  • Low blood counts

Rare 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000

  • Blood clots
  • Heart attack and stroke (higher in older smokers with heart risk)
  • Serious infections, cancers

Get help urgently if

  • Calf pain, chest pain or sudden breathlessness
  • Painful blistering rash
  • Fever or signs of serious infection

Good to know

  • Get the non-live shingles vaccine (Shingrix) before starting if possible.
  • Use effective contraception: avoid in pregnancy and for 4 weeks after stopping.
  • Blood tests including cholesterol at baseline and during treatment.

Common questions

How fast does upadacitinib work?

Often within days to a few weeks, faster than most biologics.

Is upadacitinib safe in pregnancy?

No. It should be avoided in pregnancy, with contraception during and for 4 weeks after treatment.

Why does upadacitinib cause shingles?

JAK inhibitors weaken antiviral defences. The shingles vaccine before starting reduces the risk.
