Drug info

Fish oil and krill oil (omega-3)

Omega-3 supplement Complementary medicine (AUST L) or food

Australia’s favourite supplement. Great for very high triglycerides at high doses; for most other claims, the big trials disappoint.

Quick answer: Fish oil lowers high triglycerides at 2 to 4 g a day of EPA and DHA. In large trials in healthy people it didn’t prevent heart attacks, dementia or dry eye. Eating oily fish twice a week is still worthwhile. Krill oil has no proven advantage over fish oil.

Evidence first, marketing second.

Brand names

  • Blackmores, Nature’s Way, Swisse, Ethical Nutrients, Nature’s Own; krill: Blackmores Krill, Swisse Krill

How it works

EPA and DHA are omega-3 fats built into cell membranes. At higher doses they lower the liver’s production of triglycerides.

Used for

  • High triglycerides
  • Rheumatoid arthritis
  • Heart disease, high-risk people

What the evidence says

From systematic reviews and large trials where they exist. ✅ good evidence · 🟡 some or mixed evidence · ❌ doesn’t work.

  • ✅ High triglycerides2 to 4 g a day of EPA plus DHA lowers triglycerides by 20 to 30%.
  • 🟡 Rheumatoid arthritisMay modestly reduce joint stiffness and pain at high doses.
  • 🟡 Heart disease, high-risk peopleA prescription form (icosapent ethyl) cut heart events in one big trial; ordinary fish oil hasn’t matched it.
  • ❌ Preventing heart attacks in healthy peopleLarge trials (VITAL, ASCEND) found no benefit.
  • ❌ Dry eyeThe DREAM trial found no better than placebo.
  • ❌ Memory and dementiaNo benefit in trials.
  • ❌ DepressionMixed, mostly small or no effect.

Avoid or check first if

  • You take warfarin or other blood thinners (check with your doctor)
  • You’re allergic to fish or shellfish (krill)

Side effects

Most people have no side effects at usual doses. Supplements can still interact with medicines: tell your doctor and pharmacist what you take.

Full side-effect list (from the product information)

How often, from the official product information. Bold = serious.

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Fishy burps or aftertaste
  • Loose stools, indigestion

Rare 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000

  • Atrial fibrillation risk rises slightly at doses of 1 g a day or more

Get help urgently if

  • Signs of an allergic reaction: swelling of the face or throat, trouble breathing (call 000)

Good to know

  • Freezing capsules or taking them with meals reduces fishy burps.
  • Two serves of oily fish a week (salmon, sardines, mackerel) gives a similar amount and more nutrients.
  • Concentrated (‘high strength’) capsules mean fewer pills.

Common questions

Does fish oil prevent heart attacks?

Not in healthy people taking usual doses. Large trials found no reduction in heart attacks or strokes.

Is krill oil better than fish oil?

It contains less omega-3 per capsule and costs more. There’s no good evidence it works better.
