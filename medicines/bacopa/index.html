Drug info

Bacopa (brahmi)

Herbal medicine Complementary medicine (AUST L) or food

Small trials suggest a modest memory boost after about 12 weeks. Gut side effects are common.

Quick answer: Several small trials of Bacopa monnieri show modest improvements in memory recall and attention after 8 to 12 weeks, in healthy adults and older people. Studies are small, often industry-funded, and effects are modest. Nausea, cramps and diarrhoea are common.

How it works

An Ayurvedic herb thought to affect brain messengers and protect nerve cells.

Used for

  • Memory and attention

What the evidence says

From systematic reviews and large trials where they exist. ✅ good evidence · 🟡 some or mixed evidence · ❌ doesn’t work.

  • 🟡 Memory and attentionSmall improvement after 8 to 12 weeks in small trials.
  • ❌ Treating dementiaNo good evidence.

Avoid or check first if

  • You take thyroid medicines, sedatives or medicines that slow the heart
  • You’re pregnant or breastfeeding

Side effects

Most people have no side effects at usual doses. Supplements can still interact with medicines: tell your doctor and pharmacist what you take.

Full side-effect list (from the product information)

How often, from the official product information. Bold = serious.

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Nausea, stomach cramps, diarrhoea

Rare 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000

  • Slow heart rate (rare)

Get help urgently if

  • Signs of an allergic reaction: swelling of the face or throat, trouble breathing (call 000)

Good to know

  • Take with food to reduce gut upset.
  • Sleep, exercise and managing blood pressure matter more for memory.

Common questions

Does bacopa improve memory?

Small trials suggest a modest effect after about 12 weeks.

What are the side effects of bacopa?

Mostly gut upset: nausea, cramps and diarrhoea. Take it with food.
