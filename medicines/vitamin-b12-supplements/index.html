Vitamin B12 supplements

Vitamin supplement Complementary medicine (AUST L) or food

Essential for vegans and people with low B12. High-dose tablets (1,000 mcg) work for most people, even many with absorption problems.

Quick answer: Vitamin B12 is needed for nerves and red blood cells. Deficiency is common with vegan diets, long-term metformin or acid-suppressing medicines, older age and pernicious anaemia. High-dose oral tablets (1,000 to 2,000 mcg daily) correct most deficiencies; injections are used when levels are very low or there are nerve symptoms. Extra B12 won’t boost energy if your level is normal.

How it works

B12 is absorbed in the lower small bowel with the help of intrinsic factor from the stomach. At high oral doses, about 1% is absorbed passively without it.

Used for

  • B12 deficiency
  • Vegans and vegetarians
  • Pernicious anaemia

What the evidence says

From systematic reviews and large trials where they exist. ✅ good evidence · 🟡 some or mixed evidence · ❌ doesn’t work.

  • ✅ B12 deficiencyTablets of 1,000 mcg daily correct most deficiencies.
  • ✅ Vegans and vegetariansPlant foods don’t contain reliable B12.
  • 🟡 Pernicious anaemiaHigh-dose tablets work for many; injections are often preferred at first.
  • ❌ Energy boost with normal levelsNo benefit.
  • ❌ Preventing dementiaNo benefit in people who aren’t deficient.

Avoid or check first if

  • You have numbness, tingling or walking problems (needs prompt treatment, often injections)

Side effects

Most people have no side effects at usual doses. Supplements can still interact with medicines: tell your doctor and pharmacist what you take.

Good to know

  • Metformin and long-term PPIs (omeprazole, pantoprazole) can lower B12: ask about checking it.
  • Folic acid can hide B12-deficiency anaemia while nerve damage continues, so test B12 too.

Common questions

Are B12 tablets as good as injections?

For most people, high-dose tablets (1,000 mcg or more daily) raise B12 as well as injections. Injections are used for severe deficiency or nerve symptoms.

Will B12 give me more energy?

Only if you’re deficient.
