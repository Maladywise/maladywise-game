Drug info

Milk thistle (silymarin)

Herbal medicine Complementary medicine (AUST L) or food

Sold as a liver ‘detox’. Trials in liver disease show no clear benefit. Its one real hospital use is IV silibinin for death cap poisoning.

Quick answer: Cochrane reviews found milk thistle doesn’t reduce deaths or complications in alcohol-related or viral liver disease. Evidence for fatty liver is small and early. The liver doesn’t need a ‘detox’. In hospitals, an IV form (silibinin) is sometimes used for death cap mushroom poisoning.

How it works

Silymarin is an antioxidant that may protect liver cells in lab studies.

Used for

  • Fatty liver

What the evidence says

From systematic reviews and large trials where they exist. ✅ good evidence · 🟡 some or mixed evidence · ❌ doesn’t work.

  • 🟡 Fatty liverSmall early trials suggest lower liver enzymes.
  • ❌ Alcohol-related liver diseaseNo benefit in Cochrane review.
  • ❌ Hepatitis B or CNo benefit.
  • ❌ Hangovers and ‘detox’No evidence.

Avoid or check first if

  • You’re allergic to daisies, ragweed or chrysanthemums
  • You take diabetes medicines (may lower sugars)

Side effects

Most people have no side effects at usual doses. Supplements can still interact with medicines: tell your doctor and pharmacist what you take.

Get help urgently if

  • Signs of an allergic reaction: swelling of the face or throat, trouble breathing (call 000)

Good to know

  • Weight loss and cutting alcohol are what actually heal fatty liver.
  • Suspected mushroom poisoning: call Poisons 13 11 26 or 000.

Common questions

Does milk thistle detox the liver?

No. Your liver doesn’t need a detox, and trials in liver disease show no clear benefit.

Is milk thistle ever used in hospital?

Yes. An IV form, silibinin, is sometimes used for death cap mushroom poisoning.
