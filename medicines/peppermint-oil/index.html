Drug info

Peppermint oil capsules

Herbal medicine Complementary medicine (AUST L) or food

One of the better-proven options for IBS: enteric-coated capsules ease tummy pain and bloating.

Quick answer: Meta-analyses show enteric-coated peppermint oil improves overall IBS symptoms and tummy pain compared with placebo, and guidelines (including the American College of Gastroenterology) recommend it. The main side effect is heartburn.

Evidence first, marketing second.

Brand names

  • Mintec, Buscopan Peppermint (some markets), Nature’s Way; choose enteric-coated capsules

How it works

Menthol relaxes the smooth muscle of the bowel, easing spasm.

Used for

  • IBS pain and bloating
  • Indigestion (with caraway)

What the evidence says

From systematic reviews and large trials where they exist. ✅ good evidence · 🟡 some or mixed evidence · ❌ doesn’t work.

  • ✅ IBS pain and bloatingBetter than placebo in meta-analyses; guideline-recommended.
  • 🟡 Indigestion (with caraway)Some evidence.

Avoid or check first if

  • You have reflux or a hiatus hernia
  • You take antacids or acid-suppressing medicines at the same time (can dissolve the coating early)

Side effects

Most people have no side effects at usual doses. Supplements can still interact with medicines: tell your doctor and pharmacist what you take.

Full side-effect list (from the product information)

How often, from the official product information. Bold = serious.

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Heartburn
  • Minty burps

Get help urgently if

  • Signs of an allergic reaction: swelling of the face or throat, trouble breathing (call 000)

Good to know

  • Swallow whole: chewing releases the oil too early and causes heartburn.
  • Try it for 4 weeks alongside diet changes (like a low FODMAP diet with a dietitian).

Common questions

Does peppermint oil help IBS?

Yes. Enteric-coated capsules improve pain and overall symptoms in trials.

Why does peppermint oil give me heartburn?

It relaxes the valve at the top of the stomach. Enteric-coated capsules swallowed whole reduce this.
