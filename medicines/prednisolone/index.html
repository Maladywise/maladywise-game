Drug info

Prednisolone and prednisone

Oral corticosteroid Prescription only (S4)

Powerful anti-inflammatory tablets. Short courses are usually safe; long-term use needs care, tapering and bone protection.

Quick answer: Prednisolone (and prednisone, which the liver converts to prednisolone) calms inflammation fast. Short bursts treat asthma attacks, flares of arthritis, eczema or colitis. Longer use raises many risks, and after more than about 3 weeks, the body’s own steroid production switches off, so the dose must be tapered.

Brand names

  • Panafcortelone, Solone, Predmix (liquid), Redipred (liquid); prednisone: Sone, Panafcort

How it works

Corticosteroids switch off many inflammatory genes and immune cells.

Used for

  • Asthma and COPD flare-ups
  • Polymyalgia rheumatica and giant cell arteritis
  • Inflammatory arthritis, lupus, vasculitis flares
  • Inflammatory bowel disease flares
  • Severe skin conditions, allergic reactions, croup (as a liquid)

Side effects

Short courses mainly cause temporary effects; long-term effects build with dose and time.

Full side-effect list (from the product information)

How often, from the official product information. Bold = serious.

Very common more than 1 in 10

  • Increased appetite
  • Trouble sleeping

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Mood changes (high or low)
  • High blood sugar
  • Fluid retention
  • Indigestion

Uncommon 1 in 100 to 1 in 1,000

  • Bone loss (long term)
  • Weight gain, rounded face
  • Thin skin, bruising
  • Cataracts, glaucoma
  • Infections

Rare 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000

  • Steroid psychosis
  • Avascular necrosis of the hip

Get help urgently if

  • Severe mood change, confusion or hallucinations
  • Fever or infection while on long-term steroids
  • Black stools or vomiting blood
  • Feeling faint, vomiting or very unwell after stopping a long course

Good to know

  • Take in the morning with food.
  • Don’t stop suddenly after more than about 3 weeks: taper as directed.
  • On long-term steroids, ask about bone protection, blood sugar checks and vaccines.
  • Carry a steroid card if on long-term treatment.

Common questions

Do I need to taper prednisolone?

Not usually after a short course of under about 3 weeks. After longer use, taper slowly as your doctor advises, because your adrenal glands need time to restart.

Why can’t I sleep on prednisolone?

Steroids are stimulating. Take the dose in the morning to reduce this.

What’s the difference between prednisone and prednisolone?

Prednisone is converted to prednisolone in the liver. Doses are equivalent milligram for milligram.
