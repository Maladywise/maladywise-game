Drug info

Allopurinol

Urate-lowering therapy Prescription only (S4)

The long-term fix for gout: lowers uric acid so crystals dissolve and attacks stop. Start low, increase until the urate target is reached, and keep taking it.

Quick answer: Allopurinol reduces uric acid production. Over months, it dissolves urate crystals and prevents attacks and joint damage. Gout often flares in the first months, so a low-dose colchicine or anti-inflammatory is usually taken alongside. A serious rash is rare but must be reported straight away.

At a glance

For
Recurrent gout, tophi, gout with kidney stones or kidney disease
Works
Most people reach target and become attack-free with enough dose
Cost
PBS: up to $25 a script ($7.70 concession)
Kicks in
Urate falls in weeks; fewer attacks after 6 to 12 months
Dose
Start 50 to 100 mg daily, increase every 2 to 4 weeks until urate is below 0.36 mmol/L
Watch for
Rash, early gout flares

Treat to target, not to taste.

Brand names

  • Zyloprim, Progout, Allosig

How it works

It blocks xanthine oxidase, the enzyme that makes uric acid.

Used for

  • Recurrent gout attacks (2 or more a year)
  • Tophi or gout damage on X-ray
  • Gout with kidney disease or stones
  • Preventing tumour lysis syndrome in chemotherapy

Side effects

Most people take it for years without problems.

Full side-effect list (from the product information)

How often, from the official product information. Bold = serious.

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Gout flares in the first months
  • Rash (mild)

Uncommon 1 in 100 to 1 in 1,000

  • Tummy upset
  • Abnormal liver tests

Rare 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000

  • Severe skin reactions (SJS/TEN, DRESS), usually in the first 2 to 3 months; higher risk with HLA-B*5801 (more common in Han Chinese, Thai and Korean people) and kidney disease

Get help urgently if

  • Any rash, especially with fever, mouth ulcers or peeling: stop and see a doctor today
  • Yellow skin or eyes

Good to know

  • Don’t stop during a gout attack: keep taking it and treat the attack.
  • Ask whether you need the HLA-B*5801 test if you have Han Chinese, Thai or Korean ancestry.
  • Get urate checked every few weeks while the dose is adjusted, then yearly.
  • Avoid azathioprine and mercaptopurine interactions: tell your doctors.

Common questions

Should I stop allopurinol during a gout flare?

No. Keep taking it and treat the flare separately. Stopping and starting causes more flares.

What uric acid level should I aim for?

Below 0.36 mmol/L, or below 0.30 mmol/L if you have tophi.

Why did my gout get worse when I started allopurinol?

Dissolving crystals can trigger flares in the first months. Low-dose colchicine or an anti-inflammatory alongside it helps prevent this.
