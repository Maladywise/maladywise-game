Drug info

Budesonide for IBD

Gut-targeted corticosteroid Prescription only (S4)

A steroid released in the bowel and largely broken down by the liver, so it calms ileal Crohn’s flares with fewer body-wide side effects than prednisolone.

Quick answer: Oral budesonide treats mild to moderate Crohn’s disease affecting the end of the small bowel and right colon (Entocort), and some ulcerative colitis (Cortiment). Most is inactivated by the liver on first pass, so steroid side effects are fewer. It’s for flares, not long-term maintenance.

Prednisolone’s gentler cousin.

Brand names

  • Entocort (Crohn’s), Cortiment (ulcerative colitis), Budenofalk

How it works

Released in the ileum and colon, it acts on the gut lining, then the liver breaks down about 90% before it reaches the body.

Used for

  • Ileal and ileocaecal Crohn’s disease flares
  • Mild to moderate ulcerative colitis (Cortiment)
  • Microscopic colitis (very effective)

Side effects

Steroid side effects are less common than with prednisolone.

Full side-effect list (from the product information)

How often, from the official product information. Bold = serious.

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Headache
  • Acne, rounded face (less than prednisolone)
  • Mood or sleep changes

Uncommon 1 in 100 to 1 in 1,000

  • High blood sugar
  • Bone loss with repeated courses

Rare 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000

  • Adrenal suppression after long use

Get help urgently if

  • Severe mood changes
  • Signs of infection
  • Worsening bleeding or pain

Good to know

  • Swallow capsules whole in the morning.
  • Avoid grapefruit (raises levels).
  • It’s for flares: ask about a long-term maintenance plan if you need repeated courses.

Common questions

Is budesonide better than prednisolone for Crohn’s?

It has fewer side effects but is slightly less powerful, and only works for disease in the ileum and right colon.

Can I stay on budesonide long term?

It isn’t recommended for long-term maintenance in Crohn’s. Repeated courses mean a steroid-sparing treatment is needed.

Why avoid grapefruit with budesonide?

Grapefruit blocks the liver enzyme that breaks budesonide down, increasing side effects.
