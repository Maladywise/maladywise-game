Drug info

Bupropion

Smoking cessation aid; off-label antidepressant Prescription only (S4)

A quit-smoking tablet that’s an antidepressant almost everywhere else. Energising rather than sedating, and kinder to libido and weight than most.

Quick answer: In Australia, bupropion (Zyban) is approved and on the PBS for quitting smoking. Overseas it’s a common antidepressant, and doctors here sometimes use it off label for depression, low energy, ADHD and to counter sexual side effects from SSRIs. It doesn’t usually cause weight gain or sexual problems. The main risk is seizures, so it’s avoided in epilepsy, eating disorders and heavy alcohol withdrawal.

The antidepressant that doesn’t flatten your libido or add kilos. The catch: seizure risk, so it’s not for everyone.

Brand names

  • Zyban (sustained-release). Overseas brands include Wellbutrin

How it works

Bupropion blocks the reuptake of dopamine and noradrenaline, which lifts energy and motivation. It also blocks nicotine receptors, which takes the edge off cravings and withdrawal.

Used for

  • Quitting smoking, with support (TGA-approved)
  • Off label, depression: approved overseas; useful when tiredness, low motivation, weight gain or sexual side effects are a problem
  • Off label, SSRI sexual side effects: sometimes added to help
  • Off label, ADHD: modest benefit in trials, less than stimulants
  • Off label, weight: with naltrexone as Contrave (not PBS listed)

Side effects

Trouble sleeping is the most common side effect; take the second dose by mid-afternoon. Dry mouth, headache, nausea and feeling anxious or shaky are also common. Seizures are uncommon to rare, more likely at higher doses or with risk factors.

Good to know

  • Not for people with epilepsy or seizures, eating disorders (anorexia or bulimia), or who are suddenly stopping alcohol or sedatives.
  • Interactions: it strongly blocks the liver enzyme CYP2D6, raising levels of some antidepressants, antipsychotics, beta blockers (e.g. metoprolol) and atomoxetine. It can make tamoxifen and codeine less effective.
  • Keep alcohol low; it raises seizure risk.
  • Don’t take two doses close together, and don’t crush or chew the tablets.

Common questions

Is bupropion approved for depression in Australia?

No. In Australia it’s approved (as Zyban) for quitting smoking. It’s an approved antidepressant in many other countries, and doctors here sometimes prescribe it off label for depression.

Does bupropion cause weight gain or sexual side effects?

Usually not. It’s one of the few antidepressant-type medicines that tends not to cause weight gain or sexual problems, and it’s sometimes added to help SSRI sexual side effects.

Who shouldn’t take bupropion?

People with epilepsy or past seizures, eating disorders, or who are suddenly stopping alcohol or sedatives, because it raises seizure risk. It also interacts with several medicines through CYP2D6.
