Drug info

Collagen peptides

Peptide supplement Complementary medicine (AUST L) or food

The most popular peptide supplement. Industry-funded trials look good; independent, high-quality trials show no benefit for skin.

Quick answer: A 2025 meta-analysis of 23 trials found collagen improved skin hydration and wrinkles only in industry-funded or low-quality studies; independent and high-quality trials showed no effect. Evidence for joint pain and bone density is small and early. Collagen is safe, but it’s an incomplete protein: food protein does the same job for less.

How it works

Collagen is digested into small peptides and amino acids. Some peptides (like hydroxyprolyl-glycine) reach the blood, but whether they rebuild skin or cartilage isn’t proven.

Used for

  • Joint pain in athletes and osteoarthritis
  • Bone density after menopause

What the evidence says

From systematic reviews and large trials where they exist. ✅ good evidence · 🟡 some or mixed evidence · ❌ doesn’t work.

  • 🟡 Joint pain in athletes and osteoarthritisSmall trials suggest modest benefit.
  • 🟡 Bone density after menopauseOne industry-funded trial showed a small gain.
  • ❌ Skin ageing and wrinklesNo effect in independent, high-quality trials (Am J Med 2025).
  • ❌ Hair and nail growthLittle evidence.

Avoid or check first if

  • You have a fish or shellfish allergy (marine collagen) or follow a vegetarian diet (all collagen is animal-derived)
  • You have kidney disease and need to limit protein

Side effects

Most people have no side effects at usual doses. Supplements can still interact with medicines: tell your doctor and pharmacist what you take.

Good to know

  • Sunscreen, not smoking and retinoid creams are proven for skin ageing.
  • Collagen lacks tryptophan, so it doesn’t count as a complete protein.

Common questions

Does collagen work for skin?

Independent, high-quality trials show no benefit. Positive results come mainly from industry-funded studies.

Is collagen safe?

Yes for most people. It’s a food protein. Check for fish allergy with marine collagen.
