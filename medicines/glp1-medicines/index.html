Drug info

GLP-1 medicines

Diabetes and weight medicine Prescription only

Injections (and now tablets) that copy gut hormones controlling blood sugar and appetite. Same family, very different strengths. Social media calls them miracle drugs; the trials call them very effective, with fine print.

At a glance

For
Type 2 diabetes; weight management; some also lower heart attack and stroke risk or treat sleep apnoea
Works
Weight: from about 8% of body weight (liraglutide) to about 15% (semaglutide) and 15–21% (tirzepatide) over 1 to 1.5 years, versus 2–3% on a dummy treatment. Diabetes: HbA1c (3-month sugar average) down by roughly 1 to 2 points
Cost
PBS for type 2 diabetes only: Ozempic and Trulicity (criteria apply). None are on the PBS for weight loss, and Mounjaro isn’t on the PBS for anything (October 2026)
Kicks in
Appetite drops in the first weeks; doses stepped up about every 4 weeks; full effect over 1 to 1.5 years
Taken for
Long term: weight usually returns after stopping
Watch for
Nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea or constipation, reflux, gallstones

Not a miracle and not cheating: it’s a hormone. It turns down the food noise. Protein, sleep and moving are still your job.

Brand names

  • Semaglutide: Ozempic (weekly injection, diabetes), Wegovy (weekly injection, weight), Rybelsus (daily tablet, diabetes). Semaglutide guide
  • Tirzepatide: Mounjaro (weekly injection). Tirzepatide guide
  • Liraglutide: Victoza (daily injection, diabetes); generic liraglutide for weight (the Saxenda brand left Australia in December 2025)
  • Dulaglutide: Trulicity (weekly injection, diabetes)
  • Exenatide: Byetta (stopped in Australia in 2022) and Bydureon (stopped in the US in 2024)

How it works

GLP-1 is a hormone your gut releases after eating. These medicines copy it and last much longer: they boost insulin when blood sugar is high, slow stomach emptying and tell the brain you’re full. Tirzepatide also copies a second gut hormone, GIP.

Used for

  • Type 2 diabetes, alongside diet and exercise (all of them)
  • Weight management in adults with obesity, or overweight with a weight-related condition (Wegovy, Mounjaro, liraglutide 3 mg)
  • Lowering heart attack and stroke risk in people with heart disease and excess weight (Wegovy)
  • Moderate to severe sleep apnoea with obesity (Mounjaro)
  • Fatty liver disease with scarring (Wegovy; provisional approval in 2026)

Side effects

Gut side effects are the main ones, worst when starting and after each dose step, and usually settle. Eating smaller meals, stopping when full and going easy on fatty food helps.

Full side-effect list (from the product information)

How often, from the semaglutide (Ozempic) product information. Other GLP-1 medicines are similar; tirzepatide and liraglutide cause slightly more gut upset at weight-loss doses. Bold = serious.

Very common more than 1 in 10

  • Nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea or constipation
  • Tummy pain
  • Headache
  • Tiredness
  • Low blood sugar, if also on insulin or a sulfonylurea

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Dizziness
  • Indigestion, reflux, burping, wind or bloating
  • Gastritis
  • Gallstones
  • Hair thinning
  • Injection-site reactions
  • Low blood pressure
  • Low blood sugar with other diabetes medicines
  • Worsening of diabetic eye disease, if already present

Uncommon 1 in 100 to 1 in 1,000

  • Pancreatitis (inflamed pancreas)
  • Faster heart rate
  • Dizziness on standing
  • Altered taste
  • Raised pancreatic enzymes on blood tests

Rare 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000

  • Anaphylaxis (severe allergic reaction)
  • Angioedema (swelling of the face, lips or throat)

Very rare fewer than 1 in 10,000

  • Sudden vision loss in one eye (NAION)

Frequency not known reported, rate unclear

  • Bowel obstruction

Get help urgently if

  • Severe tummy pain that won’t settle, sometimes going through to the back (possible pancreatitis)
  • Pain under the right ribs, fever or yellow skin (possible gallstones)
  • Vomiting or diarrhoea so bad you can’t keep fluids down (risk of dehydration and kidney problems)
  • Swelling of the face or throat, or trouble breathing
  • Shakiness, sweating or confusion if you also take insulin or a sulfonylurea (low blood sugar)

Good to know

  • Muscle: in trials about a quarter to two-fifths of the weight lost was lean (non-fat) tissue, which includes muscle and water. Enough protein and strength exercise help protect muscle.
  • “Ozempic face” isn’t a medical term. It’s facial fat loss after fast weight loss from any cause, not a special drug effect.
  • Compounded copies have been banned in Australia since 1 October 2024. Online “semaglutide” or “tirzepatide” may be fake, contaminated or the wrong dose.
  • Pregnancy: not used. Stop semaglutide at least 2 months, and tirzepatide at least 1 month, before trying to conceive.
  • Supply: the Ozempic shortage (2022 to mid-2025) is over; new patients can start again.
  • Not suitable with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid cancer or MEN2. Tell your doctor before any surgery or anaesthetic.

Common questions

Which GLP-1 medicine causes the most weight loss?

Tirzepatide, on current evidence. In a head-to-head trial, people lost 20.2% of their weight on tirzepatide versus 13.7% on semaglutide over 72 weeks. Individual results vary a lot.

Is “Ozempic face” real?

It isn’t a medical diagnosis. Fast weight loss from any cause, including diet or surgery, shrinks fat in the face and can make skin look looser. It isn’t a special effect of the drug.

Are compounded or online GLP-1 injections safe?

Compounding them has been banned in Australia since October 2024 because the copies aren’t checked for safety, strength or purity. Use only TGA-approved products from a pharmacy.

Does weight come back after stopping a GLP-1 medicine?

Usually, yes. A year after stopping semaglutide in the STEP 1 trial, people had regained about two-thirds of the weight they’d lost.

Can I use GLP-1 medicines in pregnancy?

No. Stop semaglutide at least 2 months and tirzepatide at least 1 month before trying to get pregnant. Tirzepatide can also make the contraceptive pill less reliable.
