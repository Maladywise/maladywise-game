Drug info

Infliximab and adalimumab (anti-TNF)

Biologic (TNF blocker) Prescription only (S4), specialist

The first biologics for IBD, and still among the most effective, especially for fistulising Crohn’s. Infliximab is a drip; adalimumab is a self-injection.

Quick answer: Anti-TNF drugs block tumour necrosis factor, a key driver of gut inflammation. They induce and maintain remission, heal the bowel lining and fistulas, and reduce surgery. Biosimilars (cheaper copies) work the same. The body can make antibodies against them, so drug levels are sometimes checked.

The original gut firefighter.

Brand names

  • Infliximab: Remicade, Inflectra, Renflexis, Remsima (incl. under-skin form). Adalimumab: Humira, Hadlima, Hyrimoz, Amgevita, Yuflyma. Golimumab (Simponi) for colitis

How it works

They bind TNF-alpha, switching off a central inflammatory pathway.

Used for

  • Moderate to severe Crohn’s disease, including perianal fistulas
  • Moderate to severe ulcerative colitis
  • Also rheumatoid arthritis, psoriasis, ankylosing spondylitis and uveitis

Side effects

Infection risk is the main concern.

Full side-effect list (from the product information)

How often, from the official product information. Bold = serious.

Common 1 in 10 to 1 in 100

  • Infections (colds, sinus, chest, urine)
  • Infusion or injection-site reactions
  • Headache

Uncommon 1 in 100 to 1 in 1,000

  • Psoriasis-like rashes (paradoxical)
  • Abnormal liver tests

Rare 1 in 1,000 to 1 in 10,000

  • TB reactivation and serious infections
  • Lymphoma (mainly with thiopurine combination)
  • Demyelination, drug-induced lupus, heart failure worsening

Get help urgently if

  • Fever, night sweats, persistent cough or weight loss
  • Shortness of breath or swelling in heart failure
  • Numbness, weakness or vision changes

Good to know

  • Before starting: screening for tuberculosis, hepatitis B and C and HIV, and catch-up vaccines.
  • Avoid live vaccines (e.g. yellow fever, some shingles and travel vaccines) unless your specialist says otherwise.
  • Tell your doctors before surgery, and if you get a fever or infection.
  • Most biologics can be continued in pregnancy after discussion with your gastroenterologist.
  • Combining infliximab with azathioprine reduces antibodies and improves response.

Common questions

What is the difference between infliximab and adalimumab?

Both block TNF. Infliximab is usually an IV infusion every 8 weeks (an under-skin form now exists); adalimumab is a self-injection every 1 to 2 weeks.

Are biosimilars as good as the original?

Yes. Biosimilars are approved as equivalent and are widely used in Australia.

Why are drug levels checked?

Low levels or antibodies can explain loss of response, guiding a dose change or switch.
