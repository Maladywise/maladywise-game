Tools

Software that makes a GP’s day easier. Nobody paid to be on this list.

AI scribes

  • Heidi. An AI medical scribe that writes notes, letters and summaries from the consult.
  • Lyrebird Health. An Australian AI scribe that works with common GP software.

Practice analytics

  • Cubiko. Dashboards for general practice: billing, appointments and team performance.

Calculators

  • MDCalc. Hundreds of clinical scores and calculators: CHA₂DS₂-VASc, Wells, PERC, GCS, MELD and more.
  • Australian CVD Risk Calculator. The current national 5-year cardiovascular risk tool.

Diagnosis aids

  • Isabel. Differential diagnosis generator from symptoms and findings.
  • FindZebra. A search engine for rare diseases. Built for zebras.

Australian references

  • Australian Immunisation Handbook. Schedules, catch-up and special groups.
  • MBS Online. Item numbers and descriptors.
  • PBS. What’s subsidised and the restrictions.
  • RACGP Red Book. Preventive activities by age.
  • Therapeutic Guidelines (eTG) and AMH. Subscription prescribing references.

Built here

  • Iron infusion dose calculator. Ganzoni deficit plus the ferric carboxymaltose dosing table, with a dose plan.
  • Vertigo sorter. Red flags, then sort by timing and trigger to likely causes, bedside tests and next steps.

Listings aren’t endorsements or sponsorships. Check each tool meets your own privacy and clinical obligations.
