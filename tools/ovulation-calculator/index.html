How it works

  • Ovulation usually happens about 14 days before the next period (this second half of the cycle, the luteal phase, is fairly steady at 10 to 16 days). So in a 28-day cycle it’s around day 14, and in a 32-day cycle around day 18.
  • The fertile window is 6 days: the 5 days before ovulation plus ovulation day. Sperm can survive several days, but an egg lasts only about a day.
  • In a landmark study of 221 women trying to conceive, almost every pregnancy came from sex in that 6-day window. The chance of pregnancy was highest from sex in the 2 days before and on the day of ovulation (about 1 in 3 on the day itself).

Why calculators get it wrong (and what helps)

  • The same study team found the fertile window was entirely within days 10 to 17 for only about 30% of women. Many ovulate earlier or later, and it varies even when periods are regular.
  • Ovulation (LH) urine tests detect the hormone surge that comes 1 to 2 days before ovulation.
  • Cervical mucus that becomes clear, stretchy and slippery (like raw egg white) is a sign that ovulation is near.
  • Basal body temperature rises slightly after ovulation, so it confirms ovulation has happened rather than predicting it.
  • If you’re trying for a baby, having sex every 2 to 3 days through the cycle avoids the guesswork.

When to see a doctor

  • You’re under 35 and have been trying for 12 months, or 35 or over and trying for 6 months.
  • Your periods are irregular, very far apart (more than about 35 days), very painful, or have stopped.
  • You or your partner know of a possible fertility problem (for example endometriosis, PCOS, past pelvic infection, or cancer treatment). See your GP straight away rather than waiting.
  • Before trying: your GP can talk about folic acid, iodine, vaccinations, medicines and a health check.

Common questions

How many days after my period do I ovulate?

In a 28-day cycle, ovulation is usually around day 14, counting the first day of your period as day 1. In longer cycles it’s later and in shorter cycles earlier, because the time from ovulation to the next period stays fairly constant at about 14 days.

How long is the fertile window?

About 6 days: the 5 days before ovulation and ovulation day itself. The 2 days before ovulation and the day of ovulation give the best chance of pregnancy.

Can I get pregnant outside my fertile window?

It’s unlikely on any given day, but ovulation often comes earlier or later than calculators predict, even with regular periods. So a calculator can’t tell you which days are safe.

Can I use an ovulation calculator as contraception?

No. Calendar estimates alone aren’t reliable enough to prevent pregnancy. Talk to your GP or a sexual health clinic about effective contraception.

How long should we try before seeing a doctor?

See your GP if you’re under 35 and have been trying for 12 months, or 35 or over and trying for 6 months. Go sooner if you have irregular periods or know of a possible fertility problem.
