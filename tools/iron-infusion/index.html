The PI advises caution with the simplified method because it rests on limited trial data.

Limits per administration

  • Single dose: no more than 20 mg/kg, and no more than 1000 mg (20 mL).
  • No more than 1000 mg per week. Give any further dose at least 7 days later.
  • Under 35 kg: use Ganzoni only, with target Hb 130 g/L and stores 15 mg/kg (filled in automatically).
  • Ganzoni rounding (PI): round down to the nearest 100 mg if 66 kg or less, round up if over 66 kg.

Safety

  • Low phosphate. Common after ferric carboxymaltose, usually silent and short-lived. Repeat doses and risk factors (malnutrition, malabsorption, low vitamin D, hyperparathyroidism) can lead to osteomalacia and fractures. Check phosphate before repeat courses; ask about bone pain or tiredness.
  • Skin staining. Extravasation can cause long-lasting brown staining. Stop the infusion at once if leakage occurs, and warn the patient beforehand.
  • Hypersensitivity: give where resuscitation is available and observe for at least 30 minutes.

Alternative: ferric derisomaltose

Ferric derisomaltose (Monofer) is another high-dose IV iron. Trials show lower rates of low phosphate than ferric carboxymaltose, so it can suit patients at risk. Its doses and limits differ: use its own product information, not this calculator.
