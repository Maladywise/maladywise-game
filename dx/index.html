About Dxtective

Dxtective is a free daily diagnosis game, a medical mystery you solve one clue at a time. Each case is a fictional patient. You see the first clue and name the diagnosis; every wrong guess reveals another, up to six. Cases run from everyday GP presentations to genuine zebras, and every answer comes with a short clinical pearl.

How to play

  • Read the first clue and type your diagnosis.
  • Each wrong guess, or Next clue, reveals another clue. You get six.
  • Fewer clues means a better score and bragging rights.
  • Difficulty rises through the week: Easy Monday and Tuesday, Medium Wednesday and Thursday, Hard Friday and Saturday, Genius Sunday.

Who it’s for

Medical students, nurses, paramedics, doctors and anyone who loves a medical mystery. Easy days suit curious beginners; Genius days will test a consultant.

Common questions

Is there a daily diagnosis game I can play online?

Yes. Dxtective is a free daily diagnosis game: a new fictional case every day at midnight, Sydney time, solved in up to six clues.

Is Dxtective free?

Yes. It's free to play in your browser, with no sign-up, no download and no ads.

Are the cases real patients?

No. Every case is fictional, made for teaching and fun. It is general education, not medical advice.

Can students and nurses play?

Yes. Early-week cases are classic presentations that students and nurses will know; later in the week they get harder.
