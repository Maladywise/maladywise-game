About Suturix

Suturix is a free daily medical word search with a twist. Find the theme words woven through the grid, plus one spangram that runs edge to edge and names the theme. Together the words use every letter exactly once, so each find makes the rest easier.

How to play

  • Drag across letters, or tap them one by one, then press Enter. Diagonals count.
  • The gold spangram runs edge to edge and names the theme.
  • Every three non-theme words you find earns a hint.
  • Use every letter in the grid to finish.

Who it’s for

Word search fans, students, nurses, doctors and anyone who enjoys a themed medical puzzle.

Common questions

Is Suturix free?

Yes. It's free to play in your browser, with no sign-up, no download and no ads.

Do letters get reused?

No. Every letter in the grid belongs to exactly one word.

When is the new theme?

Every day at midnight, Sydney time.
