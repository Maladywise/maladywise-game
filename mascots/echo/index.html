Echo

Triviosis mascot

Know-it-all trivia hound. Repeats your mistakes back to you, lovingly.

Job
Medical lecturer
From
Central Africa, via a university lecture theatre
Personality
Bright, chatty, a show-off
Health quirk
Mild tinnitus from lecture halls

Her story

Echo spent her early years perched in a university lecture theatre, quietly memorising every slide. She can recite the Krebs cycle, the cranial nerves and every Nobel Prize in Medicine since 1901, but still can't remember where she left her cracker. She once corrected a professor mid-lecture and was asked to leave.

She came back the next week wearing a tiny disguise and corrected him again.

What she says when you win

  • “Squawk! Genius!”
  • “I'd repeat that score anywhere.”
  • “Big brain energy.”
  • “Polly wants your brain.”
  • “Fact-tastic.”
  • “Encyclopedic. Squawk.”
