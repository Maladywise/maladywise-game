Lottie

Anatomegaly mascot

She regrows her own limbs, so she's very relaxed about your mistakes. Labels everything anyway.

Her story

Lottie lived in the specimen jar room of an old anatomy school, where she taught herself every structure by reading the labels on the jars. When the labels started peeling, she bought a label gun and never looked back. Being an axolotl, she can regrow a lost leg, so she is very forgiving when you mix up your nerves.

She has labelled her own gills, her friends and once, briefly, the Dean.

What she says when you win

  • “Labelled to perfection.”
  • “Textbook anatomy. Literally.”
  • “Every part in its place.”
  • “I'd regrow a leg for that score.”
  • “Gray's Anatomy called. It's jealous.”
  • “Pin-point accurate.”
