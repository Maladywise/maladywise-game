Her story

Clover is a real cat, drawn as a cartoon. She is a tortoiseshell with a lot to say, and she says it, loudly, usually about her brother Basil. She has an appetite that has earned her the nickname Miss Piggy and a talent for finding food nobody knew was there.

Her other titles include Whingapotamus and Spicy Little Muffin. In the Basil + Clover arcade game, Basil kicks and Clover eats, which is exactly how it works at home.
