Her story

Nalu is a hammerhead shark whose eyes sit so far apart she can see almost all the way around herself. That made her a natural neurologist: she notices the sign on the left and the sign on the right at the same time. She loves the brain because it's the most complicated thing in the ocean, and she finds reflexes genuinely thrilling.
