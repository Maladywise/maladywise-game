Cadi

Linkase mascot

Smooth talker, fiercely competitive. Never lets go of a grudge or a link.

Her story

Cadi grew up coiled around the stethoscope rail in a busy emergency department, listening to doctors talk in chains: this leads to that, which causes this. She started linking everything, then everyone, then refused to let go of a single connection. She is competitive, charming and a sore loser.

She once held a grudge against a crossword setter for six years, and she will tell you it was justified.

What she says when you win

  • “Every link, locked.”
  • “Sssmooth chaining.”
  • “Unbroken. Impressive.”
  • “Sssensational.”
  • “Hiss-toric effort.”
  • “Chain reaction: genius.”
