Her story

Fig is a hedgehog who ran a tiny café before training as a dietitian. She got tired of customers asking which superfood would fix everything, so she went to find out properly. Her verdict: vegetables, fibre and actually enjoying your meals.

She keeps a carrot behind her ear, has no time for fad diets, and gets visibly prickly whenever anyone says "clean eating".
