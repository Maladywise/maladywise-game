Her story

Saga is an otter GP registrar who keeps her favourite stethoscope in a pouch under her arm, the way otters keep their favourite rock. She learns by doing, asks lots of questions and is never embarrassed to say 'I don't know yet'. She runs GP School because she remembers how scary the first shift was, and she wants yours to feel a little less so.
