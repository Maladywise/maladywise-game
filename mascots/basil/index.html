His story

Basil is a real cat, drawn as a cartoon. He is enormously fluffy, entirely white and convinced every object on a table is an experiment waiting to happen. He runs these experiments daily, with a paw, at eye contact.

He likes sitting in the bathroom sink and splashing the water, and he likes swiping his sister Clover, mostly because she complains about it. His nicknames include Furby with Fangs and Fat White Seal, and he answers to none of them. He stars in the Basil + Clover arcade game, where he kicks.

Everything.
