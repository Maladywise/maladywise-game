Her story

Tilly grew up rounding up sheep, so an emergency department waiting room felt like home on her first day. She looks once, decides who goes first, and gets everyone into the right line before they have finished explaining.

She runs Triagic from 4pm to midnight, when the waiting room is busiest. She never says the Q word, and she will give you a very hard stare if you do.
