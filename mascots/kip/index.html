Her story

Kip is a koala and a sleep scientist, which she says was always going to happen. Koalas sleep up to 20 hours a day, so she has done more fieldwork than anyone. She knows the difference between tired and sleepy, can talk about circadian rhythms for hours, and thinks scrolling in bed should be a crime.
