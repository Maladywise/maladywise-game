Her story

Dex grew up in the back room of a village library, reading every whodunit twice: once for fun, once to catch the author cheating. She trained as a doctor because patients turned out to be the best mysteries of all, each one a locked room with the clues hidden in plain sight. She keeps a corkboard of unsolved cases at home, complete with red string, and is no longer allowed to bring it to work.

The deerstalker was a thank-you gift from her first zebra diagnosis, and she has not taken it off since.
