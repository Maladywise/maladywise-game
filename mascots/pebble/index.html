Her story

Pebble grew up on a cold, windy island where penguins give each other pebbles as a sign of love. She started bringing pebbles of evidence instead: a trial here, a meta-analysis there, the one study everyone else missed. She dives deeper than most and always comes up with something worth keeping.

She wears a tiny diving suit for every dive, even though penguins really don't need one. She says it's professional.

What she says when you win

  • “Deep enough for you?”
  • “I brought you a pebble. It’s evidence.”
  • “Holding my breath for the next one.”
  • “Down we go.”
  • “Evidence collected. Pebble delivered.”
  • “You read past the abstract. Proud.”
