Her story

Mira grew up in a dermatology waiting room, sitting very still on a pot plant and watching every rash that came through the door. Nobody noticed her, so she noticed everything. She can tell a target lesion from a ringworm at twenty paces and turns a faint pink whenever someone says 'it's probably eczema' without looking properly.

She speaks rarely, but when she does, it's the one detail that changes the diagnosis.

What she says when you win

  • “Eyes everywhere. Including on that.”
  • “Spotted. Blink and you’d miss it. You didn’t.”
  • “Inspection: textbook.”
  • “I change colour. You change outcomes.”
  • “Seen it. Named it. Nailed it.”
  • “360-degree vision, 100% correct.”
