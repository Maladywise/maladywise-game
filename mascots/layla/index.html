Her story

Layla is a sloth who learnt the hard way that pushing through is how you crash. She moves slowly on purpose, plans her energy like a budget and rests before she's empty. She hosts the pacing and fatigue pages because she knows how frustrating it is when people say 'just try harder'.

Her motto: slow is a strategy, not a failure.
