About Wordemic

Wordemic is a free daily medical Wordle-style word game. Guess the hidden five-letter medical word in six tries; coloured tiles show which letters are in the word and in the right spot. Every Friday is Long Friday: an eight-letter word with seven tries. Every answer is a medical or health word with Australian spelling, and you get a short plain-English definition when you finish.

How to play

  • Type any real five-letter word and press Enter.
  • Green means right letter, right spot. Yellow means it's in the word but somewhere else. Grey means it's not in the word.
  • Use the clues to narrow it down. You have six tries.
  • On Long Fridays the word has eight letters and you get seven tries.

Who it’s for

Anyone who likes word puzzles, from nursing and medical students to doctors, pharmacists and people who just enjoy a good long word. You don't need medical training: plenty of answers are everyday health words.

Common questions

Is there a medical version of Wordle?

Yes. Wordemic is a free daily medical Wordle-style game: same idea, but every answer is a medical or health word.

Is Wordemic free?

Yes. It's free to play in your browser, with no sign-up, no download and no ads.

When does the new word come out?

Every day at midnight, Sydney time. Past answers and definitions are in the archive.

Can nurses and students play?

Absolutely. Easy days are common words most people know; difficulty rises through the week to a Genius Sunday.

Does it use Australian spelling?

Yes: haemoglobin, oedema, oesophagus and friends. American-only spellings aren't answers.
