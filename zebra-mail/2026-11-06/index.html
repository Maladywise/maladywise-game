The Weekly Dose, 6 Nov 2026

US$40 HIV jabs, volcano teeth and bat-jamming moths.

Cheaper HIV prevention, an island milestone and moths with a defence.

🩺 The Weekly Dose

  • The FDA tentatively approved a generic of the twice-yearly HIV-prevention jab lenacapavir: US$40 a year, for 120 lower-income countries. The US price tops US$28,000. MSF notes 26 countries miss out.
  • Barbados has eliminated mother-to-child HIV transmission: no new infant infections from 2022 to 2024. 98% tested, 98% treated. Unglamorous, relentless, effective.
  • Women starting modern HRT at 45–59 had no more heart attacks, strokes or clots than matched non-users over about 12 years (52,330 women). Observational abstract, not peer reviewed. The menopause wars continue.

🔬 Research corner

  • Newborn deaths in Flint, Michigan, halved after cash support in pregnancy and infancy: 10.2 to 5.1 per 1,000 births. Birth and death records, 2021–25, JAMA Pediatrics. Led by Dr Mona Hanna, who runs the program. Write-up Why it's interesting: a non-medical fix moved a hard outcome. Association only.
  • Women with longer reproductive lifespans had less small-vessel disease on brain MRI at 70. Longitudinal cohort, 352 Swedish women, Neurology. Led by Giulia Lorenzon and Anna Marseglia. Write-up Why it's interesting: it links lifetime oestrogen to brain vessels. Association, not cause.
  • A resting-brain scan pattern, trained on super-agers versus Alzheimer's, flagged later decline in cognitively normal adults. Machine-learning fMRI study, 1,300+ adults, Science Advances. Write-up Why it's interesting: it beat amyloid and tau markers. Not a clinical test.

🤯 Weird and wonderful

  • Hawkmoths have evolved genital ultrasound to jam bat sonar up to seven separate times. Evolution kept having the same idea. Nobody stopped it.
  • Earwax defeats ants: it soaks up the droplets that let their feet hold 60 times their body weight. Your ears have been running free pest control.
  • Teeth from an English Quaker cemetery link childhood vitamin D deficiency to volcanoes and a dim sun. Thirty teeth; Dr Ellen Kendall led. Industrial smog now has co-accused.
