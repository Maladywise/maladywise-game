About Zebritis

Zebritis is a free daily rare disease quiz game where obscure knowledge finally pays. Each prompt, like 'Name a cause of finger clubbing', has a long list of correct answers. Every answer scores 1 to 20 by rarity, and the single rarest answer, the unicorn, always scores 20. Wednesday is Super Zebritis, with five prompts instead of three.

How to play

  • Read the prompt and type any correct answer.
  • Rarer correct answers score more, from 1 to 20. Scores of 18 to 20 are unicorns.
  • You get three tries per prompt. Answers not on the list just use a try.
  • Spelling is forgiven within reason.

Who it’s for

Medical students, doctors, nurses and anyone who collects rare diseases, eponyms and odd facts.

Common questions

What is a medical zebra?

A rare diagnosis. Students are taught 'when you hear hoofbeats, think horses, not zebras'. Zebritis rewards the zebras.

Is Zebritis free?

Yes. It's free to play in your browser, with no sign-up, no download and no ads.

What's Super Zebritis?

Every Wednesday has five prompts instead of three.

When are the new prompts?

Every day at midnight, Sydney time. Past prompts and full answer lists are in the archive.
