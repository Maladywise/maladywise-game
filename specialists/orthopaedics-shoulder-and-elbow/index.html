Languages, Auslan and free interpreters
  • Find a language: open Filters and use “Speaks”, or type a language (e.g. Mandarin, Auslan) in the search box. We only list languages stated on a practice or practitioner’s own public pages, never guessed from a name.
  • Auslan: free sign language interpreting for health and medical appointments is now through the National Sign Language Program (it replaced NABS from 1 July 2024). It is for Deaf people aged 65 and over without an NDIS plan (50 and over for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people). Book with Deaf Connect: book online, call 1300 773 803 or SMS 0476 857 251. Details. Under 65: interpreting is funded through your NDIS plan.
  • Other languages, private doctors: GPs and specialists can book a free phone interpreter through TIS National’s Free Interpreting Service, using the Medical Practitioner Priority Line (often called the Doctors Priority Line) on 1300 131 450, 24 hours a day, for Medicare-rebatable appointments. Ask the practice to use it. TIS National.
  • Public hospitals: the Hunter New England Health Care Interpreter Service is free for all patients, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week: ask staff or call (02) 4924 6285. HNE multicultural health.

From official pages, checked 11 November 2026. Rules change: check with the service.
