Public and low-cost services
  • Hunter Primary Care: PHN-funded psychology: Free for eligible people (e.g. financial hardship, First Nations, LGBTQ+, perinatal, suicide risk). GP referral or Medicare Mental Health 1800 595 212. Intake (02) 4925 2259.
  • Hunter New England and Central Coast PHN: psychological services: PHN-funded care for moderate mental illness when other services are out of reach. Ask your GP or call 1800 595 212.
  • Muswellbrook Medicare Mental Health Centre: Free, no referral: walk in or call 1800 595 212. 160 Bridge St, Muswellbrook; outreach in Scone and Singleton.
  • Gidget Foundation: Start Talking telehealth: Free perinatal video counselling for expecting and new parents and after pregnancy loss. GP referral and mental health treatment plan. 1300 443 438.
  • Open Arms: Veterans and Families Counselling: Free counselling for ex-ADF members, partners and children. Newcastle centre; phone, online or face to face. 1800 011 046 (24/7).
  • NSW Victims Services: counselling: Up to 22 hours of free counselling for victims of violent crime in NSW. Victims Access Line 1800 633 063.
  • Relationships Australia NSW: Hunter centre: Individual, couples and family counselling, men's behaviour change and children's contact service. 6 Heddon Rd, Broadmeadow. 1300 364 277.
  • University of Newcastle Psychology Clinic: Low-cost CBT for children, teens and adults with supervised trainees ($25 a session, $15 low income, as listed). Callaghan or Zoom. (02) 4921 5075.
  • ACON counselling: Counselling for LGBTQ+ people, up to 12 sessions, by phone or telehealth anywhere in NSW. Hunter office, Islington: (02) 4962 7700.
  • Hunter New England Health: Farmgate Support Program: Mental health support for farmers and farming communities, including farm visits and peer workers.
  • Hunter New England Health: hospital social work: Counselling, crisis and bereavement support for patients and families in hospital. Ask your nurse or doctor for a referral.
  • Crisis: Lifeline 13 11 14 or NSW Mental Health Line 1800 011 511. Emergency: 000.
