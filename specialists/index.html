    Condition support organisations with their own provider lists

    These organisations keep their own lists. Where we found one of our listings on a list, its card shows a small badge, e.g. “Listed by AusPATH”. Checked 9 November 2026.

    • AusPATH: Find a provider Trans and gender-diverse affirming health providers (GPs, specialists, psychologists, allied health).
    • TransHub (ACON): Find a doctor Gender-affirming doctors in NSW.
    • InsideOut Institute: Treatment Services Database Eating disorder clinicians and services (psychologists, dietitians, psychiatrists).
    • ANZAED: Find eating disorder help (credentialed clinicians) ANZAED-credentialed eating disorder clinicians, by location or telehealth.
    • Butterfly Foundation: Find a professional Eating disorder and body image referral database.
    • AADPA: ADHD Professionals Directory ADHD clinicians: psychiatrists, paediatricians, GPs, psychologists, coaches.
    • ADHD Australia: ADHD treatment by state Where to start with ADHD care in each state.
    • Amaze (Autism Connect): National autism helpline Free helpline that helps find autism assessment and supports (no public practitioner list).
    • Aspect: Autism assessments Aspect's own autism assessment and therapy services.
    • Australian POTS Foundation: Clinician directory Clinicians with an interest in POTS and dysautonomia.
    • Mito Foundation: Find a health professional Mito-aware clinicians and clinics (details via the Mito helpline).
    • Emerge Australia: ME/CFS health practitioner directory ME/CFS-aware GPs and health practitioners.
    • Australasian Lymphology Association: Find an accredited lymphoedema practitioner ALA-accredited lymphoedema practitioners (mostly physios and OTs).
    • MS Australia: MS clinics MS clinics by state (the Hunter listing is a public hospital clinic).
    • Parkinson's NSW: Parkinson's InfoLine Phone line for finding Parkinson's nurses, clinics and allied health.
    • Coeliac Australia: Ongoing management Advice on seeing an accredited practising dietitian for coeliac disease.
    • Jean Hailes: Persistent pelvic pain Pelvic pain and endometriosis information, and who can help.
    • PANDA: Perinatal mental health helpline National perinatal anxiety and depression helpline.
    • Gidget Foundation: Gidget House Free perinatal psychology, including Gidget House Green Hills (East Maitland).

    A directory, not a recommendation. Private practices only, built from public sources (practice websites, hospital profiles, Healthdirect, professional directories).
