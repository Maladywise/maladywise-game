Psychiatry (telehealth)

36 listed, A–Z. Last checked 31 October 2026.

Video appointments, Australia-wide. Still needs a GP referral. Most telehealth bulk billing is for rural patients only, and Newcastle counts as metro, so expect private fees with a Medicare rebate. Check fees, wait times and whether the first visit can be by video before booking.

Badges show only where a practice says so publicly. Tap a badge to see its wording. Many practices offer this care without advertising it.
