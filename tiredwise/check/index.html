Worth reading

    Things that help many people while you wait

    • Pace, don't push. If activity causes crashes, do a bit less than you think you can, and rest before you're exhausted. Pushing through often backfires.
    • Keep a simple diary for 2 weeks: activity, sleep, symptoms and crashes. Your GP will find it very useful.
    • If standing makes you dizzy: drink plenty of water, stand up slowly, and ask your GP whether extra salt or compression stockings suit you.
    • Protect your sleep: a regular wake-up time and morning light help the body clock.
    • Feeling low or anxious is common with long-term tiredness, and treatable. It doesn't mean it's "all in your head".
