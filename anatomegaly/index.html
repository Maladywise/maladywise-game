About Anatomegaly

Anatomegaly is a free daily anatomy quiz. Each day brings five quick rounds: name a highlighted structure on a diagram, identify a bone, or work out which nerve supplies what. Rounds cover the brain, heart, skeleton, hand, eye, ear, abdomen and more. Four options per round, and every answer comes with a short explanation.

How to play

  • Look at the diagram or read the question.
  • Pick one of four options. One tap, no take-backs.
  • Every answer comes with a quick explanation.
  • Score 3 or more out of 5 to keep your streak.

Who it’s for

Anatomy and physiology students, nurses, physios, paramedics, doctors and anyone curious about the body. Easy days are school level; Genius days will test a surgeon.

Common questions

Is there a daily anatomy quiz?

Yes. Anatomegaly is a free daily anatomy quiz with five rounds every day.

Is Anatomegaly free?

Yes. It's free to play in your browser, with no sign-up, no download and no ads.

Are the diagrams accurate?

They are simplified teaching sketches drawn for this game, not to scale. Real anatomy varies between people.

When is the new set?

Every day at midnight, Sydney time. Past rounds and answers are in the archive.
