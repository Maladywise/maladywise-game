About

Malady Wise is run by an Australian GP who enjoys the strange end of medicine: zebras, eponyms and the case that doesn’t quite fit.

What you’ll find here

  • Daily puzzles. More than a dozen free games, new every midnight Sydney time: a diagnosis case (Dxtective), blood tests (Panelysis), clinical photos (Glimpsis), X-rays and scans (Opacitis), word games, trivia and a medical sudoku. They get harder through the week, Genius on Sundays, with a rare Einstein day.
  • ECGs twice a week. Arrhythmix: one fictional case and one ECG to read, every Monday and Thursday.
  • Weekly specials. A medical cryptic crossword every Saturday (Cryptoplexy) and twenty hard trivia questions every Sunday (Grand Rounds).
  • Normie mode. Flip the switch at the top for everyday, non-medical versions of the games.
  • Plain-English health guides. Conditions and Medicines explained in a couple of minutes, plus tools for health professionals.
  • Zebra Mail. Our free newsletter. Every Friday’s issue, The Weekly Dose, brings the week’s health news, interesting research and puzzles.

Every case and patient in the puzzles is fictional. Real clinical images are credited under each one.

How we make these pages

  • Drafted with AI, checked against sources. Fact sheets, medicine guides, tools and puzzles are drafted with the help of AI tools. The facts are then checked against the sources cited on each page, such as national guidelines, health department and hospital pages, and medicine information.
  • GP oversight. An Australian GP oversees the site and is personally reviewing pages over time. Health pages say “Made with AI tools and overseen by an Australian GP”. Once a GP has read and checked a page, it says “Reviewed by an Australian GP” with the date.
  • No sponsors. No pharmaceutical company sponsorship, no paid placements and no affiliate links. Brand names appear only so you can recognise a medicine.
  • General information only. Nothing here is personal medical advice. It can’t take your history, examine you or know your medicines, so talk to your own doctor or pharmacist before making changes.
  • Found an error? Tap “Report a problem” at the bottom of any page, or email maladywise@gmail.com with the page address and what’s wrong. We read every report and fix errors quickly.

What it isn’t

Personal medical advice. For that, see your own GP. Read the disclaimer.
