About

Malady Wise is made by an Australian GP who enjoys the strange end of medicine: zebras, eponyms and the case that doesn’t quite fit.

What you’ll find here

  • Daily puzzles. Nine games, new every midnight Sydney time. They get harder through the week, Genius on Sundays, with a rare Einstein day.
  • Grand Rounds. Twenty hard medical trivia questions every Sunday. Play solo or against a colleague.

Fictional cases and plain-English fact sheets are on the way.

What it isn’t

Personal medical advice. For that, see your own GP. Read the disclaimer.
