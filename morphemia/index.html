About Morphemia

Morphemia is a free daily medical spelling bee. Make as many words as you can from seven letters, always using the centre letter. Everyday Australian English words count, medical words score double, and a pangram that uses all seven letters earns a bonus. Climb the ranks from Student to Genius.

How to play

  • Make words of four or more letters from the seven on the hive.
  • Every word must use the centre letter. Letters can be reused.
  • Four-letter words score 1; longer words score a point per letter. Medical words score double.
  • Find a pangram (all seven letters) for 7 bonus points.

Who it’s for

Spelling bee fans, Scrabble players, and medical, nursing and pharmacy students who know their -itis from their -osis.

Common questions

Is there a medical spelling bee game?

Yes. Morphemia is a free daily medical spelling bee where medical words score double.

Is Morphemia free?

Yes. It's free to play in your browser, with no sign-up, no download and no ads.

What counts as a word?

Common Australian English words plus medical terms. Names and abbreviations don't count.

When do the letters change?

Every day at midnight, Sydney time.
