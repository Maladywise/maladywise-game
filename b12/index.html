Fact sheet

Vitamin B12 Deficiency

Low B12 can cause tiredness, anaemia, tingling, memory problems and a sore tongue. Common causes include a vegan diet, some medicines and pernicious anaemia.

Treatment options

Ratings show how strong the research is.

  • Strong evidenceB12 injectionsNeeded for severe deficiency, nerve symptoms or pernicious anaemia. A loading course, then every 2 to 3 months.
  • Strong evidenceTreat the causeFor example diet, metformin or acid-reducing medicines, coeliac disease or pernicious anaemia.
  • Strong evidenceLifelong treatment if neededPernicious anaemia and some causes need B12 for life.
  • Moderate evidenceHigh-dose B12 tabletsFor most people, high-dose tablets work as well as injections (Cochrane review). Taken daily.
  • Limited evidenceNasal sprays and patchesLittle evidence. Not recommended.

What you can do

  • Vegans and vegetarians: use fortified foods or a supplement.
  • Don’t stop treatment just because symptoms improve.

See your GP if

  • Tingling, numbness, unsteadiness or memory changes
  • You’re pregnant, vegan or on metformin long-term
  • Symptoms return

See a doctor urgently for new weakness, falls, or trouble walking.
