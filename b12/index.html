Fact sheet

Vitamin B12 Deficiency

Low B12 can cause tiredness, anaemia, tingling, memory problems and a sore tongue. Common causes include a vegan diet, some medicines and pernicious anaemia.

Treatment options

Ratings show how strong the research is.

  • Strong evidenceB12 injectionsNeeded for severe deficiency, nerve symptoms or pernicious anaemia. A loading course, then every 1 to 3 months depending on the type.
  • Strong evidenceTreat the causeFor example diet, metformin or acid-reducing medicines, coeliac disease or pernicious anaemia.
  • Strong evidenceLifelong treatment if neededPernicious anaemia and some causes need B12 for life.
  • Moderate evidenceHigh-dose B12 tabletsFor low B12 from diet or medicines, a daily high-dose tablet (1,000 micrograms) usually works as well as injections.
  • Limited evidenceNasal sprays and patchesLittle evidence. Tablets or injections are preferred.

What you can do

  • Vegans and vegetarians: use fortified foods or a supplement.
  • Don’t stop treatment just because symptoms improve.

See your GP if

  • Tingling, numbness, unsteadiness or memory changes
  • You’re pregnant, vegan or on metformin long-term
  • Symptoms return

See a doctor urgently for new weakness, falls, or trouble walking.

Common questions

What are the symptoms of low B12?

Tiredness, anaemia, tingling, memory problems and a sore tongue are common. New weakness, falls or trouble walking need a doctor urgently.

Are B12 tablets as good as injections?

For low B12 from diet or medicines, a daily high-dose tablet (1,000 micrograms) usually works as well as injections. Injections are needed for severe deficiency, nerve symptoms or pernicious anaemia.

Can metformin cause low B12?

Yes. Long-term metformin and acid-reducing medicines can lower B12. If you’ve been on metformin for a long time, ask your GP about checking your level.

How often are B12 injections needed?

After a loading course, usually every 1 to 3 months depending on the type. Pernicious anaemia and some other causes need B12 for life.
