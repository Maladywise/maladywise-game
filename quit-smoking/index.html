Fact sheet

Quitting Smoking

Quitting at any age improves your health, and the benefits start within days. Most people take several attempts, so a slip is not a failure. Support plus medicine gives you the best chance.

Treatment options

Ratings show how strong the research is.

  • Strong evidenceQuitline and quit appsCall 13 7848 (13 QUIT) for free phone coaching and a quit plan. The iCanQuit website and My QuitBuddy app also help. Works best alongside medicine.
  • Strong evidenceCombination nicotine replacementA daily patch plus a fast-acting gum, lozenge, spray or inhalator for cravings. Works better than one type alone. Patches are cheaper on a PBS script.
  • Strong evidenceVareniclinePrescription tablet, PBS-subsidised. The most effective single medicine. Start 1 to 2 weeks before your quit date and take for about 12 weeks. Nausea is common, so take it with food.
  • Strong evidenceCytisineA plant-based tablet that works like varenicline. Now available as a pharmacist-only medicine for adults aged 18 to 65. Ask your pharmacist or GP.
  • Strong evidenceBupropionPrescription tablet, about as effective as nicotine replacement. Not suitable if you have seizures or an eating disorder.
  • Moderate evidenceNicotine vapesHelp more people quit than nicotine replacement in trials, but long-term safety is unknown and none are TGA-approved. Pharmacy only: adults after a talk with the pharmacist; under 18s need a prescription. Plan to stop vaping too.
  • Limited evidenceHypnosis or acupunctureLittle good evidence they help.
  • Not recommendedIllegal vapesNot from a pharmacy, often with undeclared nicotine and chemicals. Avoid.

What you can do

  • Set a quit date and stop completely on that day.
  • Know your triggers (coffee, alcohol, stress, after meals) and plan a swap.
  • Cravings pass in a few minutes: delay, drink water, move.
  • Withdrawal (irritability, poor sleep, hunger) peaks in the first few days and usually settles within 2 to 4 weeks.
  • A pack a day costs over $15,000 a year.

See your GP if

  • You want a prescription medicine or help choosing one
  • You are pregnant, under 18, or have heart or mental health conditions
  • You take clozapine, warfarin or insulin, as doses may need changing when you stop smoking

Stop the medicine and see a doctor promptly if you notice major changes in mood or behaviour while taking varenicline or bupropion. For thoughts of self-harm, call Lifeline on 13 11 14 or 000.

Common questions

What is the most effective way to quit smoking?

Support plus medicine gives the best chance. Varenicline is the most effective single medicine, and combination nicotine replacement works better than one type alone. Quitline (13 7848) offers free coaching.

How long do nicotine withdrawal symptoms last?

Irritability, poor sleep and hunger peak in the first few days and usually settle within 2 to 4 weeks. Individual cravings pass in a few minutes.

Can vapes help you quit smoking?

Nicotine vapes helped more people quit than nicotine replacement in trials, but long-term safety is unknown and none are TGA-approved. In Australia they are sold only through pharmacies. Avoid illegal vapes.

What is cytisine?

A plant-based tablet that works like varenicline. It is available as a pharmacist-only medicine for adults aged 18 to 65.
