About Mergeon

Mergeon is a free organ merge game. Drop organs; two of a kind merge into the next one up, all the way from a microbe to a brain. Don't let the pile cross the line. Everyone gets the same drops each day, so you can compare scores.

From 16 November 2026, every day has a twist: Microgravity Monday, a random mutation on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday, Blood Bank Wednesday (merge only compatible blood groups), Sick Day Friday and Outbreak Sunday. There are also three daily ward round goals, a collection book with hidden organs to find, and a learn mode that shows each organ's name and a fact as you merge.

How to play

  • Aim and drop each organ.
  • Two of the same organ merge into the next one up.
  • Keep the pile below the line.
  • Bigger merges score more. Aim for the brain.
  • The rare stem cell is wild: it merges with any organ and turns it into the next one up.

Who it’s for

Anyone who likes merge and drop games. No medical knowledge needed.

Common questions

Is Mergeon free?

Yes. It's free to play in your browser, with no sign-up, no download and no ads.

Is it the same for everyone?

Yes. Everyone gets the same drops each day, so scores are comparable.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. It works in any modern phone or computer browser.
