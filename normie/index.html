What is it?

Malady Wise is a daily puzzle site built by an Australian GP. Most of it is medical. Normie mode swaps in everyday-knowledge versions of all fifteen games, so your friends can play without a stethoscope. Flip the 🩺 Medical | 🙂 Normie switch at the top of any page.

The games

  • Dxtective: what am I? Six fun-fact clues, one thing. Every wrong guess unlocks the next clue.
  • Panelysis: a mystery stats card. One mystery, a card of real numbers, three tries to name it.
  • Glimpsis: what is it? A super close-up or microscope picture of an everyday thing, animal or plant. Six options, three tries, and a fun fact.
  • Opacitis: what’s inside? A real X-ray of an everyday object or animal, never a person. Six options, three tries.
  • Triviosis: daily trivia. Ten questions, one theme, no medicine.
  • Wordemic: guess the five-letter word in six tries. Word guessing, with more sass.
  • Zebritis: name a correct answer. The rarer it is, the more it scores. Saying ‘Paris’ is allowed. It just scores like a horse.
  • Sudokrine: sudoku with fruit. Every row, column and box gets one of each. A 6x6 mini early in the week, a full 9x9 at the weekend.
  • Comorbix: sixteen everyday words, four hidden groups. Find the groups, dodge the red herrings.
  • Gridectomy: a five-by-five mini crossword with everyday clues. Done before the kettle boils.
  • Suturix: find the theme words woven through a letter grid, plus the spangram that names the theme.
  • Morphemia: seven letters, one compulsory centre letter. Build words and climb from Beginner to Genius.
  • Linkase: a word chain. Fire, truck, stop, watch. Fill in the links without dropping it.
  • Arrhythmix: every Thursday, a mystery chart of real-world data. Read the trend and name what it shows.
  • Cryptoplexy: every Saturday, a 13-by-13 cryptic crossword. Every answer is everyday English, no medicine.
  • Grand Rounds: every Sunday, twenty questions on the week that was. No options to hide behind.
