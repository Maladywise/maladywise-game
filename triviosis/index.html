About Triviosis

Triviosis is a free daily medical trivia quiz. Ten multiple-choice questions on one theme each day, from anatomy and medical history to weird-but-true facts and the day's world health awareness day. Every answer comes with a short explanation, so you learn something even when you're wrong.

How to play

  • Read the question and tap one of four answers.
  • You'll see the right answer and a short explanation straight away.
  • Six or more out of ten is a pass. Ten is bragging rights.
  • Come back tomorrow for a new theme.

Who it’s for

Anyone who enjoys a medical quiz: students revising, nurses and doctors on a break, and curious people who like health facts.

Common questions

Is there a daily medical quiz online?

Yes. Triviosis is a free daily medical trivia quiz with ten questions and explanations.

Is Triviosis free?

Yes. It's free to play in your browser, with no sign-up, no download and no ads.

Do I need to be a doctor?

No. Questions mix general knowledge with medicine, and every answer is explained.

When does the new quiz come out?

Every day at midnight, Sydney time. Past quizzes and answers are in the archive.
