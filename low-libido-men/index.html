Low Libido in Men

Sex drive naturally rises and falls with stress, sleep, health and relationships. Low testosterone is a cause much less often than the ads suggest. It’s worth a chat with your GP when it bothers you or your partner.

Common causes

  • Stress, tiredness and poor sleep, including untreated sleep apnoea
  • Depression and anxiety, and the medicines for them (especially SSRIs and SNRIs)
  • Other medicines: opioids, finasteride and dutasteride, spironolactone, some blood pressure and prostate cancer treatments
  • Relationship issues, conflict, or erection problems that make sex feel like an exam
  • Alcohol, cannabis and anabolic steroids (especially coming off them)
  • Health conditions: low testosterone, diabetes, obesity, thyroid disease, high prolactin, chronic illness

Tests your GP may do

  • Morning testosterone (before 10 am, ideally fasting), repeated if low. One low result isn’t a diagnosis.
  • LH and FSH, prolactin, iron studies, thyroid, sugar and cholesterol
  • Screening for low mood, alcohol use and sleep apnoea

What helps

Start with the cause. Testosterone only helps men who truly have low testosterone.

  1. Step 1 · Self-careSleep, exercise, alcohol and stressFix the basics that quietly drain desire.
    Works
    Exercise and weight loss raise testosterone and energy; less alcohol helps erections and drive
    Cost
    Free
    How long
    Ongoing
    Watch for
    Snoring with daytime tiredness: ask about sleep apnoea.

    Libido hates being tired, drunk and stressed. So do most people.

  2. Step 2 · TalkTalk with your partner, or see a counsellor or sex therapistDesire is often about context: conflict, routine, pressure or a mismatch in drive.
    Works
    Sex therapy and couples counselling help many couples; good for desire mismatch
    Cost
    Counselling varies; Medicare rebates for some psychology with a GP plan
    How long
    Several sessions
    Watch for
    Feeling flat most days for 2 weeks or more: that may be depression.
  3. Step 3 · Review medicinesCheck whether a medicine is the culpritYour GP may lower a dose, switch, or add something.
    Works
    Switching or adding bupropion can help antidepressant-related low libido
    Cost
    Usual script costs
    How long
    Weeks
    Watch for
    Don’t stop antidepressants suddenly; taper with your doctor.

    Works
    Treating erection problems often restores confidence and desire
    Cost
    Varies
    How long
    Varies

  5. Step 5 · SpecialistTestosterone, only for confirmed low testosteronePrescribed after repeated low morning levels plus symptoms, usually with a specialist or under PBS rules.
    Works
    Improves libido in men with genuinely low levels; little benefit if levels are normal
    Cost
    $25 a script on the PBS when criteria are met; otherwise private

Save your money

Popular, heavily marketed or often asked about, but the evidence is weak or says no.

  • “Testosterone booster” supplementsTribulus, fenugreek, horny goat weed and friends: no good evidence they raise testosterone or desire.
  • Testosterone with normal levelsLittle benefit, real risks: shrunken testes, infertility, thick blood and acne.
  • Online men’s health subscriptions without a proper checkFine for some, but skipping the history and tests can miss depression, diabetes or heart disease.

Common questions

What causes low libido in men?

Most often stress, tiredness, poor sleep, depression, relationship issues, alcohol and medicines such as antidepressants. Low testosterone is a less common cause.

Should I get my testosterone checked?

Yes, if low libido persists, especially with erection problems, fatigue or loss of morning erections. It needs a morning test, repeated if low.

Will testosterone fix my libido?

Only if your testosterone is genuinely low. In men with normal levels it adds little and can cause infertility and other side effects.
