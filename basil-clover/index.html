      About Basil + Clover

      Basil + Clover is a free cat arcade game based on two real (and worse) cats. In The Kitchen, help Basil, a smug fluffy white cat, kick everything off the bench while his sister Clover eats anything in sight. Then survive The Vet: thermometers, clippers and a 'just a little scratch'.

      How to play

      • Tap a thing (or use the arrow keys): Basil kicks it, Clover eats it.
      • Hands off anything marked with a red sign, and don't let a timer run out.
      • Ten rounds, faster every round, with bosses in rounds 5 and 10.
      • Beat the Kitchen to open the Vet. A daily challenge gives everyone the same rounds.

      Who it’s for

      Cat people, mostly. Anyone who's watched a cat push a mug off a table on purpose.
