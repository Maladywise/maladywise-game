How to play
  • Tap or click a thing (or keys ← ↓ → / A S D): Basil kicks it, Clover eats it. ↑ / W grabs anything flying.

      About Basil + Clover

      Basil + Clover is a free cat arcade game based on two real (and worse) cats. In The Kitchen, help Basil, a smug fluffy white cat, kick everything off the bench while his sister Clover eats anything in sight. Then survive The Vet: thermometers, clippers and a 'just a little scratch'. Then the Farm: hay bales, a rooster, Farmer Fern and an onion you must not eat. Malady Wise mascots wander past too.

      How to play

      • Tap a thing (or use the arrow keys): Basil kicks it, Clover eats it.
      • Hands off anything marked with a red sign, and don't let a timer run out.
      • Ten rounds, faster every round, with bosses in rounds 5 and 10.
      • Ten stages: Kitchen, Vet, Farm, Beach, Ward, Hell, Bath Time, plus Christmas, Easter and Halloween. The daily challenge picks a different stage each day of the week, and the holiday ones in season.

      Who it’s for

      Cat people, mostly. Anyone who's watched a cat push a mug off a table on purpose.
