About Gridectomy

Gridectomy is a free daily medical mini crossword. Fill a five-by-five grid where every row and column is a word, with clues drawn from medicine, anatomy and health. It's a quick solve for a coffee break: most people finish in a few minutes, and Hard days take a bit longer.

How to play

  • Tap a square to select it; tap again to switch between Across and Down.
  • Type your answer. The arrows or Tab jump between clues.
  • Check marks wrong letters; Reveal and Give up end your clean solve.
  • The timer pauses when you leave the page.

Who it’s for

Crossword fans, students, nurses, doctors and anyone after a short daily medical crossword.

Common questions

Is there a daily medical crossword?

Yes. Gridectomy is a free daily medical mini crossword, and Cryptoplexy is a weekly medical cryptic crossword every Saturday.

Is Gridectomy free?

Yes. It's free to play in your browser, with no sign-up, no download and no ads.

How long does it take?

Most people finish in a few minutes. Hard days take longer.

When is the new grid?

Every day at midnight, Sydney time.
