Depression criteria checklist

NameDate

Symptoms: tick each present most of the day, nearly every day, for the same 2 weeks

  • Low, sad or empty mood (or irritable, in young people)
  • Lost interest or pleasure in most things
  • Big change in appetite or weight
  • Sleeping much less or much more than usual
  • Moving or thinking noticeably slower, or restless, as seen by others
  • Tired or low on energy
  • Feeling worthless or excessively guilty
  • Trouble thinking, concentrating or making decisions
  • Thoughts of death or suicide, or a plan or attempt

The rest of the criteria: all of these

  • 5 or more ticked, including low mood or loss of interest
  • Causes real distress or gets in the way of daily life
  • Not due to a substance, medicine or another medical condition
  • Never had a manic or hypomanic episode (that would suggest bipolar disorder)

Safety

  • If you have thoughts of suicide, talk to someone today: Lifeline 13 11 14, or 000 in an emergency.

Structure based on the DSM-5-TR criteria for a major depressive episode. Symptom descriptions are in our own words, not the DSM wording. Diagnosis needs a clinical assessment.
