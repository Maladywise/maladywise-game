Answer yes or no to each question.

QuestionAnswer (points)Score
1Snoring: Do you snore loudly (louder than talking or loud enough to be heard through closed doors)? No (0) Yes (1)____
2Tired: Do you often feel tired, fatigued or sleepy during the daytime? No (0) Yes (1)____
3Observed: Has anyone observed you stop breathing during your sleep? No (0) Yes (1)____
4Pressure: Do you have or are you being treated for high blood pressure? No (0) Yes (1)____
5BMI: Is your body mass index more than 35 kg/m²? No (0) Yes (1)____
6Age: Are you over 50 years old? No (0) Yes (1)____
7Neck: Is your neck circumference greater than 40 cm? No (0) Yes (1)____
8Gender: Are you male? No (0) Yes (1)____

Total score: ______

  • Count the yes answers. Total 0 to 8.
  • 0–2 low risk · 3–4 intermediate risk · 5–8 high risk of obstructive sleep apnoea.
  • Also high risk: 2 or more of the first four (STOP) plus male, BMI over 35 or neck over 40 cm. A sleep study confirms it.

Good to know

  • It screens; it doesn’t diagnose. A clinician puts the score together with the whole story.
  • If you’re not safe right now, call 000. Lifeline is 13 11 14 (24 hours).
