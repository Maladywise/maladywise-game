Not here, and why

  • DASS-21: free for clinicians, but its authors ask that it isn’t offered to the public on websites. Ask your GP or psychologist. Details: www2.psy.unsw.edu.au/dass.
  • ASRS 18-question checklist: needs permission; the free 6-question screener is here.
  • Epworth Sleepiness Scale: licensed, so we can’t reproduce it. Your GP or sleep clinic will have a copy.
  • RAADS-R: copyrighted; ask the clinician doing your autism assessment.
