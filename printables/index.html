Diagnostic criteria checklists

  • Fibromyalgia criteriaA checklist version of the 2016 fibromyalgia criteria
  • hEDS criteriaA checklist version of the 2017 criteria for hypermobile Ehlers-Danlos syndrome. All three criteria must be met.
  • ADHD criteria checklistA plain-English checklist that follows the structure of the DSM-5-TR ADHD criteria, for taking to an appointment.
  • Depression criteria checklistA plain-English checklist that follows the structure of the DSM-5-TR criteria for a major depressive episode.
  • ME/CFS criteriaA checklist version of the 2015 National Academy of Medicine criteria for ME/CFS (myalgic encephalomyelitis/chronic fatigue syndrome).
  • IBS criteria (Rome IV)A checklist version of the Rome IV criteria for irritable bowel syndrome (IBS), plus the warning signs that need checking first.
  • PCOS criteriaA checklist version of the 2023 international criteria for polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) in adults, with the different rules for teenagers.
  • MCAS criteriaA checklist version of the consensus criteria for mast cell activation syndrome (MCAS). All three criteria are needed.
  • Autism criteria checklistA plain-English checklist that follows the structure of the DSM-5-TR autism criteria, for taking to an assessment.
  • Giant cell arteritis criteriaA points checklist following the 2022 ACR/EULAR classification criteria for giant cell arteritis (temporal arteritis).

Not here, and why

  • DASS-21: free for clinicians, but its authors ask that it isn’t offered to the public on websites. Ask your GP or psychologist. Details: www2.psy.unsw.edu.au/dass.
  • ASRS 18-question checklist: needs permission; the free 6-question screener is here.
  • Epworth Sleepiness Scale: licensed, so we can’t reproduce it. Your GP or sleep clinic will have a copy.
  • RAADS-R: copyrighted; ask the clinician doing your autism assessment.
