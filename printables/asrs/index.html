Ticks in shaded boxes: ______

  • Count the ticks in shaded boxes.
  • 4 or more suggests symptoms consistent with adult ADHD and that a full assessment is worthwhile.

Good to know

  • It screens; it doesn’t diagnose. A clinician puts the score together with the whole story.
