Kessler RC and colleagues. The K10 is free to use; Australian scoring bands as used in Australian health surveys. Printed from maladywise.com/printables/k10/. A screening tool, not a diagnosis.

Good to know

  • It screens; it doesn’t diagnose. A clinician puts the score together with the whole story.
