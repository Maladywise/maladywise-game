New atrial fibrillation: work-up checklist

Patient: ____________________ Date: ____________ AF type: first detected / paroxysmal / persistent / permanent

Urgent first

  • Unstable (low BP, chest pain, heart failure, syncope) or rate over 150: send to the emergency department
  • Onset under 48 hours and very symptomatic: discuss with ED or cardiology about cardioversion

Tests

  • 12-lead ECG (QT, LVH, pre-excitation)
  • FBC
  • UEC, eGFR, CrCl (Cockcroft-Gault)
  • LFTs
  • TSH
  • HbA1c or glucose
  • Coags (baseline INR/APTT)
  • Magnesium and calcium
  • Lipids
  • Transthoracic echo
  • BNP if heart failure suspected
  • Troponin only if ACS suspected

Total (out of 8): ______

  • 2 or more: anticoagulation recommended. 1: consider it. 0: no anticoagulant. (Sex isn’t counted.)
  • A DOAC is preferred over warfarin unless there’s a mechanical valve or moderate–severe mitral stenosis. Aspirin isn’t stroke prevention in AF.

Bleeding risk

  • Fix what can be fixed: blood pressure, alcohol, NSAIDs or antiplatelets, anaemia, labile INR, kidney and liver function
  • A high bleeding score is a reason to review more often, not on its own a reason to withhold anticoagulation

Rate and rhythm

  • Rate control: beta-blocker, or diltiazem/verapamil if no heart failure with reduced EF; digoxin as an add-on
  • Lenient target: resting heart rate under 110 if symptoms are controlled
  • Refer for rhythm control (cardioversion, drugs, ablation) if still symptomatic, young, new heart failure, or AF diagnosed within the last year
  • Before elective cardioversion: anticoagulated for 3 weeks, or a TOE

Risk factors (they change AF burden)

  • Weight: aim for 10% loss if overweight
  • Alcohol: cut down or stop
  • Blood pressure to target
  • Screen for sleep apnoea (STOP-Bang)
  • Regular moderate exercise
  • Smoking

Based on the NHFA/CSANZ Australian atrial fibrillation guideline (2018) and the ESC AF guideline (2024). Check current Therapeutic Guidelines and product information for doses. Printed from maladywise.com/printables/new-af-checklist/.
