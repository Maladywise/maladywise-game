  • Supports POTS: heart rate rises by 30 or more (40 or more if under 19) on at least two readings within 10 minutes, without blood pressure falling by 20/10 mmHg or more.
  • One normal test doesn’t rule POTS out. Repeat on a bad day, in the morning, before caffeine and fluids.
  • Bring this sheet to your GP. It doesn’t diagnose POTS on its own: other causes need to be checked.
