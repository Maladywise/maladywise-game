IBS criteria (Rome IV)

NameDate

Tummy pain: needed

  • Recurring abdominal pain, on average at least 1 day a week over the last 3 months

Plus at least 2 of these

  • The pain is linked to doing a poo (better or worse)
  • The pain started with a change in how often you go
  • The pain started with a change in what your poo looks like (harder or looser)

Timing

  • Symptoms for the last 3 months, starting at least 6 months ago

Warning signs to raise with your GP first (they need tests, not just an IBS label)

  • New symptoms after age 50
  • Weight loss you can’t explain
  • Blood in the poo or black poo
  • Waking at night to open your bowels
  • Low iron or anaemia
  • Family history of bowel cancer, coeliac disease or inflammatory bowel disease
  • Fever or a lump in the tummy

Based on the Rome IV criteria for irritable bowel syndrome (Lacy et al. 2016). Summarised in our own words. Printed from maladywise.com/printables/ibs-criteria/. For discussion with a clinician; it can’t diagnose on its own.
