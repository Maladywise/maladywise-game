Printable Autism criteria checklist

A plain-English checklist that follows the structure of the DSM-5-TR autism criteria, for taking to an assessment.

Autism criteria checklist

NameDate

A. Social communication: all three, now or in the past

  • Back-and-forth social connection is different (e.g. conversation, sharing interests or feelings)
  • Non-verbal communication is different (e.g. eye contact, gestures, facial expressions, tone)
  • Making, keeping or understanding relationships is hard (e.g. adjusting to social settings, making friends)

B. Repetitive patterns: at least two, now or in the past

  • Repetitive movements, use of objects or speech (e.g. lining things up, echoing phrases, stimming)
  • Strong need for sameness, routines or rituals; small changes are very distressing
  • Intense, focused interests that are unusual in strength or focus
  • Over- or under-sensitivity to sound, light, touch, smell, taste, pain or temperature

The rest of the criteria: all of these

  • Signs were present from early childhood, even if they only became obvious later or were masked
  • They cause real difficulty in social, work, study or daily life
  • Not better explained by intellectual disability or global developmental delay

Structure based on the DSM-5-TR criteria for autism spectrum disorder. Descriptions are in our own words, not the DSM wording. Diagnosis needs a full assessment by an experienced clinician.
