Printable ADHD criteria checklist

A plain-English checklist that follows the structure of the DSM-5-TR ADHD criteria, for taking to an appointment.

ADHD criteria checklist

NameDate

Inattention: tick each that has often been true for 6 months or more

  • Misses details or makes careless mistakes
  • Struggles to keep attention on tasks or play
  • Seems not to listen when spoken to directly
  • Doesn’t follow through or finish tasks
  • Has trouble organising tasks and belongings
  • Avoids or dislikes tasks needing sustained mental effort
  • Loses things needed for tasks
  • Easily distracted by things going on around them (or by thoughts, in adults)
  • Forgetful in daily activities

Hyperactivity and impulsivity: tick each that has often been true for 6 months or more

  • Fidgets, taps or squirms
  • Leaves their seat when expected to stay put
  • Runs or climbs at the wrong times (adults: feels restless)
  • Can’t do leisure activities quietly
  • Always ‘on the go’, as if driven by a motor
  • Talks a lot
  • Blurts out answers before questions are finished
  • Struggles to wait their turn
  • Interrupts or intrudes on others

The rest of the criteria: all of these

  • 6 or more in either list (5 or more if aged 17 or older)
  • Several symptoms were present before age 12
  • Several symptoms show up in 2 or more settings (e.g. home, school, work)
  • They clearly get in the way of school, work or social life
  • Not better explained by another condition (e.g. anxiety, mood, substance use)

Structure based on the DSM-5-TR criteria for ADHD. Symptom descriptions are in our own words, not the DSM wording. Diagnosis needs a full clinical assessment. Printed from maladywise.com/printables/adhd-criteria/. For discussion with a clinician; it can’t diagnose on its own.
