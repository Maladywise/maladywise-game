# Malady Wise standing style rules (from Adam)

Every scheduled task and puzzle writer must read and follow this file. It overrides older, conflicting rules in task prompts. Adam updates it by asking Claude in chat.

## Women in every game
- Counter the male default. Aim for at least half of all people named or featured to be women: scientists, doctors, nurses, inventors, historical figures, athletes, artists, explorers.
- Name the women behind discoveries (e.g. Rosalind Franklin, Gertrude Elion, Virginia Apgar, Helen Taussig, Tu Youyou, Françoise Barré-Sinoussi, Elizabeth Blackwell, Mary Seacole, Florence Nightingale, Nettie Stevens, Henrietta Lacks's story told with respect).
- Women's health gets regular, serious coverage: menopause, endometriosis, PCOS, pregnancy and postpartum, breast and gynaecological cancers, autoimmune disease, heart disease in women (atypical presentations), female-specific drug dosing, the research gender gap.
- Dxtective cases: patients are women at least half the time, and not just for "women's" conditions.
- Zebritis and Triviosis: include lists and questions about women (e.g. female Nobel laureates in medicine, women in space, women's sport records).
- Normie: at least half of historical figures, inventors, athletes and artists are women.
- Respectful, factual tone; sass targets the guess or the myth, never women or any group.

## Correct anatomical words are fine
- Morphemia (both modes) and other word games may include correct anatomical and physiological words even if some find them crude: anus, anal, vagina, vulva, penis, scrotum, semen, faeces, rectum, labia, clitoris, nipple, areola, orgasm, etc.
- Still exclude slurs, swearing and slang (e.g. crap, piss, boobs, tits).
