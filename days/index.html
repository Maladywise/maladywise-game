Common questions

What health awareness day is today?

Check the top of this page: it shows today’s health day, awareness week or medical-history anniversary (Sydney time) and the next 30 days. Every date on Malady Wise has a theme, and days marked 🧩 get themed puzzles.

When is World Health Day?

World Health Day is on 7 April every year. It marks the founding of the World Health Organization in 1948, and each year WHO picks a different global health theme.

What are the WHO official health days?

WHO runs a set of official global public health campaigns, including World Health Day (7 April), World TB Day (24 March), World Immunization Week (last week of April), World Malaria Day (25 April), World No Tobacco Day (31 May), World Blood Donor Day (14 June), World Hepatitis Day (28 July), World Patient Safety Day (17 September), World AMR Awareness Week (18–24 November) and World AIDS Day (1 December). It also backs days such as World NTD Day, World Chagas Disease Day and World Hand Hygiene Day. Tap the WHO filter in the list below to see them all with dates.
