# Medical word list (Australian spelling)

`medical-au.txt` is the extra medical vocabulary accepted in Malady Wise games.

- Derived from [hunspell-en-med-glut](https://github.com/Glutanimate/hunspell-en-med-glut) (GPL v3), which merges OpenMedSpel (e-MedTools) and MTH-Med-Spel-Chek (MT-Herald).
- Changes: converted to Australian/British spelling (haem-, oedema, -aemia, -rrhoea, -ise, tumour...), dropped US-only spellings, proper nouns and rare fragments, filtered by word frequency, and added a short supplement of newer drug names.
