Six clues, six tricks

Anagram, hidden word, charade, container, homophone and double definition. All medical, all ours.

  1. 1. Severn flowing, giving you the jitters (6) Anagram: SEVERN, “flowing”, rearranges to NERVES. Jitters is the definition.
  2. 2. Organ hidden in Bali very much (5) Hidden word: baLI VERy.
  3. 3. Tablet: a constant, then two lefts (4) Charade: PI (a constant) + L + L (two lefts).
  4. 4. Like inside right hand? It’s an outbreak on the skin (4) Container: AS (like) inside R (right) + H (hand).
  5. 5. Get better, we hear, at the back of the foot (4) Homophone: sounds like HEAL.
  6. 6. Calm person in the waiting room (7) Double definition: calm, and someone waiting to see the doctor.
