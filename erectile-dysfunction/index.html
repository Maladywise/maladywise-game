Erectile Dysfunction

Trouble getting or keeping an erection. Very common, very treatable, and sometimes an early warning from your blood vessels. Worth a proper check-up, not just a script.

How common: more than 1 in 10 men, and more with age. Occasional problems are normal and usually down to stress, tiredness or alcohol.

Why it happens: blood vessels (high blood pressure, cholesterol, diabetes, smoking), nerves, hormones (low testosterone, thyroid), some medicines (for blood pressure, depression or prostate cancer) and the mind (performance anxiety, stress, depression, relationship strain). Often a mix.

The heart link: erection problems can be the first sign of heart disease, diabetes, high blood pressure or high cholesterol. Expect your GP to check blood pressure, blood sugar and cholesterol, and testosterone (on a morning sample) if needed.

Save your money

Popular, heavily marketed or often asked about, but the evidence is weak or says no.

  • Erection pills bought online without a prescriptionThey may be fake, the wrong dose or contaminated. Suspected counterfeits can be reported to the TGA in Australia.
  • “Herbal” erection boostersUnregulated and untested. If one works, ask what’s really in it.
  • Low-intensity shockwave therapyEvidence that it works isn’t robust yet, and courses are expensive.
  • Testosterone with normal levelsOnly helps when a properly timed morning test shows low testosterone.

What you can do

  • See your GP for a heart and sugar check, not just a script.
  • Take tablets 1–2 hours before, avoid a big meal or alcohol beforehand, and give them 5–6 tries.
  • Talk with your partner. Pressure makes it worse; teamwork makes it better.
  • Pelvic floor training may help a little: see our pelvic floor exercises for men.
  • Struggling with stress or mood? MensLine Australia offers free 24/7 counselling on 1300 78 99 78.

See your GP if

  • Erection problems keep happening
  • You take nitrate medicines for angina
  • You also have low sex drive, tiredness or breast changes
  • Your penis has developed a bend or lump
  • Problems started after a new medicine or prostate treatment

Get emergency help (call 000 (Australia) or your local emergency number. From a mobile, 112 works in most countries.) for chest pain during sex or exercise, or an erection that won’t go down after 4 hours.

Common questions

Is erectile dysfunction just part of getting older?

It becomes more common with age, but it is not inevitable. It can be an early sign of heart disease, diabetes or high blood pressure, so it is worth a check-up.

Is it safe to buy erection tablets online?

Only from a reputable pharmacy with a prescription. Products sold online without a prescription may be fake, poor quality or unsafe.

Why didn’t the tablets work for me?

They need sexual arousal, work best 1 to 2 hours after taking them, and can be blunted by a heavy meal, alcohol or anxiety. Judge them over 5 to 6 attempts before deciding.

Will erection tablets increase my sex drive?

No. They improve blood flow to the penis but do not increase desire. Low sex drive needs a separate check.

What causes erectile dysfunction?

Most often reduced blood flow from diabetes, high blood pressure, high cholesterol or smoking, plus nerve problems, low testosterone, some medicines, alcohol, stress and relationship issues. Because it shares causes with heart disease, it can be an early warning sign worth checking.

Is sildenafil (Viagra) or tadalafil (Cialis) better?

They work about equally well, helping roughly 2 to 3 in 4 men. Sildenafil (Viagra) works for about 4 to 6 hours, while tadalafil (Cialis) lasts up to 36 hours and can be taken daily at a low dose. Which suits you depends on your routine and side effects.

Can erectile dysfunction be cured?

Sometimes. Treating the cause can restore erections: in one trial, about 1 in 3 men with obesity regained normal erections after 2 years of weight loss and more exercise. Stopping smoking, cutting alcohol and reviewing medicines can help too.
