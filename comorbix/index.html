About Comorbix

Comorbix is a free daily medical connections game. Sixteen medical terms hide four groups of four that share something: a body system, a drug class, an eponym, or a sneaky bit of wordplay. Find all four groups before you make four mistakes. Groups get trickier from yellow to purple, and the week gets harder from Monday to Sunday.

How to play

  • Tap four tiles you think belong together and press Submit.
  • A correct group locks in; a wrong one costs a mistake. You get four.
  • Three out of four gets a 'one away' warning.
  • Yellow is the easiest group, purple the hardest (usually wordplay).

Who it’s for

Medical and nursing students, doctors, allied health and anyone who enjoys connections-style word puzzles with a medical twist.

Common questions

Is there a medical version of Connections?

Yes. Comorbix is a free daily medical connections game: four groups of four medical terms, new every day.

Is Comorbix free?

Yes. It's free to play in your browser, with no sign-up, no download and no ads.

When is the new puzzle released?

Every day at midnight, Sydney time. Past groups and answers are in the archive.

Do I need to be a doctor?

No. Early-week puzzles lean on everyday health words; Sunday is where it gets clinical.
