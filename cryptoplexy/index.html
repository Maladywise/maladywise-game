About Cryptoplexy

Cryptoplexy is a free weekly medical cryptic crossword, released every Saturday. Every answer is medical, and each clue has a straight definition plus wordplay that builds the same answer: anagrams, hidden words, sound-alikes and more. Solve a clue and tap it to see how it works, so it's a gentle way to learn cryptics too.

How to play

  • Each clue has a definition at one end and wordplay at the other.
  • The number in brackets is the answer length.
  • Tap a square to choose it; tap again to switch across or down.
  • Check word and Reveal letter help if you're stuck. Progress saves automatically.

Who it’s for

Cryptic crossword fans, doctors, nurses and students, and beginners who want every clue explained.
