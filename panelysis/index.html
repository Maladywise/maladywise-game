About Panelysis

Panelysis is a free daily blood test quiz. Each day you get a fictional patient and their results: full blood count, electrolytes, kidney and liver tests, thyroid, iron studies, blood gases and more. Read the panel and pick the diagnosis from six options in three tries. Every answer shows the clinching values and a teaching point.

How to play

  • Read the patient's story and their blood results. H is high, L is low.
  • Pick the diagnosis from six options. You get three tries.
  • Each wrong pick reveals a hint and highlights the results that matter.
  • Finish to see why: the key values and a teaching pearl.

Who it’s for

Medical, nursing and science students learning to read blood results, plus doctors, nurses and pharmacists who want daily practice.

Common questions

Is there a quiz to practise reading blood tests?

Yes. Panelysis is a free daily blood test quiz with a new fictional case every day.

Is Panelysis free?

Yes. It's free to play in your browser, with no sign-up, no download and no ads.

Are the reference ranges real?

They are typical Australian adult ranges. Every lab differs slightly, so always use the range printed on a real report.

When is the new panel?

Every day at midnight, Sydney time. Past panels and answers are in the archive.
