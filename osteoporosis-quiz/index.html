Osteoporosis Quiz

Twelve questions, easy to hard, on osteoporosis: what it is, who gets tested, what a T-score means and which treatments prevent fractures.

  1. Question 1 of 12

    What is osteoporosis?

    1. A long-lasting infection in the bone
    2. Bones growing too thick and heavy
    3. Inflamed, swollen and stiff joints
    4. Bones become thinner and weaker, so break more easily
    Show answer

    Answer: Bones become thinner and weaker, so break more easily. It’s silent until a fracture happens.

  2. Question 2 of 12

    Which fracture is a classic osteoporotic fracture?

    1. A toe stubbed on furniture
    2. A broken finger in a car door
    3. Hip, spine or wrist from a minor fall
    4. A skull fracture in a car crash
    Show answer

    Answer: Hip, spine or wrist from a minor fall. A fracture from standing height or less is called a minimal trauma fracture and should prompt testing.

  3. Question 3 of 12

    Which scan measures bone density?

    1. A DXA (bone density) scan
    2. An ultrasound of the heel only
    3. An MRI
    4. A CT of the head
    Show answer

    Answer: A DXA (bone density) scan. It gives a T-score comparing your bones with a healthy young adult’s.

  4. Question 4 of 12

    What T-score means osteoporosis?

    1. −1.0
    2. −2.5 or lower
    3. +2.5
    4. Above zero
    Show answer

    Answer: −2.5 or lower. Between −1 and −2.5 is osteopenia (low bone density).

  5. Question 5 of 12

    Who is at higher risk?

    1. Professional weightlifters
    2. People who eat lots of dairy
    3. Teenage boys
    4. Women after menopause
    Show answer

    Answer: Women after menopause. Falling oestrogen speeds bone loss. Men get it too, often later.

  6. Question 6 of 12

    Which medicine is a well-known cause of bone loss?

    1. Long-term steroid tablets (e.g. prednisolone)
    2. Antihistamines for hay fever
    3. Paracetamol, taken daily
    4. Most antibiotics
    Show answer

    Answer: Long-term steroid tablets (e.g. prednisolone). Bone protection is often needed for people on prednisolone 7.5 mg or more for 3 months or longer.

  7. Question 7 of 12

    What type of exercise is best for bones?

    1. Bed rest to protect them
    2. Gentle stretching only
    3. Weight-bearing and resistance exercise
    4. Swimming only
    Show answer

    Answer: Weight-bearing and resistance exercise. The LIFTMOR trial showed supervised high-intensity resistance training improved bone density safely in women with low bone mass.

  8. Question 8 of 12

    How should most adults get enough calcium?

    1. High-dose supplements, for everyone
    2. From food first, such as dairy or fortified foods
    3. It isn't needed once bones stop growing
    4. From several cups of coffee a day
    Show answer

    Answer: From food first, such as dairy or fortified foods. Supplements are used when diet falls short, often with vitamin D if levels are low.

  9. Question 9 of 12

    What do bisphosphonates such as alendronate do?

    1. Thin the blood to protect bones
    2. Build muscle around the bones
    3. Cure arthritis in the joints
    4. Slow bone breakdown and reduce fracture risk
    Show answer

    Answer: Slow bone breakdown and reduce fracture risk. Weekly tablets must be taken on an empty stomach with water, staying upright for 30 minutes.

  10. Question 10 of 12

    Why must denosumab injections not be stopped without a plan?

    1. They stop working after a single dose
    2. They make bones too strong and brittle
    3. Stopping suddenly can cause rapid bone loss and fractures
    4. They are addictive, causing withdrawal symptoms
    Show answer

    Answer: Stopping suddenly can cause rapid bone loss and fractures. It’s given every 6 months. If stopping, a follow-on treatment is usually needed.

  11. Question 11 of 12

    What rare side effect is linked to bone-strengthening medicines?

    1. Osteonecrosis of the jaw
    2. Colour blindness
    3. Hair loss
    4. Deafness
    Show answer

    Answer: Osteonecrosis of the jaw. Very rare at osteoporosis doses. Good dental care and telling your dentist reduce the risk further.

  12. Question 12 of 12

    Which medicine builds new bone, rather than just slowing loss?

    1. Alendronate
    2. Teriparatide or romosozumab
    3. Calcium
    4. Vitamin C
    Show answer

    Answer: Teriparatide or romosozumab. These anabolic medicines are used for severe osteoporosis, often followed by a medicine that slows breakdown.

Common questions

Can osteoporosis be reversed?

Treatment can increase bone density and cut fracture risk substantially, though bones rarely return to full young-adult strength.

Should I take calcium supplements?

Food is best. Ask your GP whether you need a supplement based on your diet and bone health.

Is this quiz medical advice?

No. It’s general education. See your GP about your own symptoms or results.
