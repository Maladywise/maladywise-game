Mouth Ulcers

A small, round, painful sore that makes every meal a negotiation. Mouth ulcers are very common, affecting up to 1 in 4 people at some point. Most heal by themselves within 2 weeks. One that lasts longer than 3 weeks needs a check.

What they are: most are aphthous ulcers: shallow white or yellow sores with a red edge. Minor ulcers are under about 5 mm and heal in around 2 weeks without scarring. Larger ulcers can take weeks and leave a scar.

Common triggers: biting your cheek, a sharp tooth or brace, burns from hot food, stress and tiredness, hormone changes, stopping smoking, and toothpastes containing sodium lauryl sulphate. They often run in families.

Linked conditions: low iron, B12, folate or zinc, coeliac disease, Crohn’s disease, and, rarely, immune conditions such as Behçet’s disease. Several ulcers at once can also be a virus such as hand, foot and mouth disease.

What you can do

  • Note when ulcers appear: stress, periods, new toothpaste or a sharp tooth.
  • See a dentist for sharp teeth, rough fillings or loose dentures.
  • Eat a varied diet with iron and B vitamins.
  • If you also have diarrhoea, bloating or weight loss, read our coeliac disease fact sheet.
  • For blisters on the lips, see our cold sores fact sheet.
  • Keep up regular dental check-ups.

See your GP if

  • An ulcer lasts more than 3 weeks
  • It looks different from usual: bigger, at the back of the mouth, or bleeding
  • It gets more painful and red, which can mean infection
  • You get ulcers very often, or several at once
  • You also have sore eyes, genital sores, rashes or painful, swollen joints
  • You have diarrhoea, weight loss or tiredness as well

The 3-week rule: most mouth ulcers heal within 2 weeks. Any ulcer, lump or patch in the mouth that lasts more than 3 weeks needs checking by a GP or dentist.

Common questions

What causes mouth ulcers?

Often minor injury such as biting your cheek, stress, tiredness, hormone changes or certain toothpastes. Frequent ulcers can be linked to low iron, B12 or folate, or coeliac disease.

How do I make a mouth ulcer heal faster?

Steroid treatments started early, at the first tingle, work best. Antiseptic mouthwash can reduce how bad and how long an ulcer lasts. Avoid spicy, acidic and crunchy foods while it heals.

When is a mouth ulcer serious?

If it lasts more than 3 weeks, keeps bleeding, or comes with other symptoms such as weight loss or joint pain. Long-lasting ulcers need checking to rule out mouth cancer.

Are mouth ulcers contagious?

Common aphthous ulcers are not. Some viruses, such as hand, foot and mouth disease and cold sores, cause sores that are.

How long do mouth ulcers last?

Most common mouth ulcers heal within 1 to 2 weeks without scarring. Any ulcer that hasn’t healed after 3 weeks should be checked by a dentist or GP.

Can stress cause mouth ulcers?

Stress is a common trigger, along with biting your cheek, sharp teeth, some toothpastes with sodium lauryl sulfate, and hormone changes. Frequent ulcers can also point to low iron, B12 or folate, or coeliac disease.

Is it a mouth ulcer or a cold sore?

Mouth ulcers are round, white or yellow sores with a red edge on the soft skin inside the mouth and aren’t contagious. Cold sores are fluid-filled blisters on or around the lips caused by a virus, and they are contagious.
