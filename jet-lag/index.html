Fact sheet

Jet Lag

Tiredness, poor sleep, poor concentration and an upset stomach after flying across 3 or more time zones, because your body clock is still on home time. Usually worse flying east. It settles on its own, at roughly a day per time zone crossed (a little faster flying west).

Treatment options

Ratings show how strong the research is.

  • Moderate evidenceDaylight at the right timeThe strongest signal for resetting your body clock. After flying east, get morning light. After flying west, get afternoon and evening light.
  • Moderate evidenceMelatoninA low dose (0.5 to 5 mg) at bedtime at your destination for a few days helps many travellers, especially flying east across 5 or more time zones. Needs a prescription in Australia, except slow-release tablets for people over 55.
  • Limited evidenceShift your sleep before you goFor 2 to 3 days before you leave, go to bed and get up an hour earlier each day if flying east, or later if flying west.
  • Limited evidenceCaffeineHelps daytime alertness. Avoid it in the 6 hours before bed.
  • Limited evidenceShort-acting sleeping tabletsA doctor may suggest one for a few nights on arrival. They help sleep but don’t reset your body clock.
  • Not recommendedAlcohol to help sleepWorsens sleep quality and dehydration, on the plane and after.

What you can do

  • Set your watch to destination time when you board, and sleep or stay awake to match.
  • Drink water and move around the cabin on long flights.
  • On arrival, eat and sleep on local time. Keep naps under 30 minutes.
  • For trips of 2 days or less, staying on home time may be easier.
  • Avoid important decisions on your first day.

See your GP if

  • You take medicines at set times, such as insulin or the pill, and need a plan for time zones
  • You’d like to discuss melatonin or sleeping tablets before you travel
  • Sleep problems last more than 2 weeks after you return

See a doctor urgently for a painful, swollen calf, chest pain or sudden breathlessness after a long flight, as these can be signs of a blood clot. Call 000 if severe.

Common questions

How long does jet lag last?

Roughly a day for each time zone crossed, a little faster flying west. It settles on its own.

Does melatonin help jet lag?

A low dose (0.5 to 5 mg) at bedtime at your destination for a few days helps many travellers, especially flying east across 5 or more time zones. It needs a prescription in Australia, except slow-release tablets for people over 55.

Is jet lag worse flying east or west?

Usually east. After flying east, get morning daylight. After flying west, get afternoon and evening light.
