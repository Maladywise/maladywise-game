Heart Physiology Quiz

10 questions on how the heart works: pacemakers, pressures, output and the ECG. Easy to hard. Tap an answer to see if you’re right.

  1. Question 1 of 10

    What sets the normal heart rhythm?

    1. Atrioventricular (AV) node
    2. Sinoatrial (SA) node
    3. Aortic valve
    4. Purkinje fibres
    Show answer

    Answer: Sinoatrial (SA) node. In the right atrium, firing about 60 to 100 times a minute at rest.

  2. Question 2 of 10

    Cardiac output equals…

    1. Stroke volume ÷ heart rate
    2. Heart rate × blood volume
    3. Heart rate × stroke volume
    4. Blood pressure × heart rate
    Show answer

    Answer: Heart rate × stroke volume. About 5 litres a minute at rest, up to 20 or more in athletes.

  3. Question 3 of 10

    What does the P wave on an ECG show?

    1. Ventricular depolarisation
    2. Valve closure
    3. Ventricular repolarisation
    4. Atrial depolarisation
    Show answer

    Answer: Atrial depolarisation. QRS is the ventricles firing; the T wave is them resetting.

  4. Question 4 of 10

    The first heart sound (S1, ‘lub’) is caused by…

    1. Mitral and tricuspid valves closing
    2. The ventricles relaxing
    3. Blood rushing into the atria
    4. Aortic and pulmonary valves closing
    Show answer

    Answer: Mitral and tricuspid valves closing. S2 (‘dub’) is the aortic and pulmonary valves closing.

  5. Question 5 of 10

    The Frank–Starling law says the heart…

    1. Beats faster when stretched
    2. Pumps harder when it’s stretched by more filling
    3. Only responds to adrenaline
    4. Pumps less when overfilled at normal volumes
    Show answer

    Answer: Pumps harder when it’s stretched by more filling. Within normal limits, more blood in means more blood out.

  6. Question 6 of 10

    The coronary arteries fill mostly during…

    1. Systole
    2. Both equally
    3. Atrial contraction
    4. Diastole
    Show answer

    Answer: Diastole. Contracting heart muscle squeezes its own vessels shut. Fast heart rates shorten diastole.

  7. Question 7 of 10

    A normal adult resting blood pressure is about…

    1. 200/120 mmHg
    2. 90/40 mmHg
    3. 120/80 mmHg
    4. 160/100 mmHg
    Show answer

    Answer: 120/80 mmHg. Systolic over diastolic. Lower is generally better down to about 100 systolic.

  8. Question 8 of 10

    Which hormone made by the heart helps the kidneys lose salt and water?

    1. Atrial natriuretic peptide
    2. Aldosterone
    3. ADH
    4. Renin
    Show answer

    Answer: Atrial natriuretic peptide. Released when the atria are stretched. BNP from the ventricles is a heart failure blood test.

  9. Question 9 of 10

    The AV node delays the signal so that…

    1. The atria finish emptying before the ventricles contract
    2. The valves can open
    3. The heart rate slows to 30
    4. The SA node can rest
    Show answer

    Answer: The atria finish emptying before the ventricles contract. That delay is the PR interval on the ECG, normally 0.12 to 0.2 seconds.

  10. Question 10 of 10

    How much blood does the heart pump in a day at rest?

    1. About 70 litres
    2. About 7,000 litres
    3. About 700 litres
    4. About 70,000 litres
    Show answer

    Answer: About 7,000 litres. Roughly 5 L a minute × 1,440 minutes.

Common questions

What causes the heartbeat?

Special pacemaker cells in the SA node fire on their own. The signal spreads through the atria, pauses at the AV node, then races down to the ventricles.

What is cardiac output?

The amount of blood the heart pumps each minute: heart rate times the amount per beat. About 5 litres a minute at rest.
