Bowel Physiology Quiz

10 questions on how the bowel works: absorption, movement, the microbiome and stools. Easy to hard. Tap an answer to see if you’re right.

  1. Question 1 of 10

    Most nutrients are absorbed in the…

    1. Stomach
    2. Rectum
    3. Small intestine
    4. Large intestine
    Show answer

    Answer: Small intestine. Villi and microvilli give it a huge surface area.

  2. Question 2 of 10

    Vitamin B12 is absorbed in the…

    1. Colon
    2. Terminal ileum
    3. Stomach
    4. Duodenum
    Show answer

    Answer: Terminal ileum. It needs intrinsic factor from the stomach. Crohn’s or ileal surgery can cause deficiency.

  3. Question 3 of 10

    Iron is mainly absorbed in the…

    1. Duodenum
    2. Terminal ileum
    3. Stomach
    4. Colon
    Show answer

    Answer: Duodenum. Coeliac disease damages the duodenum, so iron deficiency is a common sign.

  4. Question 4 of 10

    What is the main job of the large bowel (colon)?

    1. Absorbing B12
    2. Digesting protein
    3. Making bile
    4. Absorbing water and salt
    Show answer

    Answer: Absorbing water and salt. It turns about 1.5 litres of liquid a day into formed stool.

  5. Question 5 of 10

    The wave-like muscle movement that pushes food along is…

    1. Defecation
    2. Segmentation
    3. Peristalsis
    4. Mastication
    Show answer

    Answer: Peristalsis. Controlled by the enteric nervous system, the ‘second brain’ with about 500 million neurons.

  6. Question 6 of 10

    Which body part has the most bacteria?

    1. Stomach
    2. Skin
    3. Small intestine
    4. Colon
    Show answer

    Answer: Colon. Tens of trillions of microbes, which make vitamin K and short-chain fatty acids.

  7. Question 7 of 10

    The gastrocolic reflex makes you…

    1. Need to open your bowels soon after eating
    2. Feel full
    3. Vomit
    4. Hiccup
    Show answer

    Answer: Need to open your bowels soon after eating. Stretch of the stomach boosts colon movement. Strong in babies and in IBS.

  8. Question 8 of 10

    Brown stool colour mainly comes from…

    1. Iron
    2. Stercobilin, from bilirubin
    3. Food dye
    4. Bacteria alone
    Show answer

    Answer: Stercobilin, from bilirubin. Pale stools can mean bile isn’t reaching the gut.

  9. Question 9 of 10

    Lactose intolerance is due to low levels of…

    1. Amylase
    2. Lipase
    3. Lactase
    4. Pepsin
    Show answer

    Answer: Lactase. Most adults worldwide make less lactase after childhood. Undigested lactose is fermented by bacteria.

  10. Question 10 of 10

    Gut transit time (mouth to bottom) is usually about…

    1. 1 to 3 weeks
    2. 12 minutes
    3. 1 to 3 hours
    4. 1 to 3 days
    Show answer

    Answer: 1 to 3 days. Most of that time is spent in the colon.

Common questions

What does the gut microbiome do?

Gut microbes ferment fibre into helpful fatty acids, make vitamins, train the immune system and keep harmful bacteria in check.

How long does food take to pass through the body?

Usually 1 to 3 days, though it varies a lot between people and with diet.
