Bone Physiology Quiz

10 questions on how the bone works: remodelling, calcium, vitamin D and healing. Easy to hard. Tap an answer to see if you’re right.

  1. Question 1 of 10

    Which cells break down bone?

    1. Osteoclasts
    2. Osteocytes
    3. Osteoblasts
    4. Chondrocytes
    Show answer

    Answer: Osteoclasts. Osteoblasts build bone. Clasts ‘cut’, blasts ‘build’.

  2. Question 2 of 10

    About how much of the body’s calcium is in bone and teeth?

    1. About 75%
    2. About 99%
    3. About 10%
    4. About 50%
    Show answer

    Answer: About 99%. Bone is a calcium bank the body can draw on.

  3. Question 3 of 10

    Which hormone raises blood calcium by releasing it from bone?

    1. Thyroxine
    2. Insulin
    3. Parathyroid hormone (PTH)
    4. Calcitonin
    Show answer

    Answer: Parathyroid hormone (PTH). It also boosts vitamin D activation and kidney calcium reabsorption.

  4. Question 4 of 10

    Vitamin D mainly helps by…

    1. Building cartilage
    2. Lowering calcium
    3. Making bone cells divide
    4. Increasing calcium absorption from the gut
    Show answer

    Answer: Increasing calcium absorption from the gut. Severe deficiency causes rickets in children and osteomalacia in adults.

  5. Question 5 of 10

    The whole adult skeleton is replaced about every…

    1. 1 year
    2. 10 years
    3. 1 month
    4. 50 years
    Show answer

    Answer: 10 years. About 10% a year through constant remodelling.

  6. Question 6 of 10

    Where are blood cells made in adults?

    1. Red marrow in flat bones and spine
    2. Yellow marrow in long bones
    3. The liver
    4. The spleen
    Show answer

    Answer: Red marrow in flat bones and spine. Children make blood in nearly all bones; adult long bones fill with fatty yellow marrow.

  7. Question 7 of 10

    Bones grow in length at the…

    1. Marrow cavity
    2. Periosteum
    3. Joint cartilage
    4. Growth plates (epiphyseal plates)
    Show answer

    Answer: Growth plates (epiphyseal plates). They fuse after puberty, ending height growth.

  8. Question 8 of 10

    The first stage of fracture healing is a…

    1. Scar of cartilage only
    2. Hard callus
    3. Blood clot (haematoma)
    4. Remodelled bone
    Show answer

    Answer: Blood clot (haematoma). Then a soft callus, a hard callus, and remodelling over months.

  9. Question 9 of 10

    Which medicines treat osteoporosis by slowing osteoclasts?

    1. Loop diuretics
    2. Proton pump inhibitors
    3. Steroids
    4. Bisphosphonates (e.g. alendronate)
    Show answer

    Answer: Bisphosphonates (e.g. alendronate). Denosumab works on osteoclasts too. Steroids and PPIs can weaken bone.

  10. Question 10 of 10

    Weight-bearing exercise strengthens bone because…

    1. Bone responds to load by building more
    2. It lowers calcium
    3. It stops growth plates fusing
    4. It raises vitamin D
    Show answer

    Answer: Bone responds to load by building more. Wolff’s law. Astronauts lose bone in zero gravity.

Common questions

Is bone alive?

Yes. Bone is living tissue with cells, blood vessels and nerves, constantly being broken down and rebuilt.

How can I keep my bones strong?

Weight-bearing and resistance exercise, enough calcium and vitamin D, not smoking, limiting alcohol and checking medicines that weaken bone.
