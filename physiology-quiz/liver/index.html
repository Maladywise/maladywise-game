Liver Physiology Quiz

10 questions on how the liver works: bile, detox, clotting proteins and sugar storage. Easy to hard. Tap an answer to see if you’re right.

  1. Question 1 of 10

    Which protein made by the liver keeps fluid in the blood vessels?

    1. Haemoglobin
    2. Insulin
    3. Albumin
    4. Ferritin
    Show answer

    Answer: Albumin. Low albumin in liver disease lets fluid leak out: swollen ankles and ascites.

  2. Question 2 of 10

    Bilirubin comes from the breakdown of…

    1. Bile acids
    2. Haemoglobin
    3. Fat
    4. Muscle
    Show answer

    Answer: Haemoglobin. The liver makes it water-soluble so it can leave in bile. A build-up causes jaundice.

  3. Question 3 of 10

    The liver stores glucose as…

    1. Glycogen
    2. Albumin
    3. Starch
    4. Cholesterol
    Show answer

    Answer: Glycogen. It releases glucose between meals and overnight to keep blood sugar steady.

  4. Question 4 of 10

    Which clotting factors need vitamin K, made in the liver?

    1. I, V, VIII and XII
    2. Platelets
    3. Only factor VIII
    4. II, VII, IX and X
    Show answer

    Answer: II, VII, IX and X. Warfarin blocks vitamin K recycling, so it lowers these factors.

  5. Question 5 of 10

    About what share of the liver’s blood comes from the portal vein?

    1. About 100%
    2. About 25%
    3. About 10%
    4. About 75%
    Show answer

    Answer: About 75%. Nutrient-rich gut blood. The hepatic artery supplies the rest, with more of the oxygen.

  6. Question 6 of 10

    What does bile mainly help digest?

    1. Protein
    2. Fat
    3. Fibre
    4. Starch
    Show answer

    Answer: Fat. Bile salts emulsify fat so lipase can work. Bile is stored in the gallbladder.

  7. Question 7 of 10

    In paracetamol overdose, the liver runs out of…

    1. Bilirubin
    2. Glycogen
    3. Glutathione
    4. Albumin
    Show answer

    Answer: Glutathione. A toxic metabolite (NAPQI) builds up. Acetylcysteine replenishes glutathione.

  8. Question 8 of 10

    The liver turns ammonia from protein breakdown into…

    1. Urea
    2. Uric acid
    3. Creatinine
    4. Glucose
    Show answer

    Answer: Urea. Failing livers let ammonia build up, causing confusion (hepatic encephalopathy).

  9. Question 9 of 10

    Which is true of liver regeneration?

    1. It regrows in an hour
    2. It can’t regenerate
    3. It only regrows in children
    4. It can regrow to full size after up to about two-thirds is removed
    Show answer

    Answer: It can regrow to full size after up to about two-thirds is removed. That’s why living donors can give part of their liver.

  10. Question 10 of 10

    Alcohol is mainly broken down in the liver by…

    1. Alcohol dehydrogenase
    2. Pepsin
    3. Lipase
    4. Amylase
    Show answer

    Answer: Alcohol dehydrogenase. Then aldehyde dehydrogenase. Some people have a slow version, causing flushing.

Common questions

What does the liver do?

Over 500 jobs, including making bile, clotting proteins and albumin, storing glucose and vitamins, and breaking down drugs, alcohol and toxins.

Can the liver repair itself?

Yes, it regenerates remarkably well, but long-term damage (from alcohol, fatty liver or hepatitis) can cause scarring (cirrhosis).
