Skin Physiology Quiz

10 questions on how the skin works: barrier, temperature, pigment and healing. Easy to hard. Tap an answer to see if you’re right.

  1. Question 1 of 10

    The outermost layer of skin is the…

    1. Hypodermis
    2. Dermis
    3. Epidermis
    4. Fascia
    Show answer

    Answer: Epidermis. It has no blood vessels; it’s fed from the dermis below.

  2. Question 2 of 10

    Which cells make the pigment melanin?

    1. Langerhans cells
    2. Merkel cells
    3. Keratinocytes
    4. Melanocytes
    Show answer

    Answer: Melanocytes. Everyone has a similar number. Skin colour depends on how much and what type of melanin they make.

  3. Question 3 of 10

    Sweat cools the body by…

    1. Radiating heat
    2. Evaporating
    3. Conducting heat to clothes
    4. Reflecting sunlight
    Show answer

    Answer: Evaporating. Humid air slows evaporation, which is why heat feels worse when it’s muggy.

  4. Question 4 of 10

    Skin makes vitamin D when exposed to…

    1. UVB light
    2. UVA light
    3. Infrared light
    4. Blue light
    Show answer

    Answer: UVB light. UVB converts 7-dehydrocholesterol to vitamin D3.

  5. Question 5 of 10

    How often is the epidermis replaced?

    1. Never
    2. Every year
    3. Every day
    4. About every 4 weeks
    Show answer

    Answer: About every 4 weeks. We shed tens of thousands of skin cells every minute, a big part of household dust.

  6. Question 6 of 10

    Goosebumps are caused by tiny muscles called…

    1. Arrector pili
    2. Dartos
    3. Orbicularis
    4. Platysma
    Show answer

    Answer: Arrector pili. Useful for fluffing up fur in our ancestors. Less useful now.

  7. Question 7 of 10

    Which skin cells are immune sentinels?

    1. Fibroblasts
    2. Langerhans cells
    3. Adipocytes
    4. Melanocytes
    Show answer

    Answer: Langerhans cells. They catch invaders and carry them to lymph nodes.

  8. Question 8 of 10

    Blushing happens because…

    1. Sweat glands fill
    2. The skin thickens
    3. Small vessels in the face dilate
    4. Melanin increases
    Show answer

    Answer: Small vessels in the face dilate. Darwin called it ‘the most peculiar and most human of all expressions’.

  9. Question 9 of 10

    Which protein gives skin its strength?

    1. Keratin
    2. Collagen
    3. Elastin
    4. Albumin
    Show answer

    Answer: Collagen. Made by fibroblasts in the dermis, with vitamin C. Elastin gives stretch; keratin toughens the surface.

  10. Question 10 of 10

    The skin is the body’s largest organ, weighing about…

    1. 10 kg
    2. 1 kg
    3. 300 g
    4. 3 to 4 kg in an adult
    Show answer

    Answer: 3 to 4 kg in an adult. About 1.5 to 2 m² of surface area.

Common questions

What does the skin do?

It’s a barrier against germs and water loss, controls temperature, senses touch and pain, makes vitamin D and helps the immune system.

Why do we get wrinkles?

Collagen and elastin decline with age and sun damage, the dermis thins, and repeated expressions crease the skin.
