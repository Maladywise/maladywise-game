Pancreas Physiology Quiz

10 questions on how the pancreas works: insulin, glucagon and digestive enzymes. Easy to hard. Tap an answer to see if you’re right.

  1. Question 1 of 10

    Which cells in the pancreas make insulin?

    1. Delta cells
    2. Alpha cells
    3. Beta cells
    4. Acinar cells
    Show answer

    Answer: Beta cells. In the islets of Langerhans, which are only about 1 to 2% of the pancreas.

  2. Question 2 of 10

    Glucagon, from alpha cells, mainly…

    1. Lowers blood sugar
    2. Digests fat
    3. Lowers blood pressure
    4. Raises blood sugar
    Show answer

    Answer: Raises blood sugar. It tells the liver to release glucose. Glucagon injections treat severe hypos.

  3. Question 3 of 10

    Insulin lowers blood sugar by…

    1. Helping cells take up glucose
    2. Breaking down muscle
    3. Making the kidneys lose glucose
    4. Making the liver release glucose
    Show answer

    Answer: Helping cells take up glucose. It moves GLUT4 transporters to the surface of muscle and fat cells.

  4. Question 4 of 10

    Which enzyme from the pancreas digests fat?

    1. Pepsin
    2. Lipase
    3. Amylase
    4. Trypsin
    Show answer

    Answer: Lipase. Raised blood lipase is a key test for pancreatitis.

  5. Question 5 of 10

    Trypsin is released as inactive trypsinogen to…

    1. Help it reach the bloodstream
    2. Save energy
    3. Work in the stomach
    4. Stop the pancreas digesting itself
    Show answer

    Answer: Stop the pancreas digesting itself. It’s activated in the gut by enterokinase. Early activation causes pancreatitis.

  6. Question 6 of 10

    The pancreas neutralises stomach acid in the duodenum with…

    1. Bicarbonate
    2. Bile
    3. Pepsin
    4. Insulin
    Show answer

    Answer: Bicarbonate. Triggered by the hormone secretin.

  7. Question 7 of 10

    Type 1 diabetes happens because…

    1. The liver makes too much glucose
    2. The immune system destroys beta cells
    3. Too much sugar is eaten
    4. The body ignores insulin
    Show answer

    Answer: The immune system destroys beta cells. Type 2 is mainly insulin resistance with failing beta cells.

  8. Question 8 of 10

    Which gut hormone boosts insulin after meals and is the basis of Ozempic?

    1. Gastrin
    2. Secretin
    3. GLP-1
    4. Ghrelin
    Show answer

    Answer: GLP-1. GLP-1 also slows stomach emptying and reduces appetite.

  9. Question 9 of 10

    Fatty, pale, floating stools can mean the pancreas isn’t making enough…

    1. Insulin
    2. Glucagon
    3. Bicarbonate
    4. Lipase
    Show answer

    Answer: Lipase. Exocrine pancreatic insufficiency. Enzyme capsules with meals help.

  10. Question 10 of 10

    A hormone from delta cells that switches off insulin and glucagon is…

    1. Somatostatin
    2. Cortisol
    3. Oxytocin
    4. Thyroxine
    Show answer

    Answer: Somatostatin. Octreotide, a somatostatin copy, treats some hormone-secreting tumours.

Common questions

What does the pancreas do?

It has two jobs: making digestive enzymes and bicarbonate for the gut, and making hormones like insulin and glucagon that control blood sugar.

What is the difference between type 1 and type 2 diabetes?

Type 1 is autoimmune destruction of insulin-making cells. Type 2 is mainly insulin resistance, often linked to weight and genes.
